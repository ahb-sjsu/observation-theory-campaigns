\begin{abstract}
Observation Theory (OT) treats reliability and distortion as properties of a
\emph{consumer} through a \emph{read operator} $\PC\succeq0$ that keeps only the
distinctions the consumer can make, inducing a quotient of the underlying
distinguishability geometry. Ricci flow is the gradient flow of Perelman's
entropy, and its analysis is driven by a Bakry--\'Emery/Fisher-information
substrate that OT also lives on. This note asks, honestly, whether OT's
\emph{read-operator quotient} says anything nontrivial about entropy-monotonic
geometric flows: specifically, whether coarse-graining a flow by $\PC$ carries a
\emph{consumer-relative} entropy with a monotone ``discarded-distinguishability''
gap. I state the observation precisely, situate it against known results
(Perelman; O'Neill submersions; Bakry--\'Emery; Lott--Villani--Sturm; Otto), and
give the ways an expert can falsify or dismiss it in minutes. I claim no theorem;
OT's contribution here, if any, is vocabulary and a reframing, not a new
technique. I write at the edge of my competence and expect correction.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}
On $(M^n,g)$, Ricci flow is $\partial_t g=-2\Ric(g)$. Perelman's
$\mathcal F(g,f)=\int_M(R+|\nabla f|^2)e^{-f}\,dV_g$, coupled to a backward heat
equation for $f$ preserving $\int e^{-f}dV$, is monotone:
\[
\tfrac{d}{dt}\mathcal F = 2\int_M \big|\Ric+\nabla^2 f\big|^2 e^{-f}\,dV \ge 0 .
\]
The integrand is $|\Ric_f|^2$ for the Bakry--\'Emery tensor
$\Ric_f=\Ric+\nabla^2 f$, so Perelman's weight $f$ is a Bakry--\'Emery weight and
the flow is the gradient flow of an entropy. The scale-invariant
$\mathcal W$-entropy is likewise monotone, with critical points the gradient
shrinking solitons.

\section{OT's read operator as coarse-graining}
Read a point of $M$ as (the sufficient statistics of) a probability model, with
the Fisher--Rao metric as the distinguishability geometry. A read operator $\PC$
that retains only some distinctions is a projection onto a sub-distribution of
observables; geometrically it is a smooth surjection --- a coarse-graining
$\pi:(M,g)\to(N,h)$, the consumer's world $N=M/\!\sim$ being the quotient by the
fibers $\pi^{-1}(y)$ it cannot separate. When $\pi$ is a Riemannian submersion,
$\PC$ is orthogonal projection onto the horizontal distribution.

\section{Three facts that make the question well-posed}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(O)] \textbf{Coarse-graining does not lose Ricci (O'Neill).} For a
Riemannian submersion $\pi:(M,g)\to(N,h)$, O'Neill's equations give
$\Ric_h = (\text{horizontal }\Ric_g) + (\text{non-negative }A\text{-tensor
corrections})$; the base is \emph{more} positively curved. The consumer's
quotient inherits curvature \emph{plus} a correction.
\item[(D)] \textbf{The flow descends under symmetry.} Ricci flow does not
generally preserve a submersion, but for metrics invariant under an isometry
group $G$ (homogeneous / cohomogeneity-one), it does: $g(t)$ stays invariant and
descends to a flow $h(t)$ on $N=M/G$. So a \emph{consumer-relative Ricci flow} is
well-defined at least when $\PC$ quotients by a symmetry.
\item[(P)] \textbf{Entropy contracts under coarse-graining (DPI).} Relative
entropy is non-increasing under a Markov/coarse-graining map:
$\Ent(\pi_\#\mu\Vert\pi_\#\nu)\le\Ent(\mu\Vert\nu)$. Perelman's
$\mathcal F,\mathcal W$ are entropies; a consumer reads their pushforward.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The question}
Combining (O), (D), (P): let $\pi$ quotient by a symmetry so the flow descends,
and let $\mathcal W_{\mathcal C}(h,\bar f,\tau)$ be Perelman's $\mathcal W$
computed on the quotient $(N,h)$.

\begin{question}\label{q:mono}
Along the descended (consumer-relative) flow, is $\mathcal W_{\mathcal C}$
monotone, and is the \emph{gap}
$\;\Delta_{\mathcal C}(t)=\mathcal W\big(g(t)\big)-\mathcal W_{\mathcal C}\big(h(t)\big)\;$
monotone (say non-increasing)?
\end{question}

\begin{conjecture}[consumer-discarded distinguishability]\label{c:gap}
For quotients by a compact isometry group with totally geodesic fibers, the gap
$\Delta_{\mathcal C}(t)\ge0$ is monotone non-increasing along Ricci flow: the
distinguishability the consumer cannot read is dissipated monotonically by the
flow, at a rate controlled by the O'Neill $A$-tensor and the fiber Bakry--\'Emery
Ricci curvature.
\end{conjecture}

The OT reading: $\Delta_{\mathcal C}$ is the reliability/entropy in the fibers ---
exactly what $\PC$ quotients out --- and its monotonicity would be a geometric
data-processing inequality \emph{coupled to} the flow, indexed by the consumer.

\section{Honest situating}
Every ingredient is classical and active, which bounds the novelty sharply:
homogeneous and cohomogeneity-one Ricci flow are well developed; O'Neill
submersion curvature is 1966; entropy DPI is elementary; and the synthetic
picture (Lott--Villani--Sturm $\mathrm{CD}(K,N)$, Otto calculus, Bakry--\'Emery)
already couples Ricci, entropy, and optimal transport, with substantial theory on
quotients and measured-Gromov--Hausdorff stability. So Question~\ref{q:mono} is
very possibly (i) an immediate corollary of Bakry--\'Emery $+$ O'Neill $+$ DPI ---
in which case OT contributes only a clean \emph{name} for a known gap; or (ii)
false because the O'Neill correction enters the $\mathcal W$-monotonicity slot
with an uncontrolled sign; or (iii) vacuous outside the symmetric case, where the
flow need not descend. I do not know which. What OT genuinely adds is
\emph{not} a technique but a viewpoint: ``what the consumer reads'' as the
invariant object, and the discarded-distinguishability gap as the quantity to
track --- a reframing that is only worth anything if it makes an otherwise-opaque
statement obvious, and worthless otherwise.

\section{How to dismiss or sharpen this in five minutes}
An expert can grade it fast: (a) Is $\Delta_{\mathcal C}$ monotonicity immediate
from Ni's/Perelman's entropy formula applied to the quotient with the
Bakry--\'Emery weight induced by the fiber volume? If yes, it is known --- please
name the reference. (b) Does the O'Neill $A$-tensor term have a definite sign in
$d\mathcal W_{\mathcal C}/dt$? If not, Conjecture~\ref{c:gap} is false as stated.
(c) Is there a homogeneous example (e.g., a Berger-sphere collapse
$S^3\to S^2$ under Ricci flow) where $\Delta_{\mathcal C}(t)$ can be computed
explicitly and checked? That example alone would confirm or kill it.

\paragraph{Non-applicability, stated plainly.} OT's operational apparatus ---
witnessed certificates, false-clear rates, the sealed-measurement discipline ---
does \emph{not} apply here: Ricci flow is a deductive problem with no consumer to
serve and no outcome to measure. Only the information-geometric core of OT (the
read-operator quotient of a Fisher/entropy geometry) is even a candidate, and
only in the speculative sense above.
