# educational local minimum

**id.** educational-local-minimum
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Education, Volume 9 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A learner state x from which the geodesic cost to the goal is greater than from some nearby state y, while the cost of moving from x to y exceeds the time, effort and motivation the student has. The student cannot progress without outside intervention.

**Example.** A student with a 1.8 average over 90 credits has 162 grade points and needs 2.0 to graduate, the volume's example threshold. Nine more credits at 4.0 give 198 points over 99 credits, which is 2.0. At 3.0 it takes 18 credits, since 216 over 108 is 2.0.

## conditions

- The volume's three examples are the prerequisite trap, the remediation loop and the recursive GPA trap.
- In the GPA trap a cumulative average below the graduation threshold resists improvement, and the courses that raise it fastest may not count toward the degree.
- The definition depends on the student's available resources, so the same state can be a local minimum for one student and not for another.
- The examples are described in words. The volume reports no measured case.
