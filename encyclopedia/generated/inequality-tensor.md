# inequality tensor

**id.** inequality-tensor
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Economics, Volume 4 of the Geometric Series

## definition

For a population of N agents, the entrywise variance across agents of the metric tensor each agent experiences. Its entry for dimensions i and j is the mean over agents of the squared deviation of that agent's $g_{ij}$ from the population average.

**Example.** In the volume's illustration a 100 dollar expense costs 4.0 at monetary scale 50 and 0.0004 at scale 5,000, a ratio of 10,000. For two agents whose $g_{11}$ are 1 and 3 the average is 2 and the monetary entry of the tensor is 1.
