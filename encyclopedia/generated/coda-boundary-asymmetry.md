# asymmetry of coda decision boundaries

**id.** coda-boundary-asymmetry
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Communication, Volume 7 of the Geometric Series

## definition

Under the perturbations of the Decoder Robustness Index, misclassifications between sperm whale coda types run mostly one way. The mean boundary asymmetry is 0.87 on a scale where 0 is symmetric and 1 is purely one-directional. Perturbed 5R3 codas were decoded as 4R2 in 223 instances and the reverse occurred 0 times.

**Example.** The volume's case is the pair 5R3 and 4R2. With 223 crossings one way and 0 the other, all 223 of 223 crossings run in one direction, which is the value 1 on the asymmetry scale for that pair.

## conditions

- The asymmetry characterises the decoder's decision boundaries, not necessarily the whales' perceptual space. The chapter says so.
- It was measured on one nearest-centroid decoder and on synthesised signals. Section 7.8 lists whether it is a property of the signal or of the decoder as unknown.
- Replication across decoder architectures is named as needed and has not been done.
