# Communication BIP (Bond Invariance Principle for communication)

**id.** communication-bond-invariance-principle
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Communication, Volume 7 of the Geometric Series

## definition

An evaluation $\phi$ that claims to measure meaning must satisfy $\phi(\tau(m)) = \phi(m)$ for every act m and every transformation $\tau$ of the communication gauge group. A dependence on the signal coordinates that is not mediated by the signal-pragmatic and signal-content cross-terms is called a gauge anomaly.

**Example.** Take a reference translation and a perfect paraphrase of it. They are related by an element of R, so a meaning evaluation must score them equally. The volume notes that the BLEU score of the paraphrase may be near zero, which is a gauge anomaly.

## conditions

- The chapter calls it a diagnostic criterion, not a normative aspiration. The test is to apply gauge transformations to a tool and check whether its output changes.
- Chapter 10 reads it as the claim that translation has zero holonomy, and says the principle is an ideal and not a description, because the content manifold is not flat everywhere.
- Features computable from the signal are invariant under a subgroup of the gauge group only. The chapter says they are not fully gauge-invariant.
