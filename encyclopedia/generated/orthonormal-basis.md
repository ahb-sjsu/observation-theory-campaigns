# orthonormal basis

**id.** orthonormal-basis
**kind.** concept

## definition

A basis of unit vectors orthogonal to each other. Projection onto their span is the sum of the projections onto each, and a square matrix whose columns form one is an orthogonal matrix. Primer L section L.5.

**Example.** (1, 1) over root 2 and (1, -1) over root 2 are orthonormal, each of length one and with dot product zero.
