# identity matrix

**id.** identity-matrix
**kind.** concept

## definition

The square matrix with ones on the diagonal and zeros elsewhere, which leaves every vector unchanged. The identity reader is the read operator equal to it. Primer L section L.3.

**Example.** The two by two identity has rows (1, 0) and (0, 1), and sends (3, 4) to (3, 4).
