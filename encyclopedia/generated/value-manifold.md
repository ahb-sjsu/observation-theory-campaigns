# Value manifold

**id.** value-manifold
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric AI, Volume 11 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The nine-dimensional Riemannian manifold on which the volume places AI alignment. A value state is a point with one coordinate in [0, 1] on each of nine dimensions, an action is a trajectory between two states, and the minimum-cost trajectory, the value geodesic, is the aligned behaviour.

**Example.** A response rated 0.9 on welfare and 0.2 on dignity is a point with those two coordinates among its nine. Its average over the two, 0.55, is also the average of a response rated 0.55 on both, so the average does not identify the point.
