# null hypothesis

**id.** null-hypothesis
**kind.** concept

## definition

The statement that the effect under study is absent, under which a test statistic's p-value is computed. A null model is its simulated form. Primer S, equation S.15.

**Example.** For two groups the null says the labels carry no information, so every reassignment of the labels is as likely as the observed one.
