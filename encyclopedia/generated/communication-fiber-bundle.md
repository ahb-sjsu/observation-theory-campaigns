# communication fiber bundle

**id.** communication-fiber-bundle
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Communication, Volume 7 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A bundle whose base space is the content manifold and whose fiber over a content is the space of surface realisations that can express it (linguistic forms, signals, pragmatic framings). The projection sends an act (s, p, c) to c. The structure group, acting on fibers and fixing the base point, is the gauge group of communication.

**Example.** In the volume's example the fiber over the content five units of silver is parameterised by language, register and notation. An English and a Hebrew rendering are two points of that fiber with the same image under the projection.

## conditions

- The bundle is taken to be locally trivial and not globally trivial. The chapter offers untranslatable concepts as the reason, since some fibers have components that others lack.
- Identifying fibers at different contents needs a path and a connection. The chapter reads a connection as an interpretive framework and a misunderstanding as failed transport.
- Here the pragmatic framing lies in the fiber and the base is the content manifold alone. Chapter 3 places the pragmatic manifold in the base with the content manifold. Section 13.1.1 names both descriptions and says they differ only in where the pragmatic manifold is counted.
