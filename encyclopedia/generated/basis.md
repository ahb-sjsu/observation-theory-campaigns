# basis

**id.** basis
**kind.** concept

## definition

A set of independent vectors whose span is the whole space, so that every vector is exactly one combination of them. The standard basis vectors have a one in one coordinate and zeros elsewhere. Primer L section L.1.

**Example.** In the plane, e1 = (1, 0) and e2 = (0, 1) are a basis, and (3, 4) = 3 e1 + 4 e2.
