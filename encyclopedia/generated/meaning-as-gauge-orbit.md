# meaning as a gauge orbit

**id.** meaning-as-gauge-orbit
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Communication, Volume 7 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The meaning of a communicative act m is its equivalence class under the communication gauge group, the set of all $\tau(m)$ for $\tau$ in G. Two acts have the same meaning exactly when a gauge transformation relates them.

**Example.** An English assertion, its French translation and its written transcript are three points of one orbit, so they have one meaning. The sarcastic utterance of the same English words is a point of a different orbit.

## conditions

- It is a definition, and it depends on which transformations are admitted to the gauge group.
- The space of orbits is parameterized by the content manifold together with the pragmatic manifold.
- Sarcasm is not a gauge transformation, because it changes the pragmatic coordinates. A sarcastic and a sincere utterance of the same words lie in different orbits.
