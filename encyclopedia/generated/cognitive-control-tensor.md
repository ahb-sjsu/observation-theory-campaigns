# control tensor

**id.** cognitive-control-tensor
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Cognition, Volume 6 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A (2,0)-tensor $C$ at each cognitive state that gives, for each direction of perturbation and each direction of response, the control system's capacity to manage the interaction. Its four named components are cognitive flexibility, inhibitory control, counterfactual sensitivity and working memory management, sampled by subtasks E1 to E4.

**Example.** The volume's diagonal for Claude is (0.673, 0.492, 0.562, 0.886). Its anisotropy is 0.886 / 0.492 = 1.80, the largest of the five models, and for Gemini 2.0 Flash it is 0.710 / 0.500 = 1.42.
