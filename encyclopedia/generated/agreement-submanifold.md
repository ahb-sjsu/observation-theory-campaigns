# Agreement submanifold

**id.** agreement-submanifold
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Politics, Volume 10 of the Geometric Series

## definition

For a coalition, the set of dimensions on which every pair of members differs by at most a tolerance $\epsilon$. Its dimension measures the depth of the coalition. One shared dimension is called fragile and four is called robust.

**Example.** Three members have environmental positions 1.0, 1.2 and 1.4 and economic positions $-1$, 0 and 1, and the tolerance is 0.5. The largest environmental gap is 0.4 and the largest economic gap is 2, so only the environmental dimension is in the agreement submanifold and its dimension is 1.
