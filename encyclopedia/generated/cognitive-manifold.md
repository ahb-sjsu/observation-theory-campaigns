# cognitive manifold

**id.** cognitive-manifold
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Cognition, Volume 6 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The set of cognitive states a system actually visits, hypothesized to form a smooth submanifold $M$ of the activation space whose dimension is far below the number of units. Its dimension is the number of independent ways the cognitive state can vary.

**Example.** The volume's analogy is a body with about 244 joints at six degrees of freedom each, which has 6 times 244 = 1464 configuration dimensions, while walking varies in about two, the phase of the gait cycle and the speed.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** The manifold hypothesis of machine learning, which the volume cites from Bengio and colleagues, Tenenbaum and colleagues, and Roweis and Saul.
