# curvature-triggered transition (System 1 to System 2)

**id.** curvature-triggered-transition
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Cognition, Volume 6 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The volume's geometric form of dual-process theory. A system follows the gradient of its heuristic field (System 1) where the curvature of the cognitive manifold is below a threshold, and computes geodesics (System 2) where the curvature reaches it.

**Example.** In the volume's illustration a framing perturbation of 0.1 that produces a displacement of 0.3 is an amplification of 3.0, and over five reasoning steps $e^{5s} = 3$ gives $s = (ln 3)/5$, about 0.22, so the curvature magnitude is about 0.048. The inputs 0.1, 0.3 and 5 are modelled values.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** Dual-process theory, System 1 and System 2, as in Kahneman.
