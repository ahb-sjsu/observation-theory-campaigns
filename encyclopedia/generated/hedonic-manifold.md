# hedonic manifold

**id.** hedonic-manifold
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Gastronomy, Volume 12 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A hedonic function $H$ assigns each flavor point a scalar pleasantness. The hedonic manifold is its high-value region, the set where $H$ exceeds a threshold $\theta$, or its ridge, where $H$ is locally maximal across the transverse directions. Pairing is the choice of two flavors whose sum lands on it.

**Example.** On one axis with $H(f) = -(f - 3)^2$ and a threshold of -1, the high-value region is the interval from 2 to 4 and the ridge is the point 3. Pleasantness falls on both sides of the optimum, the inverted-U the volume describes. The function is illustrative.
