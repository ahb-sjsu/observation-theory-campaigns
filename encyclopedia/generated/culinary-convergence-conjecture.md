# Culinary Convergence conjecture

**id.** culinary-convergence-conjecture
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Gastronomy, Volume 12 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The conjecture that cuisines descending independent gradients on the same fitness landscape, set by a shared receptor complement, metabolic needs and cooking physics, reach clustered attractors. The shared peak the volume names is acid, fat, umami, salt and aromatic contrast.

**Example.** On the landscape $-(x^2 - 1)^2$, gradient ascent from 0.5 and from 2 both reach the peak at 1, and a start at -0.5 reaches the other peak at -1. Independent searches on one landscape share a small set of attractors, and the basin decides which one a search finds. The landscape is illustrative.
