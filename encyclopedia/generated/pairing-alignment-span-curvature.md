# alignment, span and curvature of a pairing

**id.** pairing-alignment-span-curvature
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Gastronomy, Volume 12 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The volume's conjecture that pairing quality is a function of three geometric quantities. Alignment is the cosine of the two flavor vectors, span is how much of the space they jointly cover, and curvature is the symmetric bilinear interaction $\mathbf{B}(\mathbf{f}_1, \mathbf{f}_2)$ added to their sum. A single-number summary discards two of the three.

**Example.** Two unit vectors at angle 0 sum to a vector of length 2 in the same direction. Two at a right angle sum to length square root of 2, about 1.41, in a new direction. Their cosines are 1 and 0, and neither number records the resultant or any interaction term.

## conditions

- Tagged a conjecture. The volume says the cross-corpus validation is still partial.
- The interaction term is a modeling axiom. It adds along an axis for synergy, as with salt and umami, subtracts for antagonism, as with sweet and bitter, and is zero in a flat space.
- Similar summands add nearly colinearly and complementary summands add by the Pythagorean rule. Both statements are for the linear sum without the interaction term.
- The claim that the three-term model tracks recipe co-occurrence and panel ratings better than cosine alone is stated without printed numbers.
