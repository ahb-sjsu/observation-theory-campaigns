# language chart and translation map

**id.** language-chart
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Communication, Volume 7 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A language L is a coordinate chart on the content manifold, a domain $U_L$ of expressible meanings with a map $\varphi_L$ to the language's expressions. The translation map from $L_1$ to $L_2$ is the transition function $\varphi_{L_2} \circ \varphi_{L_1}^{-1}$, defined on the overlap of the two domains.

**Example.** In the volume's example the English word silver, the Akkadian word kaspum and the Sumerian logogram KU.BABBAR label one point of the content manifold. The translation map sends the Akkadian label to the English one through that point.

## conditions

- The chart is partial. The domain need not cover the whole content manifold, and different languages have different domains.
- A meaning outside the overlap is untranslatable in this formulation. A meaning charted at lower resolution in the target language translates with a loss of precision.
- The chapter calls the transition map incomplete because it is pointwise. It adds parallel transport along a path, on the assumption that the content manifold is curved.
