# Gauge-invariant alignment

**id.** gauge-invariant-alignment
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric AI, Volume 11 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A system is gauge-invariantly aligned when every entry of its gauge violation tensor is zero. Partial alignment is invariance under some transformations and not others, and the degree is the norm of the tensor, the square root of the sum of its squared entries.

**Example.** A tensor whose only non-zero entries are 0.03 and 0.04 has norm equal to the square root of 0.0009 + 0.0016, which is 0.05. The system is partially aligned, and it is gauge-invariantly aligned only when every entry is 0.
