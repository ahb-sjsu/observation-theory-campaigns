# Bond Index disparity ratio

**id.** bond-index-disparity-ratio
**kind.** instrument
**volume.** Geometric Medicine, Volume 8 of the Geometric Series

## definition

For one policy and two populations, the clinical Bond Index of the first population divided by that of the second. A ratio of 1.0 means equal cost beyond the optimum, and a ratio of 2.3 means the first population bears 2.3 times the cost of the second.

**Example.** If the index is 4.6 for one population and 2.0 for another under the same policy, the ratio is 4.6 / 2.0 = 2.3, the volume's example reading. On the volume's illustrative thresholds a ratio of 2.0 or more is a critical disparity. The two index values are chosen to give the volume's 2.3.
