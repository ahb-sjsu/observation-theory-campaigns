# Community of interest, manifold criterion

**id.** manifold-community-of-interest
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Politics, Volume 10 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A connected subset of voters whose image on the preference manifold is also connected and compact. The test is to cluster the district's voters by manifold position. One cluster passes, and two or more mean separated communities have been combined.

**Example.** In the volume's illustration the clustering test finds one cluster under the bipartisan Map A and two under the partisan Map B, where the Route 9 corridor and the exurban belt are joined. Map B's district fails the criterion.

## conditions

- The definition rests on shared manifold position and not on shared geography. Adjacent neighborhoods can fail it and distant ones can meet it.
- The test depends on a clustering method that chooses the number of clusters. The volume suggests Gaussian mixture models.
- The cluster counts for the three District 7 maps are illustrative.
