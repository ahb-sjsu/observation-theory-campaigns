# Falsification criteria (Appendix E.8)

**id.** aesthetic-falsification-criteria
**kind.** instrument
**volume.** Geometric Aesthetics, Volume 13 of the Geometric Series

## definition

Four stated tests. The symmetry claim is falsified if cross-lingual correlation falls below 0.5 across several language families on a pre-registered bundled corpus with n at least 200. The sign-flip claim is weakened or overturned if the flip fails on a new music corpus with a different encoder. The within-genre claim is weakened if the residual fails to replicate. Any proved claim with a proof error is retracted.

**Example.** Criterion (i) needs a correlation below 0.5 on at least 200 bundles. The volume's Finnish (288) and French (227) pairs meet the size bar and show 0.77 and 0.78, above the threshold.

## conditions

- The criteria are commitments for future replications. The appendix says the first measurements came out in the stated direction on the volume's own data.
- The replications named (a different music encoder, a new corpus, a pre-registered bundled corpus) have not been carried out, and the appendix says so.
- The appendix answers the objection that four channels, 71 axes and many residualization choices leave the framework free to fit any story.
