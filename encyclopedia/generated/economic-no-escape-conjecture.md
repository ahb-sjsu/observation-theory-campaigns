# Economic No Escape conjecture

**id.** economic-no-escape-conjecture
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Economics, Volume 4 of the Geometric Series

## definition

If the economic decision manifold has components separated by the absence of any connecting path, and not merely by high boundary penalties, then no financial instrument, regulatory arbitrage or contract can create a transition between them.

**Example.** The volume's candidate is the organ market. If no path joins the state of holding an organ to the state of holding money, the conjecture says that no instrument can complete the trade, whatever its price.
