# power iteration

**id.** power-iteration
**kind.** concept

## definition

Multiplying a starting vector by a symmetric matrix and rescaling, repeatedly, so that the component along the top eigenvector grows fastest and the vector turns toward it. Primer L section L.7.

**Example.** From (1, 0) under rows (2, 1) and (1, 2) come (2, 1), (5, 4), and (14, 13), the ratio of the coordinates 2, 1.25, and 1.077 closing on 1.
