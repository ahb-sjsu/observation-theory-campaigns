# four geometric pathologies of markets

**id.** market-pathology-taxonomy
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Economics, Volume 4 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The volume's classification of market failure into four kinds. Heuristic corruption is a price heuristic that points away from the geodesic. Objective hijacking is search on the wrong objective. A local minimum is a suboptimal basin that traps agents. Gauge breaking is an evaluation that depends on the description.

**Example.** The volume's reading of 2008. Mortgage models that ignored tail risk are heuristic corruption, rating agencies paid by issuers are objective hijacking, the housing bubble is a local minimum, and rising nominal prices over flat real prices are gauge breaking.
