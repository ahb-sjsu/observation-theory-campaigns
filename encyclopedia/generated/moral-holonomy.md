# Moral holonomy

**id.** moral-holonomy
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Ethics, Volume 2 of the Geometric Series

## definition

For a closed loop at a point p of the moral manifold, the holonomy is the linear map on the tangent space at p given by parallel transport of an obligation around the loop. If it is the identity for all loops the connection is flat at p. If not, the connection has curvature, and evaluation depends on the path.

**Example.** In the volume's illustration an obligation with care component 0.5 is carried around the loop friendship, confidentiality, duty to warn, resolution, friendship. It returns with care 0.35, privacy 0.1 and epistemic 0.05, so the holonomy is not the identity. The numbers are illustrative, not measured.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** Holonomy of a connection, from differential geometry.

## conditions

- The volume states that curvature has not been directly measured. Tracing an obligation around a moral circuit and measuring the holonomy is an experiment that has not been designed.
- The discrete part of a loop that crosses Hohfeldian boundaries is an element of V4, which is abelian. Any order dependence must come from the continuous connection or be a departure from the structure, and the data do not yet separate the two.
- The order effects the volume reports (path dependence in 2 of 8 scenarios, tagged a preliminary empirical result) come from one language model, not from people.
- Proposition 10.2 was corrected in v1.25. The infinitesimal holonomy is the Riemann tensor with a minus sign.
