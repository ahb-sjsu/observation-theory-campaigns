# effective flavor dimensionality

**id.** effective-flavor-dimensionality
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Gastronomy, Volume 12 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The count of eigenvalues of an individual's receptor-response covariance $\Sigma_r$ that exceed that person's perceptual noise threshold. Supertasting raises gain and lifts marginal eigenvalues above the threshold. Specific anosmias and non-taster haplotypes drop particular eigenvalues below it and collapse dimensions.

**Example.** With eigenvalues (5, 2, 0.8, 0.3) and a noise threshold of 1, the effective dimensionality is 2. Doubling the gain with the threshold unchanged multiplies every eigenvalue of the covariance by 4, giving (20, 8, 3.2, 1.2), and all 4 clear the threshold. The numbers are illustrative.
