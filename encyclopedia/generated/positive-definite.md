# positive definite

**id.** positive-definite
**kind.** concept

## definition

A symmetric matrix whose quadratic form is positive for every nonzero vector, which is the same as every eigenvalue being positive. Positive semidefinite allows zero. Primer L section L.6.

**Example.** Rows (2, 1) and (1, 2) have eigenvalues 3 and 1 and are positive definite, while rows (1, 2) and (2, 1) give -2 at (1, -1) and are not.
