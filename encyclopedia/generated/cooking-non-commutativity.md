# non-commutativity of cooking operations

**id.** cooking-non-commutativity
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Gastronomy, Volume 12 of the Geometric Series

## definition

Cooking operations are operators on flavor space, and in general their order matters. For searing $S$ and braising $B$, $S(B(\mathbf{f}_0)) \neq B(S(\mathbf{f}_0))$. The commutator $[S, B] = SB - BS$ is zero exactly when two operations can be freely reordered. Sequenced operations form a non-abelian monoid.

**Example.** Let one step double a number, $D(x) = 2x$, and another add one, $A(x) = x + 1$. Then $D(A(1)) = 4$ and $A(D(1)) = 3$, so the two orders differ by 1. Two doublings commute. The operators are illustrative stand-ins, not the volume's.
