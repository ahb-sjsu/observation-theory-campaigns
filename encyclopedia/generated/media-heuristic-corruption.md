# Media heuristic corruption

**id.** media-heuristic-corruption
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Politics, Volume 10 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The media environment is treated as the heuristic field voters use to estimate political distances. It is corrupted in four modes, which are dimensional suppression, distance inflation, axis rotation by engagement-optimizing algorithms, and echo-chamber metric collapse.

**Example.** In the volume's illustration two District 7 voters are 2.3 Mahalanobis units apart and each perceives 5.5. The ratio $5.5 / 2.3$ is about 2.4, the distortion factor the chapter reports.

## conditions

- A well-calibrated media heuristic is one that reports on all six dimensions and estimates distances without systematic distortion. The four modes are departures from that standard.
- The coverage patterns assigned to cable news, public radio, social media and local news are the volume's characterization and carry no cited measurement.
- The perceived and actual distances between the two District 7 voters are illustrative. The cumulative size of the distortion is argued from a framing result quoted from Geometric Communication.
