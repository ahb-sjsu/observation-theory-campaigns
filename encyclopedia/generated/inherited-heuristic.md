# inherited heuristic and the empty-heuristic problem

**id.** inherited-heuristic
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Education, Volume 9 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The inherited heuristic $h_P$ is the part of the educational heuristic field supplied by parents or family who have already been through the educational system. For a first-generation student it is about zero, and the volume calls navigation without it the empty-heuristic problem.

**Example.** With the parents' estimate at zero and all other sources absent, the field is zero everywhere and A* expands states in order of cost from the start alone. The volume's stated indicator is 1.5 to 2 extra semesters to a degree for first-generation students, given without a source.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** The hidden curriculum of educational research, which Section 7.6 identifies with it.

## conditions

- The claim is that the field is deficient and not the student, who may sit at the same learner state as a continuing-generation peer.
- The search-theory statement behind it is that A* with a zero heuristic reduces to uniform-cost search, which finds the goal and explores every state up to the geodesic cost.
- The volume cites longer time to degree, more changes of major and more late withdrawals as indicators, without a source at that point. Prediction 16.3.4 asks whether the excess persists after controlling for ability, and Chapter 18 says it has not been tested.
- Chapter 7 identifies the hidden curriculum with this field.
