# communication manifold

**id.** communication-manifold
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Communication, Volume 7 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The product $M = S \times P \times C$ of a signal manifold S (the physical carriers), a pragmatic manifold P (intentions, social relations, illocutionary forces) and a content manifold C (semantic meanings). A communicative act is a point (s, p, c) of M, and a scalar evaluation is a projection of M to the real line.

**Example.** In the volume's illustration, a spoken English apology and a formal Japanese apology with a bow are close in pragmatics and content and far apart in signal. A casual text message two hours later is closer to the English one in signal and far in pragmatics.
