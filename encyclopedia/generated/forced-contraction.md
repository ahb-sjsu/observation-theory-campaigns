# Forced contraction

**id.** forced-contraction
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Medicine, Volume 8 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A decision process in which a nine-dimensional clinical-moral state must be mapped to one of a finite set of triage categories under a time limit that prevents full nine-dimensional pathfinding. The contraction operator projects onto fewer dimensions as the available time falls, and onto survival probability alone at zero time.

**Example.** In the volume's vignette Dr. Kim has ninety seconds for four patients, 22.5 seconds each, and can assess about two dimensions per patient. At zero time the operator keeps the outcomes score only, so two patients with outcomes 0.3 and trust 0.2 and 0.9 get the same category. The ninety seconds are the volume's and the two patients are arithmetic from the definition.

## conditions

- The time limit is below a threshold. With unlimited time the operator is the clinical geodesic on the full complex.
- Time pressure contracts the assessment. Resource scarcity is treated separately, as the removal of edges from the complex.
- The claim that the operator loses dimensions as time falls is stated as part of the definition and is not measured.
