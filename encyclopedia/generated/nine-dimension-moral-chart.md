# Nine-dimension moral chart

**id.** nine-dimension-moral-chart
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Ethics, Volume 2 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The canonical chart of the moral manifold. A generic point has nine coordinates, one per cell of a grid of three ethical scopes (individual, relational, collective) by three epistemic modes (what matters, who decides, what we know). Lower strata may have fewer active dimensions.

**Example.** Three scopes times three modes give 3 x 3 = 9 cells, and Rights and Duties is the cell Relational by What Matters. The volume's measured effective rank is 5.19 of 8 validated axes, so the nine are not nine independent measured coordinates.

## conditions

- The volume calls the nine a canonical engineering chart chosen for coverage and audit. It does not claim nine is the unique or minimal basis.
- The completeness claim carried its own falsification condition and the volume reports that the condition is met. Identity attack passed a preregistered gate as a tenth axis, and purity and loyalty also passed a gate.
- Measured on text, four learned axes do no better than one general-valence channel, and the eight validated axis scores have effective rank 5.19 of 8, below the six the section expected.
- The rights-and-duties axis could not be learned as a graded score. The volume now treats rights as a hand-specified hard constraint, not a measured coordinate.
