# Liability-damages conservation

**id.** liability-damages-conservation
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Law, Volume 5 of the Geometric Series

## definition

In a closed bilateral dispute under a fixed legal framework, the net liabilities of the two parties sum to zero. Every right that adjudication assigns to one party is a correlative duty of the other, and every dollar of damages awarded to one is a dollar of liability on the other.

**Example.** If the court awards A 100 dollars against B, the net liability of B is +100 and that of A is -100, and the sum is 0.

## conditions

- Closed means two fixed parties and a fixed legal framework. The volume names three operations that break the law, which are legislation, constitutional amendment and third-party intervention.
- Punitive damages are handled by extending the system to include the state as a third party, after which the three net liabilities sum to zero.
- The proof rests on Hohfeld's correlative pairs and on the premise that adjudication assigns positions already existing in the framework and creates none.
- Chapter 1 says the conservation laws are conditional on assumptions that may not hold in all legal contexts.
