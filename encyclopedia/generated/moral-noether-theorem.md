# Moral Noether theorem (invariance of harm accounting)

**id.** moral-noether-theorem
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Ethics, Volume 2 of the Geometric Series

## definition

If the moral Lagrangian is invariant under a re-description that shifts the path along a generator $\xi$ (the Bond Invariance Principle), then the Noether charge $H = p_\mu \xi^\mu$, the moral momentum contracted with the generator, is constant along extremal paths. The volume defines harm as this charge for the continuous factor of the gauge group.

**Example.** With a flat metric and no potential a path has constant velocity, say (2, 3). For the generator (1, 0) the charge is 2 x 1 + 3 x 0 = 2 at every time, which is the conserved quantity. The numbers are illustrative.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** Noether's theorem of classical mechanics, applied to a posited moral Lagrangian.

## conditions

- The theorem is conditional on the Bond Invariance Principle and a twice-differentiable Lagrangian. It applies inside each stratum, not on the constraint set, and at a boundary the harm ledger tracks the jumps.
- It does not say that total harm in the world is constant. Harm can be generated and repaired. The law constrains only what re-description can change.
- The continuous factor U(1)_H rests on Axiom A4, a modeling choice that there is exactly one continuous conserved quantity. The discrete factor V4 gives selection rules and not Noether charges.
- The stronger thesis Conservation of Harm, asserted through v1.23, was withdrawn by the author and replaced by this weaker claim of representation-invariance of harm accounting.

Conditions are curated in `volumes/geometric-ethics.toml` rather than read from a record.
