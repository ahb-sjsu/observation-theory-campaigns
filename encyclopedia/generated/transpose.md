# transpose

**id.** transpose
**kind.** concept

## definition

The matrix with rows and columns swapped, written with a superscript T. A column becomes a row, so x transposed times y is the dot product and x times y transposed is the outer product, and the transpose of a product reverses the order. Primer L section L.3.

**Example.** The transpose of the matrix with rows (1, 2) and (3, 4) has rows (1, 3) and (2, 4).
