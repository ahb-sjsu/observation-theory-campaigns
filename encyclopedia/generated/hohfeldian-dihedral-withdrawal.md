# Withdrawal of the dihedral group D4

**id.** hohfeldian-dihedral-withdrawal
**kind.** correction
**volume.** Geometric Ethics, Volume 2 of the Geometric Series

## definition

Versions of the volume up to v1.24 stated that Hohfeldian transitions form the dihedral group D4 of order eight, adding a quarter-turn that cycles the four positions. Version 1.25 withdraws this. The two demonstrated operations generate only V4, and the uniqueness theorem for the gauge group is withdrawn with it.

**Example.** The walk O, C, N, L, O alternates s, n, s, n, so it is four steps of two operations and not one operation applied four times. The group they generate has 4 elements, where D4 would have 8.

## conditions

- Three facts retire the claim. No quarter-turn has been demonstrated as a normative operation, the Lean proof shows it is not generated by s and n, and a direct search in a learned representation did not find it.
- Axiom A3, the cyclic ordering, read a walk as an operation. Alternating s and n visits all four positions, but each step is s or n and the walk closes because sn applied twice is the identity.
- The corollary that a double promise creates a claim rested on the quarter-turn and is withdrawn with it.
- With A3 withdrawn there is no uniqueness claim. The volume says no result in it excludes further discrete symmetries.
