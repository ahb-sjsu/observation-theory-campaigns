# estimator, unbiased

**id.** estimator
**kind.** concept

## definition

A rule that produces an estimate of an unknown quantity from a sample, unbiased when its expectation over samples is that quantity. An estimate without a statement of its spread is not interpretable. Primer S section S.6.

**Example.** The sample variance with n minus 1 is unbiased for the true variance, and with n it is too small by the factor (n minus 1) over n.
