# sycophancy gradient

**id.** sycophancy-gradient
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Cognition, Volume 6 of the Geometric Series

## definition

On the L2 test, the fraction of invalid corrections that make a model change its verdict, the wrong flip rate, rises across four models from 0 percent to 56 percent. The sycophancy index is the wrong flip rate divided by the correct flip rate.

**Example.** The volume prints for Gemini 2.0 Flash a flip on 33 percent of invalid corrections and 71 percent of valid ones. 33 / 71 is about 0.46 where the volume prints an index of 0.472, and the gap 0.71 - 0.33 is 0.38 where it prints 0.377, the printed rates being rounded.

## conditions

- There are nine wrong-correction items per model, with Wilson 95 percent intervals as wide as 27 to 81 percent. Four models are tabulated.
- The 13.3 sigma Fisher figure reflects correction integration overall. The volume says it conflates accepting valid corrections with accepting invalid ones.
- The reading as a leaky correction filter, a projection that fails to suppress invalid evidence, is the volume's interpretation.
