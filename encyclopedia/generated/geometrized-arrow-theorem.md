# Arrow's theorem, geometrized

**id.** geometrized-arrow-theorem
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Politics, Volume 10 of the Geometric Series

## definition

For a preference manifold of effective dimension at least 3, no contraction to a one-dimensional social ranking preserves all of topology, orientation and symmetry. These are the volume's readings of independence of irrelevant alternatives, unanimity and non-dictatorship.

**Example.** On a six-dimensional manifold a contraction to a single ranking must drop $6 - 1 = 5$ dimensions of information, while the three conditions together ask that none be dropped.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** Arrow's impossibility theorem of 1951.
