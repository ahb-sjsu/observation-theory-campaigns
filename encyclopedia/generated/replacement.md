# with replacement, without replacement

**id.** replacement
**kind.** concept

## definition

Drawing rows with replacement lets a row appear more than once, as the bootstrap does, and drawing without replacement lets each appear at most once. Primer S section S.11.

**Example.** A bootstrap draw of 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 might be 4, 9, 2, 5, 4, 4, 7, 9, with the 9 twice and one 5 left out.
