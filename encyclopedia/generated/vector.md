# vector

**id.** vector
**kind.** concept

## definition

A list of d numbers, and the arrow from the origin to the point with those coordinates. A row of a table with d numeric columns is one. Primer L, equation L.1.

**Example.** (3, 4) is the arrow from the origin to the point three across and four up, and has length 5.

## equation

Book equation L.1.

    x+y=(x_1+y_1,\dots,x_d+y_d),\qquad c\,x=(c\,x_1,\dots,c\,x_d).
