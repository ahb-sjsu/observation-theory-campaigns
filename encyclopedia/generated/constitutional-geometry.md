# Constitutional geometry

**id.** constitutional-geometry
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric AI, Volume 11 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The replacement of a list of natural-language constitutional rules by constraints on the value manifold of four types, which are direction, boundary, trade-off and symmetry constraints. Theorem 14.1 says the replacement preserves rule interactions through the metric, resolves conflicts by the geodesic, is enforceable under structural containment, and is auditable.

**Example.** In the volume's illustration the fictional ARIA's 47 rules become 12 direction, 18 boundary, 9 trade-off and 8 symmetry constraints, and 12 + 18 + 9 + 8 is 47.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** A geometric recasting of Constitutional AI, which the volume describes as a list of natural-language rules.
