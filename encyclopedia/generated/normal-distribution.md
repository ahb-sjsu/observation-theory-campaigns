# normal distribution

**id.** normal-distribution
**kind.** concept

## definition

The bell-shaped distribution with mean mu and standard deviation sigma, with about 68 percent of its mass within one standard deviation, 95 within two, and 99.7 within three, and 1.96 the multiplier for exactly 95. Averages of many independent variables are close to it. Primer S, equation S.8.

**Example.** A mean with standard error 0.8 has the 95 percent interval plus or minus 1.568.
