# Absorbing stratum

**id.** absorbing-stratum
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Ethics, Volume 2 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A stratum $S_0$ with a tubular neighborhood N and a retraction r from N onto $S_0$ such that every compatible evaluation satisfies $S(p) = S(r(p))$ on N. Near it the evaluation depends only on the projection onto $S_0$ and not on the transverse coordinates. It formalizes a nullifier, a condition that overrides all other considerations.

**Example.** In the abuse stratum (582 cases in the volume's corpus count) a promise of strength 0.2 and a promise of strength 0.9 give the same evaluation, because the evaluation depends only on the projection onto the stratum.

## conditions

- The definition is a modeling axiom. It models nullifiers and does not prove that any given condition is one.
- The volume's three nullifiers (abuse, danger, impossibility) come from its analysis of the Dear Abby corpus, which it says reflects one American tradition and was not preregistered.
- In the decision complex an absorbing boundary has an infinite penalty. In an executable stratification an absorbing stratum is left only through a gate triggered by a human-oversight event.
