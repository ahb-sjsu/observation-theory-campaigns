# Hohfeldian octad gauge group

## definition

The symmetry group of the full Hohfeldian octad is the semi-direct product of two copies of the dihedral group of order 8. One copy acts on the first-order positions and the other on the second-order positions, and a homomorphism $\varphi$ lets second-order operations twist first-order ones. The volume works with 64 elements.

**Example.** Each copy of the dihedral group has 8 elements, so the pairs number 8 x 8 = 64. With a trivial coupling the product of (g1, g2) and (h1, h2) is (g1 h1, g2 h2).

## conditions

- The volume gives a proof sketch. The appendix specifies the coupling homomorphism on one generator of the second copy.
- When the coupling is trivial the group is the direct product, which the volume identifies as the Geometric Ethics result and calls adequate for ethics and inadequate for law.
- The chapter body says the order is at most 64 and depends on the coupling. The appendix states order 64.
- That the legal evaluation must be unchanged by all 64 elements is the Judicial Bond Invariance Principle, which is an axiom.
