# Hohfeldian octad gauge group

**id.** hohfeldian-octad-gauge-group
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Law, Volume 5 of the Geometric Series

## definition

Withdrawn pending restatement under the Klein four-group. The statement was that the symmetry group of the full Hohfeldian octad is the semi-direct product of two copies of the dihedral group of order 8, one acting on the first-order positions and the other on the second-order positions, coupled by a homomorphism $\varphi$, with 64 elements. The chapter's note of 2026-11-26 withdraws the theorem, its order 64 and its coupling homomorphism, and says the structure of the group of Hohfeldian relabelings for the full octad is open.

**Example.** Each copy of the dihedral group has 8 elements, so the pairs of the withdrawn statement number 8 x 8 = 64. Each copy of the Klein four-group has 4 elements, so pairs of those number 4 x 4 = 16.

## conditions

- The volume gave a proof sketch, and the appendix specified the coupling homomorphism on one generator of the second copy. Both carry the withdrawal mark.
- Geometric Ethics version 1.25 restated the group of the full octad as a conjecture, $V_4 \rtimes V_4$, not proved. The note adds that the withdrawn group has order 64 while the octahedral group has order 48, so the name octahedral did not fit.
- Also withdrawn are every statement that invokes 64 transformations and the derivation of the order-dependence of legal tests from a non-abelian gauge group. The note says $V_4$ is abelian and that no non-abelian group of the octad has been established.
- Chapter 8 no longer identifies the bond-preserving transformations of the Judicial Bond Invariance Principle with this group. It says the Hohfeldian relabelings, $V_4$ on each square, are a subgroup of them.
