# Frechet mean of an electorate

**id.** political-frechet-mean
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Politics, Volume 10 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The point of the preference manifold that minimizes the sum of squared manifold distances to all voters. The volume uses it as the multi-dimensional counterpart of the median voter.

**Example.** Three voters sit at 0, 1 and 5 on a line with ordinary distance. The point 2 gives squared distances 4, 1 and 9, a sum of 14. The median voter at 1 gives 1, 0 and 16, a sum of 17, so the Frechet mean is 2 and not the median.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** The Frechet mean, or Karcher mean, of a distribution on a metric space. The one-dimensional comparison is the median voter of Downs.
