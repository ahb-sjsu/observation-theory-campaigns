# Aesthetic Hilbert space

**id.** aesthetic-hilbert-space
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Aesthetics, Volume 13 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The space of square-integrable complex functions on the aesthetic manifold. A state assigns an amplitude to each point, and its squared modulus is the probability that a reading places the work there. A work read in exactly one way is a state concentrated at one point, which recovers the classical picture.

**Example.** A work in the state 0.6 times the lyric reading plus 0.8 times the joke reading is read as a lyric with probability 0.36 and as a joke with probability 0.64, and the two sum to 1.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** The Hilbert-space formalism of quantum mechanics, used as analogy.
