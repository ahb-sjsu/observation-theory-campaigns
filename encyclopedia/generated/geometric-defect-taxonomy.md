# geometric taxonomy of cognitive pathology

**id.** geometric-defect-taxonomy
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Cognition, Volume 6 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The volume's classification of four conditions by the geometric object that is defective. ADHD is instability of the attention metric, autism is rigidity of the attention metric, depression is a collapsed heuristic field with near-zero gradient, and anxiety is an inflated heuristic field that overestimates cost.

**Example.** The volume's inflation factor is the estimated cost divided by the true cost. A true cost of 2 estimated as 6 gives a factor of 3, far above the value near 1 the volume assigns to healthy cognition, which is its picture of anxiety.
