# Style holonomy

**id.** style-holonomy
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Aesthetics, Volume 13 of the Geometric Series

## definition

For a closed loop on the aesthetic manifold that starts and ends at a point $p$, the holonomy is the linear map on the tangent space at $p$ given by parallel transport of feature vectors around the loop. If it is the identity for every loop the connection is locally flat, and otherwise the connection has curvature.

**Example.** If transport around a loop turns every tangent vector by a quarter turn, the vector (1, 0) comes back as (0, 1). The holonomy is not the identity, so the connection is curved there.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** The holonomy of a connection in Riemannian geometry.
