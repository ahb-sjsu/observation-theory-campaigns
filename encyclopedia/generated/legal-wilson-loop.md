# Legal Wilson loop

**id.** legal-wilson-loop
**kind.** instrument
**volume.** Geometric Law, Volume 5 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The ordered product of the Hohfeldian gauge transformations along the edges of a closed path of cases. If the product is the identity the cycle is consistent. Any other group element means the chain of precedents returns to its starting point with the rights and duties relabeled.

**Example.** In the volume's running example the four edges carry rs, e, e and s. The product is rs times s, and since s times s is the identity the loop equals r, which is not the identity, so the cycle is inconsistent.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** Wilson loops and holonomy in gauge theory.

## conditions

- Each edge needs a group element saying how the citing case relabels the positions of the cited case. In the volume's examples these are assigned by reading the cases.
- Relabeling at the vertices changes the product by conjugation. Only its conjugacy class, and so whether it is trivial, is gauge-invariant.
- The proposition that a body of case law is consistent if and only if all loops are trivial is stated without proof.
- Chapter 3 states that the time-ordered citation graph is acyclic. The running example closes its loop with a return step from the last case to the first that is not a citation.
