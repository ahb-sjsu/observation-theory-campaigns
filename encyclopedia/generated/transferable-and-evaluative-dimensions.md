# transferable and evaluative dimensions

**id.** transferable-and-evaluative-dimensions
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Economics, Volume 4 of the Geometric Series

## definition

Dimensions $d_1$, $d_2$ and $d_4$ are transferable, meaning a gain to one party requires a matching cost to another in a closed bilateral exchange. Dimensions $d_3$ and $d_5$ to $d_9$ are evaluative, meaning both parties can gain on them at once.

**Example.** A payment of 5 dollars changes the buyer by -5 and the seller by +5 on $d_1$, which sums to zero. In the volume's illustration both parties gain trust, +0.3 and +0.4, which sums to +0.7.

- Evaluative dimensions are not necessarily conserved. Chapter 9 adds that they can also fall for both parties.
