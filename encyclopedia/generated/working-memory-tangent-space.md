## definition

Working memory at a cognitive state is identified with the tangent space of the cognitive manifold at that state, equipped with the attention metric. Its effective capacity is the participation ratio of the metric eigenvalues, the square of their sum divided by the sum of their squares.

**Example.** Metric eigenvalues 3 and 1 give $(3 + 1)^2 / (9 + 1) = 1.6$ effective dimensions, the same value Section 10.11 of the volume computes for weights 0.75 and 0.25.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** The participation ratio. The volume sets the capacity beside the item limits of Miller and of Cowan.
