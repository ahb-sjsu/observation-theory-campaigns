# Reversion axiom

**id.** reversion-axiom
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Ethics, Volume 2 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A right granted on evidence lasts only while the evidence supports it. When the attested evidence behind an authority stratum stays below the bar that admitted it for a declared interval, the stratum is left and every right it granted reverts with it.

**Example.** Take a bar of 2 fresh attested witnesses and a declared interval of 3 cycles (illustrative numbers). Counts of 2, 1, 2, 1 never stay below the bar for 3 cycles, so the right holds. Counts of 2, 1, 1, 1 do, so the stratum is left on the fourth cycle and the right reverts.

## conditions

- The volume tags it as a modeling axiom. It is a design rule for authority strata, not a theorem.
- The interval is a penumbral zone (Definition 8.9) given a width in time. Evidence that comes and goes inside the interval does not switch the rights off and on.
- It applies to authority strata entered on attested evidence. The bar and the interval are declared by the scene and are not fixed by the volume.
