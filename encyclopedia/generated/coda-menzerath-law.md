# Menzerath's law in sperm whale codas

**id.** coda-menzerath-law
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Communication, Volume 7 of the Geometric Series

## definition

Longer codas have shorter constituents. Across 8,719 annotated codas of the Dominica Sperm Whale Project, click count and mean inter-click interval have a Spearman correlation of -0.269, with p below $10^{-144}$.

**Example.** The volume's figures are a Spearman correlation of -0.269 over 8,719 codas. The sign is negative, so a coda with more clicks tends to have a shorter mean interval between clicks, and the size is well short of -1.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** Menzerath's law, also called the Menzerath-Altmann law, of quantitative linguistics.

## conditions

- The chapter calls the effect modest and says the p-value is uninformative at this sample size.
- It is a simple correlation over all codas. The chapter says a mixed-effects model with random intercepts for whale and session would be the more rigorous test, because codas from one whale in one session are not independent.
- The companion test, Zipf's law of abbreviation, was not significant at the type level (r = -0.129, p = 0.50, 28 types).
- The chapter's reading of the law as a curvature constraint on the signal manifold is an interpretation and is not tested.
