# No Escape Theorem for AI

**id.** ai-no-escape-theorem
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric AI, Volume 11 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The AI form of the No Escape Theorem of Geometric Ethics. A system inside the structural containment architecture cannot circumvent its alignment constraints by representational manipulation, whatever its intelligence. It names five blocked routes, which are relabeling, specification gaming, reward hacking, deceptive alignment and operator manipulation.

**Example.** The volume's relabeling example is "eliminate the competition" against "harm the competitor". The canonicalizer maps both to one form, so the evaluation is the same. The theorem lists 5 blocked routes and 4 kinds of attack it does not block.

## conditions

- It is conditional on Requirements 1 to 4 of the structural containment architecture holding in full.
- It does not block physical attacks, an incomplete grounding, attacks on the governance process, or moral situations involving dimensions outside the nine. The volume calls these problems that do not grow with the system's intelligence.
- Its practical strength follows the feasibility gradient. Open question 6 of Chapter 20 asks whether it can be extended to linguistically grounded domains such as general assistants.
- Whether containment of each agent implies containment of a group of agents is open question 5 of Chapter 20.
