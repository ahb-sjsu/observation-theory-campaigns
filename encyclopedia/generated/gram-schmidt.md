# Gram and Schmidt

**id.** gram-schmidt
**kind.** concept

## definition

The procedure that turns independent vectors into an orthonormal basis for their span, dividing the first by its length and subtracting from each next vector its projections onto the basis made so far. Primer L section L.5.

**Example.** From (3, 4) and (1, 0), the first basis vector is (0.6, 0.8), then (1, 0) - 0.6 (0.6, 0.8) = (0.64, -0.48), so the second is (0.8, -0.6).
