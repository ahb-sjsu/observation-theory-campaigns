# educational heuristic field

**id.** educational-heuristic-field
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Education, Volume 9 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A non-negative function h on the learner manifold that estimates the remaining cost from a learner state to the goal region. It is a weighted sum of the estimates supplied by teachers, parents, peers, institutions and the student's own metacognition.

**Example.** With weights of 0.5 on the teacher and 0.5 on the student's own estimate, and estimates of 4 and 6, the field value is 0.5 times 4 plus 0.5 times 6, which is 5. The other three sources have weight zero in this example.

## conditions

- The weights of the five sources depend on the student's context and developmental stage. The volume gives no values for them.
- A strong field gives near-geodesic paths and a weak field gives detours and dead ends. This is the volume's reading of guidance, mentoring and advising, and it is posited.
- The formal development is in Chapter 6, where the teacher's component and the parents' component are defined separately.
