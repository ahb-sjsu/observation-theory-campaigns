# probability

**id.** probability
**kind.** concept

## definition

The fraction of repetitions in which an event would occur if the experiment were repeated without end, a number between zero and one, obeying the complement rule and the addition rule. Primer S, equation S.1.

**Example.** Two fair dice sum to seven in 6 of the 36 ordered pairs, probability one sixth.

## equation

Book equation S.1.

    \Pr[A^{c}]=1-\Pr[A],\qquad \Pr[A\cup B]=\Pr[A]+\Pr[B]-\Pr[A\cap B].
