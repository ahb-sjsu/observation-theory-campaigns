# z-score

**id.** z-score
**kind.** concept

## definition

A value with the mean subtracted and the result divided by the standard deviation, the number of standard deviations it sits from the mean. Standardizing a column is the same operation with the column's own mean and spread. Primer S section S.2.

**Example.** A roll of six has z-score (6 - 3.5)/1.708 = 1.46.
