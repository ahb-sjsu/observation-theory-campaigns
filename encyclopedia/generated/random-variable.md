# random variable

**id.** random-variable
**kind.** concept

## definition

A number attached to each outcome of an experiment, described by its distribution, the values it can take with the probability of each. Primer S, equation S.4.

**Example.** The number a fair die shows is a random variable taking 1 to 6 with probability one sixth each.

## equation

Book equation S.4.

    \mathbb E[X]=\sum_x x\,\Pr[X=x],\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb E\big[(X-\mu)^{2}\big]=\mathbb E[X^{2}]-\mu^{2},\qquad \sigma=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}.
