# nine dimensions of the economic decision manifold

**id.** economic-decision-dimensions
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Economics, Volume 4 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The nine coordinates on which the volume scores an economic state. They are consequences $d_1$, rights $d_2$, fairness $d_3$, autonomy $d_4$, privacy and trust $d_5$, social impact $d_6$, virtue and identity $d_7$, legitimacy $d_8$ and epistemic status $d_9$. Only $d_1$ is visible to a purely monetary agent.

**Example.** In the volume's running example a salary of 3,200 dollars a month is scored on $d_1$, a wish to pay at least 18 dollars an hour on $d_3$, and payroll tax compliance on $d_8$.
