# orthogonal matrix

**id.** orthogonal-matrix
**kind.** concept

## definition

A square matrix with orthonormal columns, whose transpose is its inverse and which preserves every length, dot product, and angle. The rotations and reflections. Primer L, equation L.10.

**Example.** Rows (0, -1) and (1, 0) rotate the plane by ninety degrees, sending (1, 0) to (0, 1).

## equation

Book equation L.10.

    Q^{\top}Q=I,\qquad \|Qx\|=\|x\|,\qquad (Qx)\cdot(Qy)=x\cdot y.
