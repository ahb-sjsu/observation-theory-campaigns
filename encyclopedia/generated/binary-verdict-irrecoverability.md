# Irrecoverability of the binary verdict and the scalar sentence

**id.** binary-verdict-irrecoverability
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Law, Volume 5 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A binary verdict maps the eight-dimensional legal space to one bit, and a sentence maps it to one number. Neither map is injective, so neither preserves the distances between cases, and nothing computed from the verdict or sentence alone recovers the dimensions that produced it.

**Example.** Two cases with first scores 0.9 and 0.6 that both receive the verdict 1 cannot be told apart from the verdict, although they differ by 0.3 on that dimension.
