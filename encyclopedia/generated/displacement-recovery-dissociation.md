# displacement-recovery dissociation

**id.** displacement-recovery-dissociation
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Cognition, Volume 6 of the Geometric Series

## definition

On the E2 emotional anchoring test, how far a perturbation moves a model's judgment and how often an inhibition instruction restores it vary independently across models. The volume assigns displacement to the metric and recovery to the control layer.

**Example.** The volume prints for Claude the largest displacement statistic, t = 5.10, and the lowest recovery, 0.20, and for Gemini 2.0 Flash t = 3.80 and recovery 0.73. The products in Technical Appendix 11A are 5.10 times 0.20 = 1.02 and 3.80 times 0.73 = 2.77.

## conditions

- The evidence is five models on one subtask, and the run records are not in the repository.
- Claude's high displacement statistic partly reflects its low stochastic variation, which shrinks the denominator of the t statistic.
- Recovery rates for the same models differ between the volume's appendices. Technical Appendix 6B prints 0.29, 0.43 and 0.20 for Gemini 2.5 Flash, Gemini 3 Flash and Gemini 2.5 Pro, and Technical Appendix 11A prints 0.48, 0.52 and 0.45.
- The reading of emotional anchoring as an executive control failure is held at posited in the ledger (GC-14-I).
