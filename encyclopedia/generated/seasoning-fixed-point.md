# seasoning fixed point

**id.** seasoning-fixed-point
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Gastronomy, Volume 12 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A dish is properly seasoned at a point $\mathbf{f}^\star$ that is a fixed point of taste-and-adjust, a local maximum of the hedonic function $H$. No small admissible move raises $H$, the gradient is about zero and the Hessian is negative semidefinite.

**Example.** On one salt axis with $H(s) = -(s - 2)^2$, the gradient at 2 is 0 and the second derivative is -2, so 2 is the fixed point. A step to 2.1 or to 1.9 lowers $H$ by 0.01 either way. The function is illustrative.

## conditions

- Because the inverse operators are lossy, the volume's prudent dynamics is small steps from below. Under-seasoning stops short of the peak and over-seasoning overshoots it.
