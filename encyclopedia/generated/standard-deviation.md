# standard deviation

**id.** standard-deviation
**kind.** concept

## definition

The square root of the variance, the spread of a variable in its own units. Scaling a variable by a scales it by the size of a, and shifting it changes nothing. Primer S, equation S.4.

**Example.** A fair die has variance 2.917 and standard deviation 1.708.

## equation

Book equation S.4.

    \mathbb E[X]=\sum_x x\,\Pr[X=x],\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb E\big[(X-\mu)^{2}\big]=\mathbb E[X^{2}]-\mu^{2},\qquad \sigma=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}.
