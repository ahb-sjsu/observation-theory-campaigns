# Hohfeldian D4 group

**id.** hohfeldian-d4-group
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Law, Volume 5 of the Geometric Series

## definition

Withdrawn pending restatement under the Klein four-group. The statement was that the four first-order Hohfeldian positions, right, duty, liberty and no-right, are permuted by a correlative rotation $r$ of order 4 and an opposite swap $s$ of order 2 that satisfy $srs = r^{-1}$ and generate the dihedral group of order 8. The volume's note of 2026-11-26 says there is no quarter-turn. The correlative and the opposite are commuting involutions and generate the Klein four-group $V_4$, of order 4.

**Example.** Write c for the correlative and s for the opposite. Applying c then s to right gives duty and then liberty. Applying s then c gives no-right and then liberty. The two orders agree, as they must in the Klein four-group, and no product of c and s cycles the four positions.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** Hohfeld's jural correlatives and opposites. The withdrawn group is the dihedral group of symmetries of a square, and the group that stands is the Klein four-group.
