# Hohfeldian D4 group

**id.** hohfeldian-d4-group
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Law, Volume 5 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The four first-order Hohfeldian positions, right, duty, liberty and no-right, are permuted by a correlative rotation $r$ of order 4 and an opposite swap $s$ of order 2 that satisfy $srs = r^{-1}$. These generate the dihedral group of order 8, which acts faithfully on the four positions.

**Example.** Apply s, then r, then s to right. Right goes to no-right, no-right goes to right, and right goes to no-right. The reverse rotation also sends right to no-right, so the two agree on this position, as the volume checks.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** Hohfeld's jural correlatives and opposites, and the dihedral group of symmetries of a square.

## conditions

- Geometric Ethics, the founding volume, withdrew the dihedral group for the Hohfeldian positions in its version 1.25 and uses the Klein four-group. This volume's text predates that change and has not been revised.
- The rotation $r$ sends right to duty, duty to liberty, liberty to no-right and no-right to right. The swap $s$ exchanges right with no-right and duty with liberty.
- The result covers the first square only. The second square of power, liability, immunity and disability carries its own copy, and the two are joined in the octad gauge group.
- Reading each of the eight elements as a re-description that leaves the underlying legal bond unchanged is the volume's interpretation. That evaluation must be invariant under them is a separate axiom.
