# uniform distribution

**id.** uniform-distribution
**kind.** concept

## definition

Equally likely to fall anywhere in an interval, with mean one half and variance one twelfth on the unit interval. A correctly computed p-value is uniform under its null. Primer S section S.3.

**Example.** Forty true nulls produce forty p-values uniform on the unit interval, and about two of them fall below 0.05.
