# unit vector

**id.** unit-vector
**kind.** concept

## definition

A vector of length one, a pure direction. Any nonzero vector becomes one when divided by its length. Primer L, equation L.2.

**Example.** (3, 4) has length 5, so its unit vector is (0.6, 0.8).

## equation

Book equation L.2.

    \|x\|=\sqrt{x_1^{2}+\cdots+x_d^{2}},\qquad u=\frac{x}{\|x\|},\qquad \|u\|=1.
