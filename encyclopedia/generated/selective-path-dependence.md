# Selective Path Dependence (Proposition 13.3)

**id.** selective-path-dependence
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Communication, Volume 7 of the Geometric Series

## definition

With normative relations modelled by a $D_4$ gauge group on the Hohfeldian square, the order in which two considerations are presented changes the judgment only on cross-type paths, where the factors point toward different vertices. On within-type paths the gate elements commute and the Wilson loop is the identity.

**Example.** With the volume's actions r taking O to C to L to N to O and s exchanging O with C and L with N, applying r then s to O gives s(C) = O, and applying s then r gives r(C) = L. The two orders disagree, and in the volume's table 2 of 8 scenarios showed an order effect.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** Order effects in judgment, stated in the vocabulary of lattice gauge theory. Geometric Ethics v1.25 withdrew the dihedral group D4 for Hohfeldian transitions, a withdrawal this volume's text does not record.
