# economic singularity

**id.** economic-singularity
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Economics, Volume 4 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A region of the decision manifold where the covariance matrix Sigma becomes degenerate, with its determinant going to zero, so that the metric, its inverse, diverges. There the geodesic equation has no finite-cost solution and price discovery fails. The volume models a financial crisis as this event.

**Example.** For two dimensions with unit variances and correlation r the determinant of Sigma is $1 - r^2$. It is 0.64 at r = 0.6 and 0.19 at r = 0.9, and it goes to zero as r goes to 1.
