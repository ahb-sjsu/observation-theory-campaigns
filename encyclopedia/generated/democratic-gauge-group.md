# Democratic gauge group

**id.** democratic-gauge-group
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Politics, Volume 10 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The group $G_D$ of changes to a voting function's input that should leave its output unchanged. It is generated by voter anonymity, option neutrality and re-description invariance. A voting function invariant under it is called democratically well-posed.

**Example.** Five ballots read A, A, A, B, B. Swapping the ballots of the first and fourth voters gives B, A, A, A, B, and the count is still 3 to 2 for A, as anonymity requires.

## conditions

- Option neutrality means that relabeling the candidates permutes the output in the same way. It does not mean the output is fixed.
- The volume reads Arrow's axioms as conditions of this kind. That reading is an interpretation given in prose in Chapters 5 and 7.
- Chapter 17 asks whether invariance under this group yields a conserved democratic legitimacy. It calls the idea speculative because no political Lagrangian has been specified.
