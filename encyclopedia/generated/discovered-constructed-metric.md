# Discovered-constructed metric

**id.** discovered-constructed-metric
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Aesthetics, Volume 13 of the Geometric Series

## definition

The volume's position on where the aesthetic metric comes from. The encoder and the corpus are constructions that could have been otherwise. Given them, the extracted features (divergences, coherence, trajectory statistics, Lasso axes) are what the representation forces, and that layer is called discovered.

**Example.** For books the three reporting levels the volume asks for are R = 0.241 for the full model, R = 0.093 for the within-genre residual, and a correlation near 0.70 for the features that transfer across languages.

## conditions

- The evidence offered against pure construction and pure convention is the cross-lingual invariance result. The evidence offered for the conventional share is the genre confound.
- Section 9.8 asks that the metric be reported at three levels. They are the full model with genre in it, the within-stratum residual, and the features that transfer across language families.
- Section 17.7 calls the metric's status as the aesthetic metric provisional, an artifact of a particular encoder and a particular reception signal.
- Appendix F marks as proved by construction only that a metric can be declared and is not forced by the geometry (F9.1).
