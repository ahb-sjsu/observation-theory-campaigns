# Aesthetic Scalar Irrecoverability (Theorem 15.1)

**id.** aesthetic-scalar-irrecoverability
**kind.** result
**volume.** Geometric Aesthetics, Volume 13 of the Geometric Series

## definition

Any continuous function $Q$ from a $d$-dimensional aesthetic tensor to a scalar verdict, with $d > 1$, is not injective, so distinct works share a verdict. No function recovers the tensor from the scalar. The geodesic on the full manifold generally differs from the path that is optimal for the scalar.

**Example.** Take the verdict Q(x, y) = x + y. The works (1, 3) and (2, 2) both score 4, so no rule can tell from the 4 which of them was rated.

**Known as, or related to prior art.** The Scalar Irrecoverability Theorem of Geometric Methods and Geometric Ethics, in aesthetic form.
