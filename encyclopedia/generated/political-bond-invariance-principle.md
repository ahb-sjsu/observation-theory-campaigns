# Political Bond Invariance Principle

**id.** political-bond-invariance-principle
**kind.** concept
**volume.** Geometric Politics, Volume 10 of the Geometric Series

## definition

A democratic evaluation, such as an election, a poll or a representation assessment, is epistemically well-posed if and only if it is invariant under voter anonymity, option neutrality and re-description invariance.

**Example.** Suppose the same voters with the same preferences elect A when A is listed first and B when B is listed first. The outcome depends on the labeling of the ballot, so the election fails option neutrality and is not well-posed under the principle.
