# false positive rate

**id.** false-positive-rate
**kind.** concept

## definition

The fraction of negatives a classifier labels positive, which like recall does not depend on the base rate, so that classifiers are compared across datasets by those two and not by precision. Primer S section S.9.

**Example.** 10 false positives among 950 negatives is a false positive rate of 0.011.
