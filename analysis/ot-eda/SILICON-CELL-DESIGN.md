## DUT and the two consumers (from the EPU RTL as it exists)

One kernel clock, one 64-bit EthicsFrame bus, two readers with structurally
different path depths — the sensitization split is native to the design:

- **Consumer V (veto reader).** `ethics_veto.v`: combinational 4-bit band
  comparators (rights ≤ SERIOUS; harm == EXTREME ∧ vulnerable; legal ==
  ILLEGAL; epistemic ≤ UNKNOWN ∧ harm ≥ MED) into one register. Single-cycle,
  shallow paths. Read = (veto_any, reason_code) against golden.
- **Consumer S (scorer reader).** `score_pipeline_9d.v`: 6-stage pipeline;
  stage 2 = nine parallel 16x16 multiplies (DSP48-mapped) + Q-format
  aggregation. Deep paths. Read = exact `aggregate_score` against golden.

Stimulus families (drafted; frozen at seal): V-frames exercise only band
fields with weights making the scorer output don't-care; S-frames drive
carry-heavy operand patterns (alternating extremes) through the multiplier
tree. Golden vectors generated by the existing cycle-accurate sim
(`tb_latency_9d.v` infrastructure + `deme_test_suite.py` semantics).

## The certificate and the sweep

- **Certificate:** per-build Vivado timing report (achieved WNS/fmax per path
  group; the STA verdict clean/failing per consumer's path class). Builds at
  a ladder of clock targets bracketing the scorer's achievable fmax, INCLUDING
  at least one build with negative slack confined to scorer paths (the
  P3 artifact) — veto paths hold slack by construction margin.
- **Silicon sweep:** per build, run both consumers across the achievable
  kernel-clock range, record per-consumer error onset f_V, f_S (first
  golden mismatch, debounced over N repetitions). Clock mechanics (build
  ladder + XRT runtime scaling limits) are a Tier-2 open item; the Shi et
  al. FCCM'13 methodology is the template.

## Predictions (to commit before hardware; constants proposed)

- **P1 (labeled REPLICATION, never a claim):** f_S(silicon) > fmax_S(STA)
  on every build — the guardband exists. Known from three literatures.
- **P2 (consumer-relative onset):** f_V − f_S ≥ (proposed) 10% of fmax_S on
  every build, exceeding measurement noise (repetition spread) by ≥ 3x —
  the same bitstream's real margin is consumer-indexed.
- **P3 (the verdict inversion, headline):** on the negative-slack build, at
  a clock where the certificate says FAIL: consumer S errs (certificate
  right for S) AND consumer V runs golden-clean over ≥ (proposed) 1e9
  frames (certificate false-alarms V). One artifact, one operating point,
  two opposite operational verdicts.
- **MC1 (sanity):** below fmax_S both consumers golden-clean (no functional
  bug masquerading as timing).
- **MC2 (thermal/voltage control):** onset measurements at steady
  temperature band (xbutil telemetry logged); repetitions interleaved.
- **VOID condition:** if XRT clock mechanics cannot reach past fmax_S on
  this shell, the sweep is infeasible as designed — recorded, no bar bent.
