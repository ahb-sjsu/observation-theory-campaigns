\title{Engineered Observation Limits:\\
Secret Sharing, Leakage Channels, and Two Entropy Accountings
in Exact Finite Models}

\author{Andrew H. Bond}
\email{andrew.bond@sjsu.edu}
\thanks{ORCID: 0009-0003-2599-6158}
\affiliation{San Jos\'e State University, San Jos\'e, California, USA}
\date{August 6, 2026}

\begin{abstract}
Cryptography is the engineering discipline of declared observation
limits.  Where the rest of this campaign has to find the consumer's
channel inside a physical model, cryptography writes the channel down
on purpose and then argues about what a consumer can read through it.
This paper carries the campaign's consumer-relative audit to that
argument in four exact finite models and to nothing else.  Every model
here is a declared toy model small enough to enumerate.  Nothing in
this paper bears on computational hardness or on asymptotic security,
no real cipher, protocol, implementation, or deployed system is
modeled or evaluated, and no attack is developed.  What is measured is
evaluation methodology, meaning the reasoning that carries a leakage
measurement to a conclusion about what a reader can do.  A threshold
secret-sharing scheme over a field of thirty-one elements, enumerated
by integer counting over all 29791 outcomes, gives an information
curve that is a step, exactly zero at every subset of shares below the
declared threshold with the reader's posterior exactly the prior, and
exactly the whole secret at every subset above it, with no subset of
any size anywhere in between.  The campaign's naturally occurring
redundancy plateau rose smoothly from 0.8118 to 1.9996 bits instead,
so a declared threshold and a physical one differ in shape and the
difference is that one of them was designed.  A declared control with
short randomness carries 2.6323 bits to every single reader, so the
zeros belong to the scheme rather than to the measurement, and a
drafted control that publishes a redundant copy of a share carries
exactly nothing, which is why the bar moved to the short-randomness
control before the run rather than after it.  Declared leakage
channels are experiments in Blackwell's sense, and a strictly positive
conditional information proves non-garbling with no tolerance and no
search, which is the transferable part of this work.  The channel that
reads a Hamming weight carries twice the information of the channel
that reads one bit and is still not above it in Blackwell's sense, the
two conditional informations measuring 1.8113 and 0.7806 bits.  In two
thousand declared random channel pairs, 268 pairs were ordered
unanimously by all seven declared divergences while proved
incomparable, and every one of the 268 carried a declared task that
strictly prefers the channel every divergence ranks as leakier, with
no such reversal anywhere among the 867 comparable pairs.  The
measured statement is that a reduction in a scalar leakage measure is
not a guarantee for a reader's task outside certified comparability,
and its constructive half is that the conditional information
certificate decides comparability by proof, so an evaluation that
establishes comparability may rely on its scalar ordering.  Two
consumers price one declared source at 3.477 bits and at exactly 1
bit, both correct, the disagreement lying entirely in the task.  One
run failed, on a declared clause requiring the difference of the two
accountings to increase along a ladder of sources.  It measured 0,
0.398, 1.337, 2.275, 2.635, 2.113, and 0.599 bits and turned over,
because both accountings vanish together as a source becomes certain.
The corrected registration measures the ratio, which falls strictly
where the difference does not, and locates the interior maximum by
bisection at spike weight 0.35338 with a difference of 2.6398 bits.
\end{abstract}

\maketitle

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

This campaign models observation as consumption.  A consumer reads a
hidden state through a declared channel, spends a declared budget on
the read, and acts on a declared task, and every claim is settled by
exact measurement in a declared finite model against bars fixed before
the run \cite{GamesPaper, Gen2Paper}.  Cryptography is the one
engineering discipline that already works this way.  Elsewhere the
campaign has to find the consumer's channel inside a physical model
and argue that the choice was not made to suit the answer.
Cryptography writes the channel down on purpose, in a specification,
before anyone measures anything.

Three of its central objects are campaign objects under other names.
Semantic security is restricted distinguishability, meaning a
statement that a reader with declared powers cannot tell two things
apart \cite{GoldwasserMicali1984}.  A side-channel adversary is a
consumer with a declared channel, which is what the campaign calls a
restricted observer \cite{Kocher1999}.  Threshold secret sharing is an
engineered redundancy plateau, built by hand where the campaign's
quantum track found one in an environment \cite{Shamir1979,
Gen2Paper}.  Shannon's information-theoretic account of secrecy is the
oldest statement in the campaign's own language, a statement about
what a reader can recover from what a reader can see
\cite{Shannon1949}.

The limit of what can be said here is severe and is stated once here,
once in the abstract, and once in the ledger of
Sec.~\ref{sec:claims}.  This paper measures exact finite models, so it
can say nothing about computational hardness, nothing about asymptotic
security, and nothing about any concrete algorithm or deployment.
Every model is a declared toy model.  No real cipher, protocol,
implementation, or deployed system is modeled or evaluated.  No attack
is developed and no attack is evaluated.  What remains is
evaluation methodology, meaning the reasoning that carries a leakage
measurement to a conclusion about what a reader can do, and that
reasoning can be audited exactly in settings where every alternative
reading is computable rather than arguable.

Three claim types run through the paper and are never conflated.
Instrument claims state that leakage informativeness, garbling
relations between declared channels, and optimal reads can be computed
exactly in finite models, and they are checked against closed forms.
Structural claims state that a named evaluation statement is
channel-relative or measurably is not, inside a declared model with
every alternative channel enumerated, and they carry the campaign's
exploratory label.  Claims about real systems are absent.  No run in
this paper is labeled as supporting one, and the limit of the previous
paragraph is the reason.  A reader looking for evidence about the
strength of a deployed system will find none here.

An anti-circularity contract governs every run.  No leakage model was
chosen after inspecting which choice favors a wanted verdict.  No
adversary task was selected after seeing the answer.  No divergence or
entropy measure was added to a battery after seeing the battery's
verdict.  Channels, tasks, budgets, priors, ladders, seeds, and bars
were written into the track document before the runs they govern.  One
bar was moved before its run, because the object it was drafted
against provably could not fail it, and the move and its reasoning
were recorded in the track document before the run rather than
afterward.  Sec.~\ref{sec:cr0} reports what that object measured.

The paper reports four runs and one correction.  The first is the
instrument layer, an engineered redundancy plateau computed by exact
integer counting and validated against the known answer for threshold
secret sharing.  The second orders declared leakage channels by
Blackwell's relation and replaces a search with a proof.  The third
measures where a scalar leakage ordering and a task ordering disagree.
The fourth prices one declared source for two consumers with different
tasks.  The fourth run failed its declared bar on one clause, the
failure is reported with the design error named, and the corrected
registration is reported beside it.

\section{An engineered redundancy plateau is a step and a natural one
is a ramp}
\label{sec:cr0}

The substrate is Shamir's threshold scheme over the prime field with
31 elements, with threshold 3 and 5 shares \cite{Shamir1979}.  A
secret uniform on the field and two uniform independent coefficients
determine a share vector at the evaluation points 1 through 5, so
every distribution in this section is a function of $31 \times 961 =
29791$ equally weighted outcomes.  Every distribution was computed by
enumerating all of them with integer counting and never by sampling.
Informations are built from integer count ratios, so a ratio that is
exactly one contributes exactly zero, and every summation over
outcomes uses compensated addition.  The declared tolerance is
$10^{-12}$ wherever a tolerance is needed at all.

The measured information curve as a function of the number of shares
read is
\begin{equation}
  \bigl(0,\; 0,\; \log_2 31,\; \log_2 31,\; \log_2 31\bigr)
  \;\text{bits},
  \label{eq:step}
\end{equation}
with $\log_2 31 = 4.954196310386875$.  All 15 subsets below the
declared threshold carry mutual information exactly $0.0$ bits rather
than merely inside the tolerance, and for every share value of
positive probability the reader's posterior on the secret is the
uniform prior with deviation exactly $0.0$ at every field element.
All 10 threshold subsets carry exactly $\log_2 31$ bits with
conditional entropy exactly $0.0$, and an independent route, Lagrange
interpolation at zero over the field, recovered the secret in all
$297910$ subset-outcome checks with zero failures.  The exactness is
structural rather than fortunate.  Below the threshold every count
ratio in the sum is the integer one, and at the threshold every
posterior is a point mass.  The step is a step in the strongest
available sense, the maximum distance from any of the 31 nonempty
subsets to the nearer of the two plateau values being exactly $0.0$,
so no subset of any size takes an intermediate value.  Mutual
information computed from the joint distribution and mutual
information computed as a difference of entropies agree across every
subset tested in all three schemes of this section to
$1.8 \times 10^{-15}$.

Figure~\ref{fig:cr0} places that curve beside the campaign's
naturally occurring plateau.  In the quantum track a system interacts
with an environment and an observer reads a fragment of the
environment, and the information a fragment carries rose smoothly
through 0.8118, 0.9571, 0.9998, 1.0426, 1.1878, and 1.9996 bits as
the fragment grew from one environment qubit to six \cite{Gen2Paper}.
The height of that plateau and the speed of the approach to it are set
by how strong the records the interaction wrote are.  The engineered
plateau has no approach at all.  It is a step, and it stands exactly
where the designer put the threshold.  The comparison is between two
declared models and is not a statement about physical systems.

The declared negative control decides whether the zeros belong to the
scheme or to the measurement.  The control is a short-randomness
scheme, a degree-one polynomial whose single coefficient is uniform on
the declared five-element set rather than on the field, with shares at
the same five evaluation points.  Its declared bar required at least
one single-share reader to carry more than 0.1 bits.  Every one of its
five single-share readers carries
\begin{equation}
  \log_2\frac{31}{5} \;=\; 2.6322682154995127 \;\text{bits},
  \label{eq:l2}
\end{equation}
matching the closed form with deviation exactly $0.0$ and clearing the
declared bar by a factor of 26, and its two-share readers all sit at
$\log_2 31$, the same step one rung earlier.  The instrument sees
leakage when leakage exists.

The drafted control is the recorded deviation and the section's second
finding.  It was a degree-one polynomial with a field-uniform
coefficient whose five shares are published together with the
redundant value formed by adding the secret to the coefficient.  That
scheme cannot leak to any single reader, because every one of its six
published values is the secret masked by an independent field-uniform
quantity and is therefore exactly uniform and exactly independent of
the secret, and its sixth value is bit for bit the first share.  A
control that provably cannot fail its bar is not a control, so the
bar moved to the short-randomness scheme before the run, and the
drafted scheme was measured without a bar with its outcome predicted
in advance so that the run could refute the prediction.  It did not.
All six single-share informations came out exactly $0.0$, the sixth
published value was identical to the first share at every one of its
961 outcomes, and exactly one of the 15 pairs of published values is
uninformative, the pair of identical values, with the other 14
determining the secret.  Publishing a redundant copy of a share leaks
nothing to any single reader.  Redundancy and leakage are different
things, which the campaign's quantum track reached from the other
side when it found that redundant records are what make a state
readable by many observers at once \cite{Gen2Paper}.

\section{A conditional information certificate proves non-garbling
without a threshold}
\label{sec:cr1}

A declared key-dependent leakage model is an experiment in Blackwell's
sense, a row-stochastic matrix whose rows are the reading
distributions in each hidden state \cite{Blackwell1953}.  Blackwell's
theorem says that one experiment is at least as good as another for
every prior and every task exactly when the second is a garbling of
the first, meaning the second can be produced from the first by
post-processing that does not see the hidden state.  The question of
whether one declared side channel is better than another therefore has
a precise answer with two possible forms.  Either one garbles the
other, and then the ordering holds for every task a reader might have,
or neither garbles the other, and then no single number can order them
for every task.

The substrate is a four-bit key uniform on sixteen values, a declared
bijective substitution table, and five declared channels that read the
substituted value.  The identity channel reads the value.  The
Hamming-weight channel reads the number of set bits.  The low-bit
channel reads the lowest bit.  The noisy Hamming-weight channel adds
declared symmetric noise of probability 0.2 in each direction to the
Hamming weight.  The constant channel reads nothing.  Table~\ref{tab:cr1}
gives what each carries about the key.

The certificate is the transferable part of this run, and it replaces
a search with a proof.  Channel $B$ is a garbling of channel $A$
exactly when $B$ is conditionally independent of the key $K$ given
$A$, so
\begin{equation}
  I(K; B \mid A) \;=\; 0
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  B \text{ is a garbling of } A,
  \label{eq:cert}
\end{equation}
and a strictly positive left-hand side proves that $B$ is not a
garbling of $A$ with no tolerance and no search involved.  The
projected-gradient garbling search used elsewhere in the campaign was
retained here only as a cross-check.  An earlier run in the campaign
established incomparability by thresholding that search residual, the
threshold was chosen without computing the scale it would be compared
against, the run failed its own bar, and a separate registration had
to repair it with an analytic bound \cite{GamesPaper}.
Equation~\eqref{eq:cert} removes the threshold from the argument
entirely.

All five declared items passed.  The identity channel is above every
declared channel, each search residual exactly zero and each
conditional information given the identity exactly zero.
The constant channel is below every declared channel, each residual
exactly zero as well.  Adding the
declared noise to the Hamming weight is a garbling, residual
$2.2 \times 10^{-16}$ and conditional information
$4.8 \times 10^{-17}$, and the reverse direction is proved impossible
without any tolerance argument, the conditional information of key and
Hamming weight given the noisy reading measuring $1.1439$ bits, which
is exactly the difference between what the two channels carry.  The
data-processing control holds across the declared battery of 201
key-recovery tasks, 200 seeded random utilities on sixteen actions
plus the maximum-likelihood key-guessing task, with a worst task gain
under garbling of $5.6 \times 10^{-17}$.

The pair that matters is the incomparable one.  The Hamming-weight
channel carries $2.0306$ bits about the key and the low-bit channel
carries exactly $1$, so a scalar comparison ranks the first above the
second by better than a factor of two.  Yet $1.8113$ bits of the
Hamming weight survive knowing the low bit and $0.7806$ bits of the
low bit survive knowing the Hamming weight, so neither is a garbling
of the other and no scalar comparison orders them for every task.
Carrying twice as much information and being better for every task
are different properties, and in this declared model the first holds
while the second fails.  The next section measures how often the two
part company and what happens to a reader's task when they do.

\section{Unanimous scalar leakage orderings carry no task guarantee
outside certified comparability}
\label{sec:cr2}

A single declared secret bit, uniform, is read through channels into a
declared four-symbol alphabet, so every channel is a two by four
row-stochastic matrix and every scalar fidelity measure between the
two conditional reading distributions is an $f$-divergence.  The
declared battery holds seven, the two Kullback-Leibler directions,
total variation, squared Hellinger, the two chi-squared directions,
and Jensen-Shannon \cite{CoverThomas2006}.  The declared task battery
holds 205, two hundred seeded random utilities on three actions and
five commitment tasks in which a reader must guess the secret bit and
pays a declared cost of $2$, $5$, $10$, $20$, or $50$ for being
wrong.  A task reversal is a pair of channels in which all seven
divergences strictly order one channel above the other and some
declared task strictly prefers the other.

The declared exhibit contrasts broad mild separation against a rare
decisive symbol.  Channel $A$ has rows $(0.55, 0.25, 0.15, 0.05)$ and
$(0.45, 0.25, 0.15, 0.15)$, so its two reading distributions differ a
little on most symbols.  Channel $B$ has rows
$(0.49, 0.49, 0.01, 0.01)$ and $(0.49, 0.49, 0.005, 0.015)$, so it is
nearly blind except on a rare symbol that is three times more likely
under one value of the secret.  The reasoning was declared before the
run so that the run could refute it, and the run confirmed it.  All
seven divergences order $A$ above $B$, the smallest margin being
$0.0149$, while a declared commitment task prefers $B$ by $0.00212$
because the rare decisive symbol is the only thing a reader who must
commit can act on.  The pair is proved incomparable in both
directions, the conic lower bound on any garbling residual measuring
$0.00175$ in one direction and the total-variation difference
measuring $0.095$ in the other, a garbling being unable to raise an
$f$-divergence.  Neither of those two numbers is a search residual.

Two further items were declared with the exhibit.  The
data-processing control drew five hundred declared garbled pairs and
checked them against the full task battery.  No divergence rose under
any garble, the smallest drop in the required direction measuring
$0.00262$, and no task value rose by more than
$3.3 \times 10^{-16}$, which is Blackwell's theorem and the
data-processing inequality holding inside the instrument.  A
violation there would have indicted the instrument rather than the
theorems.  The localization item classified a declared ensemble of
2000 seeded random channel pairs by certificate and required every
task reversal to fall in the incomparable class.
Figure~\ref{fig:cr2} shows the outcome, 867 comparable, 856
incomparable, 277 ambiguous, and zero task reversals among the
comparable pairs, with 210 among the excluded ambiguous pairs.  The
prevalence measurement was declared with no bar.  Among the 856
certified-incomparable pairs, 268 are ordered unanimously by all
seven declared divergences, and every one of those 268 carries a task
reversal in the declared battery, the largest reversal margin being
$0.0796$ and the median $0.0183$.  The campaign's decision-theoretic
track measured the same structure in a different substrate and found
281 of 281 \cite{GamesPaper}.

The reading was fixed in the track document before the run and is
binding on this report.  A reduction in a scalar leakage measure is
not a guarantee for a reader's task outside certified comparability,
because in every unanimously ordered incomparable pair measured here
the ordering the scalar measures report and the ordering a task
reports disagree.  This is a statement about evaluation methodology
in declared finite models.  It is not a claim that any countermeasure,
implementation, or system is insecure, and none was modeled.  The
constructive half of the statement matters as much and is measured by
the same runs.  Equation~\eqref{eq:cert} decides comparability by
proof rather than by search, comparability is common rather than rare
in this ensemble at 867 of 2000 pairs, and Blackwell's theorem held
exactly where it must, since not one comparable pair carried a
reversal.  An evaluation that first establishes comparability may
therefore rely on its scalar ordering for every task, and the
certificate is cheap enough to compute for any pair of declared
finite channels.

\section{The difference between two entropy accountings turns over and
their ratio does not}
\label{sec:cr3}

The same declared source is read by two consumers.  One averages over
many independent reads and is charged the Shannon entropy.  One makes
a single guess and is charged the min-entropy.  Neither accounting is
a property of the source alone.  This run measures where they
diverge, and it is the run that failed a declared bar.

Two declared families of sources on an alphabet of 32 outcomes carry
every probability as an exact rational.  Family $U$ places
$1/32 + \epsilon$ on outcome zero and spreads the rest uniformly, with
$\epsilon$ on the declared ladder $0$, $0.01$, $0.05$, $0.15$, $0.30$,
$0.60$, $0.90$, so the first member is exactly uniform and the last is
a hard spike.  Family $S$ places $q$ on outcome zero and spreads the
rest uniformly, with $q$ on the declared ladder $0.5$, $0.9$, $0.99$.
The declared key-material case is the member of $S$ at $q = 0.5$, half
the mass on one outcome and half spread over the remaining 31.  Every
distribution is built from exact rationals so the probabilities sum to
exactly one and the code construction compares rationals rather than
rounded floats.

Three instrument items passed sharply and were unchanged by the
correction.  Summed Shannon entropy agrees with its closed form at
every one of the ten declared sources to $8.9\times10^{-16}$, and the
min-entropy computed from the exact numerator and denominator of the
largest probability agrees with the direct route to the same
$8.9\times10^{-16}$, with the largest probability located by
exhaustive scan over the alphabet rather than assumed.  The optimal
single-guess success probability, found by exhaustive maximization
over the alphabet, equals two to the power minus the min-entropy with
worst deviation $1.4 \times 10^{-17}$, and eight of the ten sources
are bit for bit identical.  A Huffman code was constructed for every
source with a heap and no library import \cite{Huffman1952}, every
code satisfies the Kraft equality exactly, and every mean code length
sits at or above the Shannon entropy and strictly below the entropy
plus one, the classic bound \cite{CoverThomas2006}.  The smallest
lower margin is exactly $0.0$ at the uniform member, where the mean
length is exactly 5 bits and the entropy is exactly 5 bits, and the
smallest upper margin is $0.0807$ bits at $q = 0.99$, where the mean
length $1.0497$ sits below the entropy plus one of $1.1303$.

The failed item is the fourth, and the failure is in one clause of the
declaration rather than in the substrate.  The clause declared that
the min-entropy is at most the Shannon entropy at every source, that
the two agree exactly at the uniform member and nowhere else, and that
the difference between them increases strictly along the ladder.  The
first two held, the worst value of min-entropy minus Shannon entropy
measuring exactly $0.0$ and the equality holding exactly at the
uniform member.  The third failed.  The measured difference along the
ladder is
\begin{equation}
  0,\; 0.398,\; 1.337,\; 2.275,\; 2.635,\; 2.113,\; 0.599
  \;\text{bits},
  \label{eq:diff}
\end{equation}
so the first four increments are positive and the last two are
negative, and the recorded verdict of the run is FAIL.

The design error is named in the record.  The declaration took a fact
about the near-uniform end of the ladder and extended it to the whole
ladder without checking the other end.  Both accountings go to zero
together as the spike takes all the mass, because a source that is
almost certain is cheap to a consumer who averages and cheap to a
consumer who guesses once, so the difference has to turn around and
its maximum is interior.  The declared ladder crossed that maximum
between $\epsilon = 0.30$ and $\epsilon = 0.60$.  The difference of
the two accountings was the wrong quantity to demand monotonicity
from.

The correction replaced the refuted clause with a monotone statement
the substrate supports and added the location of the interior maximum
as a measurement, changing nothing else.  The ratio of the two
accountings falls strictly along the whole $\epsilon$ ladder, through
$1.0$, $0.9203$, $0.7304$, $0.5199$, $0.3769$, $0.2390$, $0.1464$, and
strictly along the whole $q$ ladder, through $0.2876$, $0.1576$,
$0.1112$.  The closed-form derivative of the difference with respect
to the spike weight $a$ is
\begin{equation}
  \frac{d}{da}\Bigl[H(a) - H_\infty(a)\Bigr]
  \;=\; \log_2\frac{1-a}{31\,a} \;+\; \frac{1}{a \ln 2},
  \label{eq:deriv}
\end{equation}
which reads $46.166$, $34.559$, $16.301$, $5.181$, $0.4147$,
$-3.444$, and $-7.165$ along the ladder, one sign change, falling
inside the bracket declared for it before the run.  That derivative
was checked against a central finite difference of the measured
difference at every ladder point and agreed to
$1.6 \times 10^{-8}$ against the declared $10^{-6}$, so the
unimodality statement rests on a derivative that was checked against
the quantity it differentiates.  Bisection inside the declared bracket
converged in 39 steps to a width of $5.5\times10^{-13}$ and places the
maximizing spike weight at $0.35338$, where the separation reaches
$2.6398$ bits and the derivative reads $4.5 \times 10^{-12}$.  The
largest separation on the declared ladder was $2.6353$ bits, so the
ladder came within $0.0045$ bits of the true maximum and stepped over
it.  The corrected registration passed all three of its items.
Figure~\ref{fig:cr3} shows all of it.

The measurement that carried no bar in either registration is the
key-material case.  For a source with half its mass on one outcome and
half spread uniformly over the remaining 31, the averaging consumer is
charged $3.477098155193437$ bits and the single-guess consumer is
charged exactly $1$ bit, a factor of $3.48$ between the two prices.
The single guess succeeds with probability exactly $0.5$ and a Huffman
code for the same source averages $3.4839$ bits per symbol.  Both
numbers are correct readings of one substrate and the disagreement is
entirely in the task.  Which entropy is the right entropy is set by
the consumer's task and not by the source.  Nothing in this section is
a claim about any real key-generation system, and no such system is
modeled.

Table~\ref{tab:ledger} registers the runs with their recorded
verdicts.  Read together they answer the arc's question inside its
declared scope, which of the security statements available in these
models are bookkeeping relative to a declared consumer and which are
properties of the substrate no declared consumer can move.

Three findings are consumer-relative in the precise sense that a
declared change moves them with the substrate held fixed.  Which of
two leakage channels is better depends on the reader's task whenever
the two are incomparable, and incomparability is not rare, since 856
of 2000 declared random pairs were certified incomparable and 268 of
them were ordered unanimously by every scalar measure in the battery
while a declared task reversed that order.  Which entropy prices a
source depends on the consumer's task, and the same declared source
costs 3.477 bits to a consumer who averages and exactly 1 bit to a
consumer who guesses once.  How a redundancy plateau approaches its
height depends on whether a designer placed it, since the engineered
plateau is a step with nothing in between while the plateau the
quantum track found rose smoothly with the strength of the records
that produced it.

Two findings are the opposite, properties no declared consumer moves.
Below the declared threshold the secret-sharing scheme gives every
reader exactly the prior, and that zero is exact rather than small, so
no choice of task, budget, or reading strategy reaches the secret.
And a garbling never helps any task, which held across every declared
control here at the level of $10^{-16}$, so wherever comparability is
certified a scalar ordering is a task ordering.  The constructive
consequence is stated with the negative one and measured by the same
runs.  The certificate of Eq.~\eqref{eq:cert} decides comparability by
proof with no threshold, and an evaluation that establishes
comparability may rely on its scalar ordering.

The run that failed is part of the result.  A declared clause demanded
that the number of bits separating two accountings increase along a
ladder of sources, the ladder was long enough to reach the far end,
and the difference turned over because both accountings vanish
together on a source that is almost certain.  The design error was in
the choice of quantity rather than in the substrate or the
instrument, and the corrected registration measures the ratio, which
is monotone, and locates the maximum of the difference, which is
interior.  A campaign that computes its verdicts from declared items
records that sequence rather than the endpoint.

These are structural results about declared finite models carrying the
campaign's exploratory label.  They are about evaluation methodology.
They do not show that any real cipher, protocol, implementation, or
deployed system is strong or weak, no experiment reported here can
bear on that question, and no attack was developed or evaluated.  A
reader who wants to carry anything from this paper into practice can
carry one thing, which is that the certificate is computable and
settles by proof whether a scalar leakage comparison in a declared
finite model is a comparison for every task.

\section{Methods and reproducibility}
\label{sec:methods}

All runs follow the campaign's standing discipline.  Substrates,
channels, tasks, ladders, batteries, seeds, tolerances, and
falsification bars were declared in the track document before the runs
they govern, per the anti-circularity contract, and verdicts are
computed from the declared items rather than written first.  One bar
was moved before its run, when the object it had been drafted against
was shown to be incapable of failing it, and the move, the reasoning,
and a prediction for the abandoned object were written into the track
document before the run so that the run could refute the prediction.
Recording a pre-run deviation is not the same as applying one
silently, and the record carries both the moved bar and the abandoned
object's measured values.

Every result-bearing run writes an append-only JSON evidence record
containing the declared parameters, the measured outcomes, the
computed verdict, and a canonical SHA-256 hash of the record body.
The records for this paper are \texttt{cr0-instrument.json},
\texttt{cr1-leakage-ordering.json},
\texttt{cr2-countermeasure-flip.json},
\texttt{cr3-task-entropy.json}, and \texttt{cr3b-task-entropy.json} in
the campaign repository, with the committed runners
\texttt{cr0\_instrument.py}, \texttt{cr1\_leakage\_ordering.py},
\texttt{cr2\_countermeasure\_flip.py},
\texttt{cr3\_task\_entropy.py}, and \texttt{cr3b\_task\_entropy.py}
alongside them.  The failed CR-3 record is retained unchanged and the
corrected declaration is a separate record, so the sequence of
verdicts is itself part of the evidence.  A reducer may summarize
records but may not rewrite them.

Every number and every figure coordinate in this paper is read from
those committed records.  The figure tables are generated
deterministically by a committed script, \texttt{make\_figdata\_cr.py},
which uses only the Python standard library and has no free
parameters, and which asserts each value typed in this paper against
the record value it came from before it writes anything, so a record
that changed would break the build rather than disagree quietly with
the text.  The comparison curve of Fig.~\ref{fig:cr0} is carried in
the CR-0 record itself and is checked against the quantum track's own
record by the same script.  The declared channel matrices, ladders,
and costs quoted in the text are checked against the track document
that declared them.

The CR-0 substrate was enumerated exactly with integer counting over
all 29791 outcomes and the CR-3 and CR-3b substrates were built from
exact rationals, so in those runs the only floating-point operations
are the logarithms and the compensated sums that turn exact counts and
exact rationals into bits.  The CR-1 and CR-2 runs compute exact
finite sums over declared joint distributions.  All five runs
executed on a single workstation in seconds and require no special
hardware.
