\title{Engineered Observation Limits in Exact Finite Models\\
What an Information-Theoretic Audit Certifies, Where It Stops,
and Where It Does Not}

\author{Andrew H. Bond}
\email{andrew.bond@sjsu.edu}
\thanks{ORCID: 0009-0003-2599-6158}
\affiliation{San Jos\'e State University, San Jos\'e, California, USA}
\date{August 7, 2026}

\begin{abstract}
Cryptography is the engineering discipline of declared observation
limits.  Where the rest of this campaign has to find the consumer's
channel inside a physical model, cryptography writes the channel down
on purpose and then argues about what a consumer can read through it.
This paper carries the campaign's consumer-relative audit to that
argument in eleven exact finite models, each a declared toy model
small enough to enumerate, and to nothing else.  Nothing
in this paper bears on computational hardness or on asymptotic
security, no real cipher, protocol, curve, standard, implementation,
device, or deployed system is modeled or evaluated, and no attack is
developed.  What is measured is
evaluation methodology, meaning the reasoning that carries a leakage
measurement to a conclusion about what a reader can do.  A threshold
secret-sharing scheme over a field of thirty-one elements, enumerated
by integer counting over all 29791 outcomes, gives an information
curve that is a step, exactly zero below the declared threshold with
the reader's posterior exactly the prior and exactly the whole secret
at and above it, with no subset of any size in between.  The
redundancy plateau the campaign found in an environment rather than
placing it takes a different value at every one of its six fragment
sizes, from 0.8118 to 1.9996 bits, so the two differ in shape and the
difference is that one of them was designed.  A declared control with
short randomness carries 2.6323
bits to each of its five single-share readers, so the zeros belong to
the scheme rather than to the measurement.
Declared leakage channels are experiments in
Blackwell's sense, and whenever two of them read the same hidden
quantity a strictly positive conditional information proves
non-garbling with no tolerance and no search.  The channel that reads
a Hamming weight carries more than twice the information of the
channel that reads one bit and is still not above it in Blackwell's
sense, the two conditional informations measuring 1.8113 and 0.7806
bits.  In two thousand declared random channel pairs, 268 were
ordered unanimously by all seven declared divergences while proved
incomparable, and every one of them carried a declared task that
strictly prefers the channel every divergence ranks as the less leaky,
with no such reversal among the 867 comparable
pairs.  The
measured statement is that a reduction in a scalar leakage measure is
not a guarantee for a reader's task outside certified comparability,
and its constructive half is that the same certificate decides
comparability by proof, so an evaluation that establishes
comparability may rely on its scalar ordering.  Two
consumers price one declared source at 3.477 bits and at exactly 1
bit, both correct, the disagreement lying entirely in the task.  One
run failed, on a declared clause requiring the difference of the two
accountings to increase along a ladder of sources.  The difference
turned over at the far end, because both accountings vanish together
as a source becomes certain, and the corrected registration measures
the ratio, which falls strictly where the difference does not.
The arc then measures where an audit of this kind stops.  A declared
generator over a toy curve was given two parameter sets, one whose
relating scalar is known by construction and one whose scalar is
fixed by a declared rule and recovered afterwards by enumeration.
That run failed two bars, because a group of ninety-six elements
cannot spread ninety-five seeds near uniformly over eight output
words, measuring 2.7890 and 2.8002 bits against three, and because the
recovery loop accepted the first candidate consistent with one
observed word instead of testing it against the next, predicting at
0.263 against a declared half.  It also asked the wrong question,
since every point of a cyclic group has such a scalar and the run
recovered the second one by enumeration.
A second registration on a larger group of prime order failed
one bar from a coding error in an inverse map, measuring 0.0314 where
the bar was one part in a billion, with its positive control already
exact.  The corrected registration passes.  A declared one-bit covert
channel carries exactly 1 bit of information about the seed bit it was
given while the unmodified generator carries exactly 0, both parameter
sets produce output words at 2.9878 and 2.9943 bits against a uniform
three, and the two routes to one conditional entropy agree exactly.  A
trapdoor scalar is a constant of the parameter set, so its entropy is
exactly zero and the information it can carry about any observable is
bounded by that zero.  That is a bound on what such an audit can see
and not advice about anything.  What such an audit sees instead is
counted next.  Over all 10100 non-singular
curves of a toy parameter space, 2275 lie in a declared rare class, a
density of 0.2252.  A procedure allowed one seed reaches one curve and
did not land in the class.  A procedure allowed 4096 seeds reaches
only 99 distinct curves, so its nominal freedom overstates the freedom
it has, and it reaches the class at its tenth seed with 526 of its
seeds landing there.  The published artifact is a curve and a seed and
carries no record of how many seeds were tried, so only a procedure's
declared freedom is auditable, which is preregistration under another
name.  The last two runs measure a place where none of this stops.  A
consumer reading four declared preparations, two of which are not
orthogonal to the other two, gains information only by disturbing what
it reads, running from 0.05 bits at an induced error of 0.025 to 0.5
bits at 0.25, agreeing with declared closed forms to sixteen decimal
places, at an exchange rate of exactly two bits of information for
each unit of induced error.  The same structure on an orthogonal
ensemble reads the whole bit at an induced error of exactly zero,
which is what every classical track in this campaign measured, most
sharply where a coarse observer's decayed mode was recovered bit for
bit.  A declared
classical record broadcast to two consumers gives each of them the
whole bit, so their informations sum to two, while a declared
three-qubit family gives them correlations whose squared strengths sum
to exactly one across the whole grid, so a consumer holding all of it
leaves the other exactly none.  Redundancy is available exactly for
information that has already become classical.
\end{abstract}

\maketitle

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

This campaign models observation as consumption.  A consumer reads a
hidden state through a declared channel, spends a declared budget on
the read, and acts on a declared task, and every claim is settled by
exact measurement in a declared finite model against bars fixed before
the run \cite{GamesPaper, Gen2Paper}.  Cryptography is the one
engineering discipline that already works this way.  Elsewhere the
campaign has to find the consumer's channel inside a physical model
and argue that the choice was not made to suit the answer.
Cryptography writes the channel down on purpose, in a specification,
before anyone measures anything.

Three of its central objects are campaign objects under other names.
Semantic security is restricted distinguishability, meaning a
statement that a reader with declared powers cannot tell two things
apart \cite{GoldwasserMicali1984}.  A side-channel adversary is a
consumer with a declared channel, which is what the campaign calls a
restricted observer \cite{Kocher1999}.  Threshold secret sharing is an
engineered redundancy plateau, built by hand where the campaign's
quantum track found one in an environment \cite{Shamir1979,
Gen2Paper}.  Shannon's information-theoretic account of secrecy is the
oldest statement in the campaign's own language, a statement about
what a reader can recover from what a reader can see
\cite{Shannon1949}.

The limit of what can be said here is stated once in the abstract,
once here, and once in the ledger of
Sec.~\ref{sec:claims}.  This paper measures exact finite models, so it
can say nothing about computational hardness, nothing about asymptotic
security, and nothing about any concrete algorithm or deployment.
Every model is a declared toy model.  No real cipher, protocol, curve,
standard, implementation, device, or deployed system is modeled or
evaluated.  No attack is developed and no attack is evaluated.  What
remains is
evaluation methodology, meaning the reasoning that carries a leakage
measurement to a conclusion about what a reader can do, and that
reasoning can be audited exactly in settings where every alternative
reading is computable rather than arguable \cite{Standaert2009}.

Three claim types run through the paper and are never conflated.
Instrument claims state that leakage informativeness, garbling
relations between declared channels, and optimal reads can be computed
exactly in finite models, and they are checked against closed forms.
Structural claims state that a named evaluation statement is
channel-relative or measurably is not, inside a declared model with
every alternative channel enumerated, and they carry the campaign's
exploratory label.  Claims about real systems are absent.  No run in
this paper is labeled as supporting one, and the limit of the previous
paragraph is the reason.  A reader looking for evidence about the
strength of a deployed system will find none here.

An anti-circularity contract governs every run.  No leakage model was
chosen after inspecting which choice favors a wanted verdict.  No
adversary task was selected after seeing the answer.  No divergence or
entropy measure was added to a battery after seeing the battery's
verdict.  Channels, tasks, budgets, priors, ladders, seeds, and bars
were written into the track document before the runs they govern.  One
bar was moved before its run, because the object it was drafted
against provably could not meet it, and the move and its reasoning
were recorded in the track document before the run rather than
afterward.  Sec.~\ref{sec:cr0} reports what that object measured.

The paper reports eleven registrations in three movements, and three
of the eleven are corrections of an earlier registration that failed
its own declared bar.

The first movement measures what an audit of this kind can certify.
It runs the instrument layer on an engineered redundancy plateau
computed by exact integer counting and validated against the known
answer for threshold secret sharing, orders declared leakage channels
by Blackwell's relation and replaces a search with a proof, measures
where a scalar leakage ordering and a task ordering disagree, and
prices one declared source for two consumers with different tasks.

The second movement measures where the same audit stops.  A trapdoor
in a declared generator is a constant of the parameter set rather than
a source of randomness, so its entropy is zero and no quantity built
from informations can see it, which is a bound rather than a
shortcoming of any instrument.  What the audit can see instead is
provenance, and provenance turns out to be a counting question about
how many results a procedure was free to choose among.

The third movement measures a place where none of that stopping
happens.  When the states a consumer reads are not distinguishable, a
consumer that learns anything changes what it read, and the price is
exact.  When two consumers want the same correlation, one of them
having all of it leaves the other with none, while a classical record
is held in full by both at once.  These are the two runs where the
campaign's own instruments have no ceiling, and they are what the
earlier movements exist to locate.

\section{An engineered redundancy plateau is a step and one left by an
interaction is not}
\label{sec:cr0}

The substrate is Shamir's threshold scheme over the prime field with
31 elements, with threshold 3 and 5 shares \cite{Shamir1979}.  A
secret uniform on the field and two uniform independent coefficients
determine a share vector at the evaluation points 1 through 5, so
every distribution in this section is a function of $31 \times 961 =
29791$ equally weighted outcomes.  Every distribution was computed by
enumerating all of them with integer counting and never by sampling.
Informations are built from integer count ratios, so a ratio that is
exactly one contributes exactly zero, and every summation over
outcomes uses compensated addition.  The declared tolerance is
$10^{-12}$ wherever a tolerance is needed at all.

The measured information curve as a function of the number of shares
read is
\begin{equation}
  \bigl(0,\; 0,\; \log_2 31,\; \log_2 31,\; \log_2 31\bigr)
  \;\text{bits},
  \label{eq:step}
\end{equation}
with $\log_2 31 = 4.954196310386875$.  All 15 subsets below the
declared threshold carry mutual information exactly $0.0$ bits rather
than merely inside the tolerance, and for every share value of
positive probability the reader's posterior on the secret is the
uniform prior with deviation exactly $0.0$ at every field element.
All 10 threshold subsets carry exactly $\log_2 31$ bits with
conditional entropy exactly $0.0$, and an independent route, Lagrange
interpolation at zero over the field, recovered the secret in all
$297910$ subset-outcome checks with zero failures.  The exactness is
structural rather than fortunate.  Below the threshold every count
ratio in the sum is the integer one, and at the threshold every
posterior is a point mass.  The step is a step in the strongest
available sense, the maximum distance from any of the 31 nonempty
subsets to the nearer of the two plateau values being exactly $0.0$,
so no subset of any size takes an intermediate value.  Mutual
information computed from the joint distribution and mutual
information computed as a difference of entropies agree across every
subset tested in all three schemes of this section to
$1.8 \times 10^{-15}$.

Figure~\ref{fig:cr0} places that curve beside the plateau the campaign
found rather than placed.  In the quantum track a system interacts
with an environment and an observer reads a fragment of the
environment, and the information a fragment carries rose
through 0.8118, 0.9571, 0.9998, 1.0426, 1.1878, and 1.9996 bits as
the fragment grew from one environment qubit to six, a different value
at every size \cite{Gen2Paper}.
The height of that plateau and the speed of the approach to it are set
by how strong the records written by the interaction are.  The engineered
plateau has no approach at all.  It is a step, and it stands exactly
where the designer put the threshold.  The comparison is between two
declared models and is not a statement about physical systems.

The declared negative control decides whether the zeros belong to the
scheme or to the measurement.  The control is a short-randomness
scheme, a degree-one polynomial whose single coefficient is uniform on
the declared five-element set rather than on the field, with shares at
the same five evaluation points.  Its declared bar required at least
one single-share reader to carry more than 0.1 bits.  Every one of its
five single-share readers carries
\begin{equation}
  \log_2\frac{31}{5} \;=\; 2.6322682154995127 \;\text{bits},
  \label{eq:l2}
\end{equation}
matching the closed form with deviation exactly $0.0$ and clearing the
declared bar by more than a factor of 26, and its two-share readers all sit at
$\log_2 31$, the same step one rung earlier.  The instrument sees
leakage when leakage exists.

The drafted control is the recorded deviation and the section's second
finding.  It was a degree-one polynomial with a field-uniform
coefficient whose five shares are published together with the
redundant value formed by adding the secret to the coefficient.  That
scheme cannot leak to any single reader, because every one of its six
published values is the secret masked by an independent field-uniform
quantity and is therefore exactly uniform and exactly independent of
the secret, and its sixth value is bit for bit the first share.  A bar
whose outcome is settled before the run tests nothing, and this one
was settled against the drafted scheme, so the bar moved to the
short-randomness scheme before the run, and the
drafted scheme was measured without a bar with its outcome predicted
in advance so that the run could refute the prediction.  It did not.
All six single-share informations came out exactly $0.0$, the sixth
published value was identical to the first share at every one of its
961 outcomes, and exactly one of the 15 pairs of published values is
uninformative, the pair of identical values, with the other 14
determining the secret.  Publishing a redundant copy of a share leaks
nothing to any single reader.  Redundancy and leakage are different
things, which the campaign's quantum track reached from the other
side when it found that redundant records are what make a state
readable by many observers at once \cite{Gen2Paper}.

\section{A conditional information certificate proves non-garbling
without a threshold}
\label{sec:cr1}

A declared key-dependent leakage model is an experiment in Blackwell's
sense, a row-stochastic matrix whose rows are the reading
distributions in each hidden state \cite{Blackwell1953}.  Blackwell's
theorem says that one experiment is at least as good as another for
every prior and every task exactly when the second is a garbling of
the first, meaning the second can be produced from the first by
post-processing that does not see the hidden state.  The question of
whether one declared side channel is better than another therefore has
a precise answer with two possible forms.  Either one garbles the
other, and then the ordering holds for every task a reader might have,
or neither garbles the other, and then no single number can order them
for every task.

The substrate is a four-bit key uniform on sixteen values, a declared
bijective substitution table, and five declared channels that read the
substituted value.  The identity channel reads the value.  The
Hamming-weight channel reads the number of set bits.  The low-bit
channel reads the lowest bit.  The noisy Hamming-weight channel adds
declared symmetric noise of probability 0.2 in each direction to the
Hamming weight.  The constant channel reads nothing.  Table~\ref{tab:cr1}
gives what each carries about the key.

The certificate is the transferable part of this run, and it replaces
a search with a proof.  Channel $B$ is a garbling of channel $A$
exactly when $B$ is conditionally independent of the key $K$ given
$A$, so
\begin{equation}
  I(K; B \mid A) \;=\; 0
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  B \text{ is a garbling of } A,
  \label{eq:cert}
\end{equation}
and a strictly positive left-hand side proves that $B$ is not a
garbling of $A$ with no tolerance and no search involved.  The
certificate needs a joint distribution of the key with both channels,
which exists here because every declared channel reads the same
substituted value.  Two channels given only as separate matrices have
no such joint, and for those the campaign's earlier tools apply
instead, a conic lower bound on the garbling residual and the
monotonicity of an $f$-divergence.  The
projected-gradient garbling search used elsewhere in the campaign was
retained here only as a cross-check.  An earlier run in the campaign
established incomparability by thresholding that search residual, the
threshold was chosen without computing the scale it would be compared
against, the run failed its own bar, and a separate registration had
to repair it with an analytic bound \cite{GamesPaper}.
Equation~\eqref{eq:cert} removes the threshold from the argument
entirely.

All five declared items passed.  The identity channel is above every
declared channel, each search residual exactly zero and each
conditional information given the identity exactly zero.
The constant channel is below every declared channel, each residual
exactly zero as well.  Adding the
declared noise to the Hamming weight is a garbling, residual
$2.2 \times 10^{-16}$ and conditional information
$4.8 \times 10^{-17}$, and the reverse direction is proved impossible
without any tolerance argument, the conditional information of key and
Hamming weight given the noisy reading measuring $1.1439$ bits, which
is exactly the difference between what the two channels carry.  The
data-processing control holds across the declared battery of 201
key-recovery tasks, 200 seeded random utilities on sixteen actions
plus the maximum-likelihood key-guessing task, with a worst task gain
under garbling of $5.6 \times 10^{-17}$.

The remaining pair is the incomparable one.  The Hamming-weight
channel carries $2.0306$ bits about the key and the low-bit channel
carries exactly $1$, so a scalar comparison ranks the first above the
second by better than a factor of two.  Yet $1.8113$ bits of the
Hamming weight survive knowing the low bit and $0.7806$ bits of the
low bit survive knowing the Hamming weight, so neither is a garbling
of the other and no scalar comparison orders them for every task.
Carrying more than twice as much information and being better for
every task are different properties, and in this declared model the
first holds while the second fails.  The next section measures how often the two
part company and what happens to a reader's task when they do.

\section{Unanimous scalar leakage orderings carry no task guarantee
outside certified comparability}
\label{sec:cr2}

A single declared secret bit, uniform, is read through channels into a
declared four-symbol alphabet, so every channel is a two by four
row-stochastic matrix.  The declared scalar battery compares the two
conditional reading distributions with seven $f$-divergences, the two
Kullback-Leibler directions, total variation, squared Hellinger, the
two chi-squared directions, and Jensen-Shannon \cite{CoverThomas2006}.
The declared task battery holds 205, two hundred seeded random
utilities on three actions and five commitment tasks in which a reader
may either decline or guess the secret bit, gaining $1$ for a correct
guess and paying a declared cost of $2$, $5$, $10$, $20$, or $50$ for
a wrong one.  A task reversal is a pair of channels in which all seven
divergences strictly order one channel above the other and some
declared task strictly prefers the other by more than the declared
$10^{-6}$.

The declared exhibit contrasts two channels that put their separation
in different places.  Channel $A$
has rows $(0.55, 0.25, 0.15, 0.05)$ and $(0.45, 0.25, 0.15, 0.15)$, so
its two reading distributions differ by $0.10$ on the most likely
symbol and by $0.10$ on the least likely one and agree exactly on the
other two.  Channel $B$ has rows $(0.49, 0.49, 0.01, 0.01)$ and
$(0.49, 0.49, 0.005, 0.015)$, so its two reading distributions agree
exactly on the two symbols carrying almost all the mass and differ by
$0.005$ on each of the two rare symbols, which are equally likely
under one value of the secret and stand in the ratio one to three
under the other.  The exhibit was declared before the run so that the
run could refute it.  All seven divergences order $A$ above $B$, the
smallest margin being $0.0149$, and a declared task prefers $B$ by
$0.00212$, so the exhibit met its bar.  The pair is proved
incomparable in both directions, the conic lower bound on any garbling
residual measuring $0.00175$ in one direction and the total-variation
difference measuring $0.095$ in the other, a garbling being unable to
raise an $f$-divergence.  Neither of those two numbers is a search
residual.

Two further items were declared with the exhibit.  The
data-processing control drew five hundred declared garbled pairs and
checked them against the full task battery.  No divergence rose under
any garble, the smallest drop in the required direction measuring
$0.00262$, and no task value rose by more than
$3.3 \times 10^{-16}$, which is Blackwell's theorem and the
data-processing inequality holding inside the instrument.
The localization item classified a declared ensemble of
2000 seeded random channel pairs by comparability and required every
task reversal to fall in the incomparable class.
Figure~\ref{fig:cr2} shows the outcome, 867 comparable, 856
incomparable, 277 ambiguous, and zero task reversals among the
comparable pairs, with 210 among the excluded ambiguous pairs.  The
prevalence measurement was declared with no bar.  Among the 856
certified-incomparable pairs, 268 are ordered unanimously by all
seven declared divergences, and every one of those 268 carries a task
reversal in the declared battery, the largest reversal margin being
$0.0796$ and the median $0.0183$.  The campaign's decision-theoretic
track measured the same structure in a different substrate and found
281 of 281 \cite{GamesPaper}.

The reading was fixed in the track document before the run and is
binding on this report.  A reduction in a scalar leakage measure is
not a guarantee for a reader's task outside certified comparability,
because in every unanimously ordered incomparable pair measured here
the ordering the scalar measures report and the ordering a task
reports disagree.  This is a statement about evaluation methodology
in declared finite models.  It is not a claim that any countermeasure,
implementation, or system is insecure, and none was modeled.  The
constructive half of the statement matters as much and is measured by
the same runs.  Comparability is common rather than rare in this
ensemble at 867 of 2000 pairs, and not one comparable pair carried a
reversal, so an evaluation that first establishes comparability may
rely on its scalar ordering for every task.  Establishing it is
cheapest when the two channels read the same hidden quantity, because
Eq.~\eqref{eq:cert} then settles the question by proof.  The channels
of this ensemble are drawn as separate matrices with no shared
reading, so they were classified by the garbling search against the
declared tolerances instead.

\section{The difference between two entropy accountings turns over and
their ratio does not}
\label{sec:cr3}

The same declared source is read by two consumers.  One averages over
many independent reads and is charged the Shannon entropy.  One makes
a single guess and is charged the min-entropy.  Both accountings are
functions of the source alone, and which of them prices the source
correctly is set by the consumer's task.  This run measures where the
two part company, and it is the run that failed a declared bar.

The substrate is two declared families of sources on an alphabet of 32
outcomes, every probability an exact rational.  Family $U$ places
$1/32 + \epsilon$ on outcome zero and spreads the rest uniformly, with
$\epsilon$ on the declared ladder $0$, $0.01$, $0.05$, $0.15$, $0.30$,

\section{A constant has zero entropy and an audit built from
informations is blind to it by that bound}
\label{sec:cr4}

Everything so far measured what an audit built from informations
certifies.  The next two sections measure where it stops, and the
first of the two stops it with a bound rather than with a failed
attempt.

The object here is an asymmetry between two readers of one output
stream who see the same words and one of whom holds a scalar the other
does not.  The substrate is a declared generator over a toy
curve, chosen small enough that every point and every state can be
enumerated.  The state advances by one point multiplication and the
output word is a declared truncation of the abscissa of a second
multiplication, keeping three bits, so there are eight output words.
Two parameter sets were declared, one whose second point is a known
multiple of the generator and one whose second point is fixed by a
declared rule and whose relating scalar is recovered afterwards by
enumeration only so that the run can state it.

The first registration failed two of its four bars, and
Table~\ref{tab:cr4} carries all three registrations together.  Its
group has 96 elements and its declared truncation has 8 output words,
so 95 seeds spread over 8 words cannot come near uniform, and the
measured output entropies of 2.7890 and 2.8002 bits missed the
declared window of 0.15 bits below a uniform 3 by finite size alone.

observed word at a rate of 0.263 against a declared half, because the
recovery loop accepted the first candidate consistent with one
observed word instead of testing candidates against the next word.
Both are errors of declaration and implementation rather than
properties of the substrate, and both are recorded.

The same run refuted its own question, which is the more useful part
of it.  The declared finding asked whether the audit can separate a
parameter set whose scalar is known from one whose scalar is merely
unknown, and it read False.  Two different second points induce
genuinely different exact output distributions, and the two recorded
word-count multisets are not the same multiset, so distributional
identity was never what the two parameter sets share.  What they share
is that the scalar of the second point was recovered by enumeration in
the same run, at the value 41.  Every point of a cyclic group is a
multiple of the generator, so the two parameter sets are not two
classes of object at all, and there is nothing for a separation test
to separate.

The corrected question replaces separation with blindness.  A trapdoor
scalar is a fixed constant of the parameter set rather than a source
of randomness, so it cannot change any conditional law of the outputs
and its whole content is the cost of a computation.  An audit built
from informations should therefore be exactly blind to it, and the
measurement is that blindness placed beside a positive control that
the same audit does detect.  The second registration moved to a
declared curve over the field of 1009 elements whose group has prime
order 1013, which makes near uniformity attainable, and its positive
control came out exact.  A declared generator that writes one seed bit
into its output word carries exactly 1 bit of mutual information with
that seed bit, and the unmodified generator carries exactly 0.  The
output entropies are 2.9878 and 2.9943 bits against a uniform 3, and
the scalar of the second point was again recovered by enumeration, at
the value 2.

That registration failed on one bar and the cause is a coding error
that the record names.  Two routes to one conditional entropy measured
2.8811 and 2.9125 bits, a difference of 0.0314 where the bar was
$10^{-9}$.  The route available to a reader holding the scalar
multiplied the observed point's abscissa as though the abscissa were
itself a scalar, where the group law requires scalar-multiplying the
observed point by the inverse of the trapdoor scalar.  The two routes
are equal by the group law, so the measured disagreement was a
measurement of the coding error and of nothing about trapdoors.

The third registration corrects the inverse map and passes all five of
its items.  The two routes now agree exactly, difference $0.0$ rather
than a small number, on the same substrate and with the same output
entropies and the same positive control.  The ceiling itself is
restated in the form that makes it a bound.  Write $T$ for the
trapdoor scalar and $X$ for any observable an audit can form from the
output stream.  Then
\begin{equation}
  I(T; X) \;\le\; H(T) \;=\; 0,
  \label{eq:ceiling}
\end{equation}
because a constant of the parameter set has exactly zero entropy, and
the record carries both quantities at exactly zero.  The blindness of
an audit built from informations is therefore not a shortcoming of
this instrument or a limit of this toy.  It is what the quantities the
audit is built from are able to express.

The same audit reads the declared covert channel at exactly 1 bit and
reads a constant at exactly 0, so a modification that changes an
output distribution is visible to it and a modification that changes
only the cost of a computation is not.  This is a statement about
what quantities of this kind can express and it is not advice about
inspecting anything.  No real curve, standard, protocol,
implementation, or deployed system is modeled or evaluated in any of
the three registrations, the toy curve was chosen only because it can
be enumerated exactly, and no attack was developed.

\section{Only a procedure's declared freedom is auditable because the
artifact carries no record of the search}
\label{sec:cr5}

Equation~\eqref{eq:ceiling} says an audit built from informations
cannot see a constant.  What such an audit can see is provenance, and
provenance turns out to be a counting question with three numbers in
it, the rarity of a declared class, the number of distinct results a
procedure can reach, and the product of the two.

The substrate is every non-singular curve over the field of 101
elements, 10100 of them, with the size of each group computed exactly
by counting points.  The declared class is the set of curves whose
group order has no prime factor above the declared bound of five.  The
protocol calls that class weak, and the label is a declaration of the
protocol rather than anything this run measures.  What the run
measures about the class is how rare it is and how many of its members
each declared procedure can reach.  It contains 2275 of the 10100
curves, a density of 0.2252.

Two declared procedures share one declared seed map, which sends a
seed to a coefficient pair and rejects the singular pairs.  The rigid
procedure may use exactly one seed.  It reaches 1 curve, its number of
reachable results times the measured density is 0.2252, and it did not
land in the declared class.  The flexible procedure may use any seed
in a declared range of 4096.  Its declared range is not its freedom.
Those 4096 seeds reach only 99 distinct curves, so the nominal freedom
of the procedure overstates the freedom it actually has by a factor of
more than forty, and the number that matters is the smaller one.  Even
so, 99 reachable results times a density of 0.2252 is 22.3, and a
product above one is the point at which choosing among results is
expected to reach the class.  It does.  The flexible procedure reaches
the declared class at its tenth seed, and 526 of its 4096 seeds land
there, an expected 7.787 seeds per success.

The published artifact carries none of that.  It is a curve and a
seed, and a seed reached on the first try and a seed reached on the
thousandth try are the same kind of object with the same appearance,
so the artifact records no search count and nothing in it distinguishes
one history from another.  What remains auditable is the freedom the
procedure declared for itself.  The
measurement is about a declared toy parameter space with a declared
toy class.  It is not a finding about any real curve, standard, or
generation procedure, none is modeled here, and nothing in it says
that any procedure or parameter set was manipulated.

The connection was recorded in the track document with the result.
Removing a designer's freedom to try again until the answer is the
wanted one is what rigid parameter generation does in cryptography and
what preregistration does in this campaign.  They are one idea in two
fields and this run computes the quantity that both of them bound.
The campaign has recorded four failures of its own against exactly
this, registrations whose declared bars could be met without the event
they were meant to detect ever occurring, and the standing rule that
came out of them requires every registration to cite a probe verifying
that the event is present before a bar can mean anything.  A bar
chosen after the answer is known and a curve chosen after its
properties are known are the same freedom being spent in two
disciplines.

\section{Reading an ensemble that is not orthogonal costs two bits of
information for each unit of induced error}
\label{sec:cr6}

The two sections above found a ceiling and a counting argument.  This
section and the next find the part of the arc where neither applies,
because the security structure in question lives in distributions
rather than in the cost of a computation, which is exactly where the
campaign's instruments have no ceiling.

Every classical track of this campaign measured the same thing about a
restricted consumer.  Its blindness costs the consumer and leaves the
substrate untouched.  The sharpest case is the hydrodynamic track,
where a coarse observer's mode decayed to a small fraction of its
initial amplitude and the exact reverse of the dynamics returned the
initial state bit for bit, so the substrate had forgotten nothing and
only the observer had \cite{Gen2Paper}.  This section measures a
declared model where that is not available, and it measures the price
exactly.

The substrate is four declared preparations of one two-level system,
two of them in one basis and two in the conjugate basis, chosen
uniformly, so no measurement distinguishes all four.  A declared
consumer intercepts each preparation with probability $\lambda$,
measures in the computational basis, and resends the state
corresponding to its outcome, and the declared ladder for $\lambda$ is
$0$, $0.1$, $0.25$, $0.5$, $0.75$, and $1$.  The receiving party
measures in the preparing party's basis, and the induced error is the
rate at which the receiving party's outcome differs from the prepared
bit on the occasions when both used the same basis.  Everything is
enumerated exactly over the finite probability space.  The declared
structure is the one Bennett and Brassard wrote down
\cite{BennettBrassard1984}, and the reason a consumer cannot copy what
it reads and pass the original along untouched is the no-cloning
theorem \cite{WoottersZurek1982}.

\section{One classical record is held in full by both consumers and
one shared correlation is held by one}
\label{sec:cr7}

The campaign's quantum track measured redundancy, meaning many
fragments of an environment carrying the same record about a system,
and it measured when redundancy appears.  On the declared ladder of
record strengths the redundancy of the record was 0 at every rung
where the interaction wrote a weak record, with the fragment
informations reading 0.0391, 0.1151, 0.2903, and 0.6122 bits, and it
became 6 only at the rung where the interaction wrote a full record
and the fragment information reached 1 bit.  A declared control state
with no preferred basis carried redundancy 0 at a system entropy of
0.9945 bits, so redundancy is a property of the declared interaction
rather than of the entanglement.  A third run measured two disjoint
consumers agreeing about the system at rates 0.5096, 0.5373, 0.6357,
0.8750, and 1.0 along the same ladder, so agreement between observers
is record strength made mechanical \cite{Gen2Paper}.  This section
measures the boundary of that plateau, meaning the information for
which redundancy is not merely absent but unavailable.

one is a declared three-qubit family carrying one excitation shared

angle on a declared seven-point grid.  Correlation between two parties
is the Wootters concurrence of the exact reduced state
\cite{Wootters1998}, and the correlation of one party against the
other two is its tangle.

Tables~\ref{tab:ledger} and \ref{tab:ledger2} register the eleven runs
with their recorded verdicts.  Read together they answer the arc's
question inside its declared scope, which of these models' security
statements are bookkeeping relative to a declared consumer and which
no declared consumer can move.

Three findings of the first movement are consumer-relative in the
precise sense that a declared change moves them with the substrate
held fixed.  Which of two leakage channels is better depends on the
reader's task whenever the two are incomparable, and incomparability
is not rare, since 856 of 2000 declared random pairs were certified
incomparable and 268 of them were ordered unanimously by every scalar
measure in the battery while a declared task reversed that order.
Which entropy prices a source depends on the consumer's task, and the
same declared source costs 3.477 bits to a consumer who averages and
exactly 1 bit to a consumer who guesses once.  How a redundancy
plateau approaches its height depends on whether a designer placed it,
since the engineered plateau is a step with nothing in between while
the plateau the quantum track found takes a different value at every
fragment size, its height and its approach set by how strong the
records are.

Two findings of the first movement are the opposite, properties no
declared consumer moves.  Below the declared threshold the
secret-sharing scheme gives every reader exactly the prior, and that
zero is exact rather than small, so no choice of task, budget, or
reading strategy reaches the secret.  And a garbling never helps any
task, which held across every declared control here at the level of
$10^{-16}$, so wherever comparability is certified a scalar ordering
is a task ordering.  The constructive consequence is stated with the
negative one and measured by the same runs.  For two channels that
read the same hidden quantity the certificate of Eq.~\eqref{eq:cert}
decides comparability by proof with no threshold, and an evaluation
that establishes comparability may rely on its scalar ordering.

The second movement locates where these instruments stop, and the stop
is a bound rather than a difficulty.  A constant of a parameter set
has zero entropy, so every quantity built from informations reads
exactly zero about it, while the same quantities read a declared
one-bit covert channel at exactly one bit.  Whatever content a
constant of that kind has is the cost of a computation, and this
campaign measures no such thing anywhere.  Where information-theoretic
quantities do keep working is provenance, and the measurement is a
count.  A procedure's declared freedom times a declared class's
measured rarity is the quantity that decides whether choosing among
results reaches the class, it stood at 0.2252 for the rigid procedure
and 22.3 for the flexible one, and the artifact each procedure
publishes records nothing about how many results were tried.  The
declared range of the flexible procedure was 4096 and its reachable
set was 99, so nominal freedom and effective freedom are different
numbers and only the second one governs.

The third movement is the part of the arc where none of that stopping
occurs, and it is where the campaign's own instruments meet a
substrate that answers back.  A consumer reading a declared ensemble
whose states are not all distinguishable obtains information only by
changing what it read, at exactly two bits per unit of induced error
on the declared family, while the same structure on a distinguishable
ensemble reads the whole bit and changes nothing.  Every classical
track of this campaign measured the opposite, most sharply where an
exactly reversible substrate returned a coarse observer's decayed mode
bit for bit.  And a classical record is held in full by two consumers
at once, informations exactly one and one, while a shared quantum
correlation on the declared family obeys an exact identity that leaves
a second consumer with nothing when the first holds all of it.  The
quantum track measured that redundancy appears exactly when a declared
interaction writes records, and this movement measures why that
plateau has a boundary.  Redundancy is available exactly for
information that has already become classical.

Three registrations failed and all three are kept.  A declared clause
demanded that the number of bits separating two accountings increase
along a ladder of sources, and the difference turned over because both
accountings vanish together on a source that is almost certain, so the
design error was in the choice of quantity rather than in the
substrate or the instrument.  A second registration asked whether an
audit can separate two parameter sets that are not two classes of
object, on a group too small for its own uniformity bar and with a
recovery loop that accepted a candidate it had not finished testing.
A third failed on a coding error in an inverse map, which the record
names, and measured that error rather than anything about its subject.
Each correction is a separate registration with its own declared
items, and the sequence of verdicts is part of the evidence rather
than a step on the way to it.

These are structural results about declared finite models carrying the
campaign's exploratory label.  They are about evaluation methodology.
They do not show that any real cipher, protocol, curve, standard,
implementation, device, or deployed system is strong or weak, no
experiment reported here can bear on that question, and no attack was
developed or evaluated.  Two objects here do not depend on the
substrates measured.  The first is the certificate, which settles by
proof whether a scalar comparison between two declared finite channels
that read the same hidden quantity is a comparison for every task.
The second is Eq.~\eqref{eq:ceiling}, which is not a measurement at
all but a bound on what the measurements of this kind can express.

\section{Methods and reproducibility}
\label{sec:methods}

All runs follow the campaign's standing discipline.  Substrates,
channels, tasks, ladders, batteries, seeds, tolerances, and
falsification bars were declared in the track document before the runs
they govern, per the anti-circularity contract, and verdicts are
computed from the declared items rather than written first.  The one
bar that moved before its run moved for the reason given in
Sec.~\ref{sec:intro}, and the record carries both the moved bar and
the abandoned object's measured values.

Every result-bearing run writes an append-only JSON evidence record
containing the declared parameters, the measured outcomes, the
computed verdict, and a canonical SHA-256 hash of the record body.
The records for this paper are \texttt{cr0-\allowbreak instrument.json},
\texttt{cr1-\allowbreak leakage-\allowbreak ordering.json},
\texttt{cr2-\allowbreak countermeasure-\allowbreak flip.json},
\texttt{cr3-\allowbreak task-\allowbreak entropy.json},
\texttt{cr3b-\allowbreak task-\allowbreak entropy.json},
\texttt{cr4-\allowbreak backdoor-\allowbreak ceiling.json},
\texttt{cr4b-\allowbreak backdoor-\allowbreak ceiling.json},
\texttt{cr4c-\allowbreak backdoor-\allowbreak ceiling.json},
\texttt{cr5-\allowbreak rigidity.json},
\texttt{cr6-\allowbreak information-\allowbreak disturbance.json}, and
\texttt{cr7-\allowbreak monogamy.json} in
the campaign repository.  Each has its committed runner beside it,
named by replacing the hyphens of the record name with underscores
and the suffix with \texttt{.py}.
The three failed records are retained unchanged and
each corrected declaration is a separate record, so the sequence of
verdicts is itself part of the evidence.  A reducer may summarize
records but may not rewrite them.

Every measured number and every figure coordinate in this paper is
read from those committed records.  The figure tables are generated
deterministically by a committed script,
\texttt{make\_\allowbreak figdata\_\allowbreak cr.py},
which uses only the Python standard library and has no free
parameters, and which asserts each value typed in this paper against
the record value it came from before it writes anything, so a record
that changed would break the build rather than disagree quietly with
the text.  The comparison curve of Fig.~\ref{fig:cr0} is carried in
the CR-0 record itself and is checked against the quantum track's own
record by the same script, and the quantum track's redundancy ladder
and agreement curve quoted in Sec.~\ref{sec:cr7} are checked against
their own records the same way.  The declared channel matrices,
ladders, grids, rules, and costs quoted in the text are checked
against the track document that declared them.

The CR-0 substrate was enumerated exactly with integer counting over
all 29791 outcomes and the CR-3 and CR-3b substrates were built from
exact rationals, so in those runs the only floating-point operations
are the logarithms and the compensated sums that turn exact counts and
exact rationals into bits.  The CR-1 and CR-2 runs compute exact
finite sums over declared joint distributions.  The CR-2 comparability
classification is made by the projected-gradient garbling search of
the campaign's decision-theoretic instrument against the declared
tolerances, and CR-1 uses that search only as a cross-check against
the certificate.  The three CR-4 runs and CR-5 do integer arithmetic
on enumerated group elements and enumerated coefficient pairs, with
every point addition and every group order computed by counting rather
than by any closed form, and the curve orders of CR-5 are obtained by
scanning the whole field for each of the 10100 curves.  CR-6 and CR-7
enumerate finite state vectors in double precision and every measured
quantity in them is checked against a declared closed form or a
declared identity, so the deviations of $1.1\times10^{-16}$ and
$1.9\times10^{-10}$ recorded in those runs are the values of those
checks.  All eleven runs executed on a
single workstation and require no special hardware.
