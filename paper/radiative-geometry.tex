\begin{abstract}
The companion paper of this campaign measured the area gate, the
requirement that the states counted for a region scale with the
region's boundary, and found one mechanism class that passes it,
deterministic local dynamics read over spacetime regions.  This paper
asks what geometry such a substrate can carry, and answers with exact
measurements.  Matter on the maximal-entropy linear substrate is
exactly invisible to every local window, and the one source class
that produces a finite distinguishability field destroys exactly one
bit per time step, with a field placed by the algebra of the dynamics
rather than by distance.  A local first law holds exactly on held-out
source patterns, with flux, temperature, and entropy defined
independently, once the flux counts the events crossing into the
spacetime region.  The Newtonian limit is rejected.  The discrete
analogue of the Poisson equation fails, no monotone flank supports a
flux reading, and the information deficits of separated sources
cancel instead of adding, so the black-hole benchmark that would
follow is forbidden by its own design bar.  Two discovery campaigns
then attacked the failed bar and closed with it intact.  A structured
vacuum removes the invisibility and yields a genuinely graded field,
but the field's curvature lives on the causal front, and escape to
infinity yields an exact static limit that is flat.  Moving to two
dimensions leaves finite-field substrates flat at every distance,
while a real-amplitude wave substrate makes superposition deviations
decay cleanly with source separation and then sends its own static
field toward zero as the transient radiates away.  On both substrate
classes that pass the area gate, every spatial gradient of
distinguishability is radiative.  The area law requires zero entropy
production, zero entropy production makes relaxation purely
radiative, and purely radiative relaxation leaves no static field for
a Poisson structure to live in.  A final experiment then measures the
one escape the chain permits.  A declared local constraint of Gauss
type, realized as lattice electromagnetism with charges entering only
through the constraint, carries an exactly static, graded, and
exactly superposing distinguishability field whose potential solves
the lattice Poisson equation.  Under a demotion ruling recorded
before the run, that result does not support entropy as the source of
geometry.  The constraint sources the geometry and distinguishability
registers it exactly, locating the missing ingredient of the failed
bar in a local conservation law rather than in entropy.  Nothing in
this paper bears on physical gravity.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Entropic accounts of gravity assume that the entropy associated with
a region of space is proportional to the area of the region's
boundary, and derive gravitational dynamics from that input together
with a local thermodynamic relation \cite{Jacobson1995, Bianconi2025,
DorauMuch2026}.  The companion paper of this campaign treated the
assumed proportionality as a gate and measured it in models where
every bit is countable \cite{AreaGatePaper}.  The verdict of that
paper was a classification.  Every declared mechanism with a positive
bulk density of entropy fails the gate before it is built, and the
one mechanism class that provably passes is deterministic local
dynamics, with the counted region read as a patch of spacetime
history rather than a snapshot of space.  Determinism concentrates
all randomness on the initial data, so the states of a spacetime
patch are bounded by causal-cone data whose size scales with the
patch perimeter, for every rule in the class and with nothing tuned.

That result opened a question rather than closing one.  A mechanism
that passes the counting requirement is only the first half of an
entropic account of geometry.  The second half needs matter to deform
the substrate, needs a local thermodynamic law to hold with its
quantities independently defined, and needs the resulting field to
obey the structural consequences of the Newtonian limit, none of them
inserted.  This paper reports the campaign's measurements of that
second half on the substrate class the gate selected, and the outcome
is exact on both sides.

The positive side is a lawful thermodynamics.  A local first law
holds exactly on every held-out source pattern, with the flux, the
temperature, and the entropy change measured by three constructions
that make no reference to one another.  The relation holds precisely
when the flux counts the events crossing into the spacetime region,
which is the ordering the thermodynamic-gravity literature assumes
rather than derives.

The negative side is the geometry.  Matter on the maximal-entropy
substrate has no local field at all, and the one source class that
leaves any trace produces a field arranged by the algebra of the
dynamics rather than by distance from the source.  The deficits of
separated sources cancel instead of adding.  Every structural test of
the Newtonian limit fails, and the failure survives two discovery
campaigns that were mounted specifically to defeat it.  The campaigns
removed two obstructions, an ensemble artifact and a topology
artifact, and then located the remaining obstruction precisely.  On
every substrate measured, the gradients of distinguishability travel.
They live on causal fronts and in transients, and whatever static
limit exists is either flat or zero.  The organizing claim of this
paper is a three-step chain stated and measured exactly in the
sections below.  The area law requires zero entropy production, zero
entropy production makes relaxation purely radiative, and purely
radiative relaxation leaves no static field for a Poisson structure
to live in.

The chain is the result for substrates without constraints, and the
final experiment measured the one escape it permits.  A local
conservation law of Gauss type, declared as a constraint rather than
discovered, holds an exactly static and graded field through the same
kind of reversible dynamics.  A ruling recorded before that run
demotes the claim such a result can earn.  The constraint sources the
geometry and the distinguishability field registers it, so the
missing ingredient of the geometric bar lies in conservation
structure, which entropy alone never supplied.

The scope of every claim is the declared model class.  The
measurements are exact computations in toy constructions whose every
ensemble, source, and instrument was declared before the runs.  No
claim is made about physical spacetime, about real gravitation, or
about the correctness of any entropic-gravity proposal.

\section{The substrate and the instruments}
\label{sec:substrate}

The companion paper's results are used here in summary form and are
not re-reported \cite{AreaGatePaper}.  Its instrument layer validated
conditional-entropy and relative-entropy measurements against exact
closed forms.  Its gate run classified constraint, gauge, and
access-restriction mechanisms as volume-law failures in the primary
count, measured an exact split between the independence count and the
correlation count of one Gauss-law ensemble, and proved two
structural statements, an entropy-density lemma forcing any passing
ensemble to zero bulk entropy density and a causal-cone theorem
giving every deterministic nearest-neighbor rule perimeter-scaling
patch counts.  The mechanism run bound those theorems to measured
rule-90 patch counts with a bulk-noise audit.  Everything below
builds on that substrate class.

The vacuum of the first three experiments is the maximal linear
substrate.  A ring of $257$ binary cells evolves for $60$ steps under
the elementary rule 90, in which each cell updates to the sum modulo
two of its two neighbors, from independent uniform initial data.
Because the rule is linear over the two-element field, the
distribution of any window of the spacetime history is uniform on the
image of a linear map, so every comparison between a matter ensemble
and the vacuum ensemble is exact subspace arithmetic.  A window
comparison lands in one of three classes.  The two supports may be
equal, so the relative entropy is zero.  The matter support may be
nested strictly inside the vacuum support, so the relative entropy is
a finite whole number of bits, the dimension deficit.  The matter
support may escape the vacuum support, so the relative entropy is
infinite.  All three classes are decided by rank computations with no
estimator and no sampling anywhere.

Matter is a rule substitution.  Cells of a declared source region
update by rule 150, which adds the cell's own value to the sum of its
neighbors, inside the rule-90 vacuum.  Sources of widths $1$, $3$,
$5$, and $7$ cells were declared.  A rule substitution carries no
potential, no distance function, and no coupling constant, and the
anti-circularity contract of the track design binds every run.

\section{EG-2: matter is exactly invisible locally}
\label{sec:eg2}

The first question is whether matter deforms the fiber measure at
all, and the measured answer on this substrate is that locally it
does not.  All numbers in this section are from the committed
evidence record of the matter run.

Every window outside the defect's causal cone carries the identical
subspace, so the causality null holds exactly for every source.
Inside the cone the invisibility is total.  Every single cell, every
two-by-two patch, and every row segment of lengths $16$, $32$, and
$64$ at every one of $25$ scanned positions classifies equal to
vacuum for every source width, $75$ window comparisons per source
with not one exception.  Matter has no local field on this substrate.
The reason is the ensemble.  Uniform initial data makes every local
marginal already maximal, so there is nothing local left to
distinguish.

Visibility arrives only globally, and how it arrives depends on the
source width in a way the algebra dictates.  Sources of widths $1$,
$5$, and $7$ remain exactly equal to vacuum on every proper segment
up to length $256$ and escape support only at the closed ring of all
$257$ cells.  The vacuum ring carries $256$ bits because rule 90
forces one global parity constraint on each row, and the defect frees
that constraint, giving the matter ring $257$ bits.  The entire
effect of these sources is one global all-or-nothing bit.

The width-$3$ source is the unique exception, and it is a sink.  It
destroys rank instead of freeing it, at the exact rate of one bit per
time step, so the global deficit after $60$ steps is exactly $60$
bits, the duration itself.  This is the only source class with a
finite distinguishability field anywhere.  The field appears at
window length $160$ and grows with window length, with deficits of
$11$, $27$, $43$, $51$, $56$, $59$, and $60$ bits at lengths $160$
through $256$, about half a bit of deficit per added cell on the
growing windows.  Its spatial arrangement is a period-$64$
self-similar pattern.  In the fixed-length position scan the finite
values repeat every $64$ cells with the profile $3$, $7$, $11$, $9$,
$5$, $1$ in the first period and $2$, $6$, $10$, $9$, $5$, $1$ in the
later ones.  The pattern is set by the automaton's algebraic
self-similarity and not by distance from the source, which is the
first sign of the geometric failure measured in
Sec.~\ref{sec:eg4}.

The reverse-engineering audit for this experiment is structural
rather than numerical.  For any potential one can define an entropy
whose gradient reproduces the corresponding force, so a source class
must be certified not to be a potential in disguise.  A positive
reweighting of a measure cannot move its support, while a rule
substitution provably does move the support of a uniform-on-subspace
measure, as the escapes above witness.  The declared source class is
therefore not potential-equivalent by construction, and the audit
required by the track design passes without a fit.

\section{EG-3: the local first law holds exactly}
\label{sec:eg3}

The first law experiment asks whether $\Delta S = T\,\Delta Q$ holds
with the three quantities defined independently, which is the
relation the entropic-gravity lineage consumes as an input
\cite{Jacobson1995, DorauMuch2026}.  On this substrate it is an
output.  All numbers in this section are from the committed evidence
record of the first-law run.

The three definitions never reference one another.  The flux
$\Delta Q$ counts declared noise injections inside the spacetime
patch, pure event bookkeeping with no entropy anywhere in its
definition.  The temperature $T$ is the patch-entropy response to a
single training injection, measured once before the held-out sweep
and frozen at exactly one bit per event.  The entropy change
$\Delta S$ is an exact rank difference between the injected and
vacuum patch ensembles.  The vacuum patch itself carries $120$ bits,
exactly the perimeter form the causal-cone bound prescribes for its
size.

\section{EG-4: the Newtonian limit is rejected}
\label{sec:eg4}

The geometric experiment takes the only field-bearing source class,
the width-$3$ sink, and tests the structural consequences of the
Newtonian limit on held-out configurations.  All numbers in this
section are from the committed evidence record of the geometry run.
An honest process note belongs with them.  A draft verdict written
before the run anticipated a different failure pattern, contradicted
the measured items, and was discarded in favor of verdicts computed
from the numbers.  The evidence record carries only the computed
verdicts.

The Poisson item fails.  The discrete Laplacian of the measured field
is as large far from the source as at it, reaching magnitude $6$ at
positions far outside the source against magnitude $3$ at the source
cells, so nothing resembling a source-localized second difference
exists.  The Gauss item fails.  The field profile is the period-$64$
algebraic pattern of Sec.~\ref{sec:eg2}, and no monotone flank exists
anywhere on it to carry a flux reading.

The superposition item fails, and the manner of failure is the
section's sharpest measurement.  Two separated width-$3$ sinks
produce a global deficit of exactly zero, at both tested separations,
against the additive expectation of $120$ bits.  Three sinks produce
a deficit of exactly one bit.  The deficits are dimensions of
subspace intersections, and they compose by the algebra of the
two-element field, not by addition.  This is not weak nonlinearity to
be tamed in a weak-field limit.  It is destructive interference of
information loss, and the linearity item fails with it.  The interior
item is unevaluable, because windows between the sources and windows
far outside both classify equal, so the field has no
interior-versus-exterior structure at the tested scale.

One law does survive, and its exactness matters for what follows.
Each isolated sink loses exactly one bit per step at either tested
position, $60$ bits in $60$ steps.  The substrate supports an exact
local law of information loss.  What it refuses to support is any
spatial organization of that loss into a potential.

\section{EG-5: the gate closes by its own bar}
\label{sec:eg5}

The black-hole benchmark of the track design may not run unless the
matter, first-law, and geometry experiments all pass, because a
horizon analogy computed on top of a failed geometry would be
theater.  The geometry experiment rejected the Newtonian limit, so
the benchmark is forbidden by construction and the planned arc
closes.  Its closing shape is the tension this paper is about.  The
counting half of the entropic program wants zero entropy production,
which is what buys the area law and the exact first law.  The
geometry half wants a graded local field, and the measurements above
show the same determinism that delivers the first half destroying
the second.  The two discovery campaigns reported next were declared,
run, and closed in the attempt to break that tension without
insertion.

\section{The second campaign: dimension, then amplitude}
\label{sec:eg4c}

The second campaign attacked the two remaining obstructions with a
declared falsifiable hypothesis per round, each recorded in the
track document before its run.

Round one tested dimensional dilution.  The hypothesis held that the
flat statics of the first campaign are a one-dimensional artifact,
since one-dimensional radiation cannot dilute, while two-dimensional
radiation thins geometrically with distance and might leave a graded
static response.  The substrate is the two-dimensional rule in which
each cell becomes the sum modulo two of its four nearest neighbors,
with the defect rule adding the cell's own value at source sites,
structured Bernoulli initial data, and the same exact Walsh
instrument with functionals computed by backward evolution.  The
hypothesis is refuted.  The two-dimensional axis profile is flat
again, $0.0611$ bits at every distance with spread $7\times10^{-6}$,
the diagonal shows only parity oscillation, and superposition
deviations sit at one half of scale at every separation.  The
refutation sharpens the diagnosis.  The obstruction is not dimension
but the finite field itself.  Influence under linear rules over the
two-element field is parity path-counting, which is zero or one and
never small, so no finite-field linear substrate can dilute with
distance in any dimension.  Geometric dilution requires amplitudes
that decay.

Round two moved to real amplitudes, with the physics motivation
declared openly in advance.  The substrate is the two-dimensional
discrete wave equation in leapfrog form, reversible, deterministic,
local, and linear over the reals, with a declared site stiffness of
$0.2$.  The vacuum is independent standard Gaussian initial data,
and matter is an impedance defect, a site of different stiffness,
still a rule substitution with no potential anywhere.  Since the
static limit of the wave equation is the Poisson equation, this is
the substrate where the Poisson item has its best chance, and saying
so before the run is what keeps the test honest.  Window marginals
are exact Gaussians whose covariances are computed by adjoint
evolution, and the covariance instrument was validated against dense
covariance evolution to $1.1\times10^{-15}$, with causality exact at
unit wave speed.  The first two instrument iterations found the
impedance signal at or below the float noise floor of the covariance
calculus, fields near $10^{-5}$ bits with hundredfold isotropy
violations diagnosed as ill-conditioning of highly correlated
neighboring cells, an instrument lesson recorded in the evidence
record.  The third iteration reads a strong-contrast scatterer,
stiffness $0.05$ in the $0.2$ vacuum, through decorrelated
stride-two windows, and the field becomes well-measured, up to
$5\times10^{-3}$ bits with isotropy within the profile spread.  The
campaign's second breakthrough is in Fig.~\ref{fig:eg4c}(a).
Superposition deviations decay cleanly with source separation,
$1.13$ to $0.46$ to $0.056$ at separations $12$, $24$, and $40$.
Distance works as the continuous weak-field knob, which no
finite-field substrate could provide.  What round two could not
reach at times $48$ and $96$ is the static regime itself, since
two-dimensional waves relax with slow algebraic tails and the
profiles remained transient-dominated.

\section{Constraint-locked structure: the escape route measured}
\label{sec:eg4d}

The closing verdict of the second campaign left one escape route, a
persistent local structure that survives its own relaxation.
Physics keeps exactly one standard mechanism of that kind, a local
constraint of Gauss type tying field flux to conserved charge on
every time slice.  A constraint-anchored field cannot relax away,
because every configuration the dynamics can reach satisfies the
same constraint.  The campaign had already met this mechanism's
information signature once, since the Gauss-law ensemble of the
companion paper carried an exact area-law mutual information across
every cut while failing the gate's primary count
\cite{AreaGatePaper}, and nothing in this section claims that
failure repaired.

A claim demotion was ruled in the track document before the run.
If a Gauss constraint is declared at the base level, the static
Poisson structure of the mean field follows from the constraint
mathematics and not from entropy, so no positive result here can
support the reading that entropy sources geometry.  The honest
claim the experiment can earn is that constraints source geometry
and distinguishability registers them exactly.  What remains
nontrivial and measurable is whether the relative-entropy field is
static under the declared dynamics, graded with a falloff measured
rather than assumed, exactly superposing with the correct charge
scaling, and Gauss and Poisson consistent on the measured field.

The declarations are these.  The substrate is two-dimensional
lattice electromagnetism on a torus of side $128$, with the
electric field on edges, the magnetic field on plaquettes, and the
standard local curl-pair dynamics, which are linear, reversible,
local, and preserve the per-vertex divergence of the electric field
exactly.  The vacuum is the identity-covariance Gaussian on the
fields conditioned on zero divergence at every vertex, and it is

\section{Every gradient is radiative}
\label{sec:synthesis}

The measurements of the two campaigns assemble into one statement.
On both constraint-free substrate classes that pass the area gate,
every spatial gradient of distinguishability is radiative, carried
by transients and causal fronts, and nothing graded survives the
static limit.  The finite-field substrate converges to a flat
static field.  The wave substrate's static field vanishes.  The
chain behind this is three exact steps.  The area law requires zero
entropy production, which is the entropy-density lemma of the
companion paper read as a constraint on dynamics.  Zero entropy
production makes relaxation purely radiative, because a reversible
system can shed a transient only by transporting it away, never by
absorbing it.  And purely radiative relaxation leaves no static
field for the Poisson structure to live in, as both campaigns
measured.  The chain is the campaign's result for constraint-free
substrates.

The contrast with ordinary statics locates the tension in familiar
territory.  A dissipative system relaxes to a graded static profile
by pushing entropy into a bath, in the manner the quantum Brownian
motion literature makes precise \cite{CaldeiraLeggett1983}, and it
is exactly that entropy production which the area law forbids.  The
transients that carry the gradients here play the role the
decoherence literature assigns to the environment as a record
carrier \cite{Zurek2003}.  The information about the source is not
destroyed but exported to infinity, and once exported it grades
nothing locally.  Within this model class the two halves of the
entropic-gravity program pull in opposite directions.  The counting
half wants zero entropy production, and the geometry half wants a
graded static field that zero entropy production forbids.

The open problem this chain appeared to leave behind, a persistent
local structure that survives its own relaxation without the answer
being inserted, is answered by the constraint experiment of
Sec.~\ref{sec:eg4d} under its demotion ruling.  The missing
ingredient of the geometric bar is located, a local conservation
law tying matter to boundary flux, and entropy alone never supplied
it.  On constraint-free substrates every gradient radiates away,
while the one measured mechanism that holds a gradient still is a
constraint that sources the geometry itself, with the
relative-entropy field registering it exactly.  Any future claim
that entropy alone produces Poisson structure on an area-law
substrate now has a measured counterexample program to answer.

anti-circularity contract.  The forbidden-input list excludes any
inserted potential, any distance function, any entropy-area
proportionality, and any temperature defined through the law it
verifies, and every ensemble, source class, probe, and bar was
declared in the track document before the corresponding run.  Both
discovery campaigns and each round of the second campaign carry
dated declarations recorded before any run, including the declared
falsification conditions of their hypotheses, and the constraint
experiment's claim demotion was likewise recorded before its run.
Verdicts are computed from the measurements after the runs.  The geometry run enforces
this discipline's value in one recorded lesson, a draft verdict
written before the data contradicted the measured items and was
replaced by the computed verdicts the evidence record now carries.

Every result-bearing run writes an append-only JSON evidence record
containing the complete declared parameter set, outcome arrays, and
a canonical hash of the record body.  The records for this paper are
\texttt{eg2-matter.json}, \texttt{eg3-first-law.json},

\texttt{eg4c-mini.json}, \texttt{eg4c2-wave.json},

campaign repository, eight records all carrying the exploratory

finite-field instruments are exact rank and Walsh computations with

wave-substrate instrument is exact Gaussian covariance calculus
validated against dense covariance evolution, and the

the analysis chain.  Every number and every figure coordinate in

records but may not rewrite them.
