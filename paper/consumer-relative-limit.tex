\title{Consumer-Relative Distinguishability in the Thermodynamic Limit}

\author{Andrew H. Bond}
\email{andrew.bond@sjsu.edu}
\thanks{ORCID 0009-0003-2599-6158}
\affiliation{San Jos\'e State University, San Jos\'e, California, USA}
\date{August 5, 2026}

\begin{abstract}
The quantum results of this campaign were measured in finite-dimensional
models, where every algebra is a full matrix algebra, every state has a
density matrix, and every consumer has a partial trace.  The local algebras
of quantum field theory are not such objects.  They are type III factors,
which admit no trace, no density matrices, no partial traces, and no
entanglement entropy of a region.  This paper reports a measured bridge
between the two settings.  The consumer-relative quantities that carry
every sealed claim of the campaign have well-defined thermodynamic limits,
while the absolute quantities that do not survive the passage were never
load-bearing.  Four results support this.  The type-III-native
computational routes, through the relative modular operator and through
states treated as expectation functionals, reproduce the sealed finite
instrument at machine precision, five decades inside the declared agreement
bar.  A ladder of growing spin chains shows every consumer-relative
restricted relative entropy converging exponentially, while a deliberately
included absolute quantity, the entanglement entropy of the cut, diverges
exactly extensively.  The task-versus-fidelity flip of the earlier quantum
paper persists at every rung of the ladder at fixed absolute budget, with
its held-out task gap constant to nine decimals.  The consumer-family
requirement of the quantum track, reformulated through a quadratic form
built from the reference state's modular data, holds with a single
constant across every window and coupling, and that constancy is proved
exactly as a theorem about rotations generated by involutions.  A
corollary is that the
sealed quantum results of the campaign, computed at a single finite size,
were already converged to their quoted precision and are effectively
infinite-volume statements.  Every number here is measured in finite
models standing in for split inclusions, no claim is made that any
extrapolated limit equals a field-theory value, and the consumer does not
create anything physical by choosing what to observe.
\end{abstract}

\maketitle

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

An earlier paper of this campaign defined consumer-relative
distinguishability, the divergence between two quantum states as seen
through the declared channel, task, and budget of a particular agent, and
measured its behavior in small spin-chain models where every quantity is
computable.  Its three principal findings were a consumer hierarchy that
divides one distinguishability total differently for different
excitations, a strict task-versus-fidelity trade at scarce storage budgets
called the flip, and a consumer-family consistency requirement that does
constraining work exactly when the consumers share an event.  Every one of
those measurements lived inside finite-dimensional type I factors.  Every
algebra was a full matrix algebra, every state had a density matrix, and
every consumer channel was built from a partial trace.

The observable algebra of a bounded region in a real quantum field theory
is not that object.  Local algebras are type III von Neumann factors, with
the most singular subtype identified for physically reasonable theories
\cite{Fredenhagen1985, Witten2018}.  A type III factor admits no trace, so
it has no density matrices, no partial trace, and no von Neumann entropy
of a restricted state.  The mathematical objects the earlier paper
computed with do not exist there.  What does exist is relative entropy,
defined for states of an arbitrary von Neumann algebra through the
relative modular operator \cite{Araki1976}, together with the
data-processing inequality in full generality \cite{Uhlmann1977} and the
characterization of its equality case \cite{Petz1986}.  Field-theory
computations confirm that relative entropies of physical states are free
of the ultraviolet divergences that destroy the absolute quantities
\cite{Lashkari2014}.

This paper asks which of the campaign's quantum objects survive the
passage from finite models to that setting, and it answers by measurement
rather than by declaration.  The candidate thesis, fixed before any run,
was that relative quantities, read consumer-relatively, survive while
absolute quantities do not.  Restricted relative entropies, their differences and
orderings, task distortions, and the flip are built from expectation
values and relative modular data, so each should have a well-defined
limit as the model grows.  The reduced density matrix, the entanglement
entropy of a cut, and dephasing in a preferred basis are type I artifacts
with no counterpart, so they should diverge or fail to be definable, and
the campaign's sealed claims should turn out never to have depended on
them.  Either half of this thesis could have failed per quantity.

Four instruments carry the answer.  The first computes the campaign's
sealed divergences by two independent type-III-native routes, one through
the relative modular operator and one through states treated purely as
expectation functionals on the consumer algebra, with no reduced density
matrix formed anywhere in the code path, and requires exact agreement
with the sealed finite instrument.  The second runs a ladder of growing
chains at fixed physics and measures which quantities converge and which
diverge, with the entanglement entropy of the cut carried deliberately as
the diverging control.  The third reruns the flip protocol at every rung
of the ladder under a budget accounting chosen, before the run, to be the
one that can possess a limit at all.  The fourth reformulates the
consumer-family quantifier through a quadratic form built from the
reference state's modular data, measures the reformulated law on the
sealed grid, and proves the constancy it finds.

Three claim types are kept separate throughout, exactly as in the earlier
papers.  Proved statements are the literature's theorems, cited and never
retested here.  Measured-in-model statements are facts about the declared
finite models and their internal limits, and every number in this paper
is of that kind at exploratory evidence label.  Design statements
describe instruments not yet run.  Renaming known
operator-algebra facts adds nothing.  The existence of Araki relative
entropy, the generality of data processing, and the type classification
of local algebras are known mathematics, and calling them a bridge would
be a relabeling.  The contribution of this paper, if any, is the measured
bridge, meaning the quantitative demonstration that the campaign's
specific sealed numbers are computed by the surviving objects and are
already converged in the growing-model limit.  The causal caution of the
campaign applies verbatim.  The consumer does not create anything
physical by choosing what to observe, and the only admissible causal
direction runs from the physical state through the accessible channel to
the consumer-relative divergence.

\section{The modular dictionary and the TB-0 instrument}
\label{sec:modular}

\subsection{The surviving formula}

For a faithful state $\rho$ on a finite-dimensional algebra with
eigendecomposition $\rho=\sum_i p_i\,|e_i\rangle\langle e_i|$, the vector
$\Omega_\rho=\sum_i \sqrt{p_i}\;e_i\otimes e_i^{*}$ on the doubled space
represents $\rho$, and under the identification of the doubled space with
Hilbert-Schmidt operators $\Omega_\rho$ is the matrix $\sqrt{\rho}$.  The
relative modular operator of the pair $(\rho,\sigma)$ acts on
Hilbert-Schmidt operators as
\begin{equation}
\Delta_{\sigma|\rho}(X) \;=\; \sigma X \rho^{-1},
\label{eq:relmod}
\end{equation}
with spectrum the set of eigenvalue ratios $s_i/p_j$.  Araki's formula
\cite{Araki1976} is
\begin{equation}
S(\rho\,\|\,\sigma) \;=\;
-\bigl(\Omega_\rho,\;\ln\Delta_{\sigma|\rho}\,\Omega_\rho\bigr),
\label{eq:araki}
\end{equation}
and in type I this equals the Umegaki trace formula identically, which a
two-line computation in the joint eigenbasis confirms.
Equation~\eqref{eq:araki} contains no trace and no density matrix in its
general form, only two states of a von Neumann algebra and the modular
data they induce, which is why it is the formula that still parses when
the algebra is type III.

One hazard is recorded because it bites in practice.  References disagree
on the index order of the relative modular operator, and some write the
operator of Eq.~\eqref{eq:relmod} with the roles of the two states
exchanged.  Relative entropy is asymmetric, so a convention slip silently
computes the divergence in the reversed order.  The instrument pins its
convention with an asymmetric commuting control pair whose divergences in
the two orders differ in closed form, so a slipped convention fails the
control rather than passing unnoticed.

A second route removes the density matrix entirely.  For two states given
only as expectation functionals on a finite-dimensional consumer algebra,
the GNS construction from the reference functional yields a Hilbert
space, a cyclic vector, and a relative modular operator built from the
two functionals alone, and Eq.~\eqref{eq:araki} then yields the
restricted relative entropy without any reduced density matrix being
formed at any point in the code path.  In type I this must equal the
restricted Umegaki value computed through the partial trace.  The
relative modular operator is the object that
survives the passage, and density matrices are its type I shadow, useful
for computation and meaningless as a foundation.

\subsection{TB-0 results}

The TB-0 instrument computes the campaign's sealed model, an eight-qubit
transverse-field Ising chain with thermal reference, by three routes.
Route one is the sealed spectral Umegaki instrument, untouched.  Route
two is Eq.~\eqref{eq:araki} evaluated spectrally through the ratio set
and the overlap matrix.  Route three is the states-as-functionals GNS
route on each consumer algebra, which also computes Uhlmann fidelity in
GNS form against the trace-norm formula.  The declared falsification bar
was route disagreement above $10^{-10}$ on any state pair or region.

The measured worst disagreement over the entire grid is
$9.4\times 10^{-16}$, five decades inside the declared bar.  The agreement
holds across the closed-form checks, which cover the commuting
Kullback-Leibler case at $5.6\times 10^{-17}$, a dephased pair at
$9.4\times 10^{-16}$, the global pair at $1.1\times 10^{-16}$, and a
near-degenerate pair with eigenvalue splittings swept downward, stable at
$5.6\times 10^{-17}$.  It holds across the eight-qubit consumer sweep,
where the single-site, pair, and half-chain consumers show functional
route agreement with the partial-trace route at or below
$2.2\times 10^{-16}$.  This result is deliberately a null test of
mathematics, exactly as the sealed data-processing margins of the earlier
paper were.  Its job is to certify that the implementation computes the
objects the theorems are about, because every later section leans on the
modular route.  Machine-precision agreement in
type I does not test any type III statement, it certifies the instrument
that the ladder then uses.

\section{The split property as the physical referent}
\label{sec:split}

Finite models cannot be honest stand-ins for local algebras, and the
split property says what they can be honest stand-ins for.  In a field
theory satisfying the nuclearity condition on the energy-level density
\cite{BuchholzWichmann1986}, for a double cone and a slightly larger
concentric double cone there is an interpolating type I factor between
the two local algebras,
\begin{equation}
\Ac(O) \;\subset\; \Nc \;\subset\; \Ac(\widehat{O}),
\label{eq:split}
\end{equation}
a standard split inclusion in the sense of Doplicher and Longo
\cite{DoplicherLongo1984}.  The interpolating factor is where density
matrices, partial traces, and all of the campaign's finite machinery
legitimately live.  The local algebras on either side of it are type
III$_{1}$ \cite{Fredenhagen1985} and have none of that machinery.

The campaign adopts this as a standing rule.  Every finite-size claim is
a statement about a split-inclusion approximation, never about a local
algebra.  The collar, the gap between the region and its enlargement, is
the resolution parameter of the approximation.  On a chain the collar is
concrete, since the consumer keeps a region and the enlarged region adds
buffer sites on each side, and the collar width in sites plays the role
the resolution parameter played in the campaign's classical
projection-entropy track.  The type III divergences are expected to
reappear as the collar shrinks for absolute quantities and not for
relative ones, which is the thesis of Sec.~\ref{sec:intro} restated in
collar language.  The ladder of the next section grows the system at
fixed cut fraction, and every one of its witnesses is a witness about a
family of such approximations.

\section{The scaling ladder}
\label{sec:ladder}

\subsection{Design}

The ladder runs chains of length $N=4$ through $12$ at fixed physics,
coupling $J=1$, transverse field $h=2$ in the gapped paramagnetic regime,
inverse temperature $\beta=0.5$, and a diagonal excitation of strength
$\theta=0.8$ at the site adjacent to the half-chain cut.  The consumers
are the single boundary site, the boundary pair, and the kept half chain,
the last being the consumer whose algebra grows with $N$.  Two lists were
declared before the run.  The candidate-convergent list holds the
consumer-relative restricted relative entropies of excitation against
reference.  The candidate-divergent list holds the von Neumann entropy of
the thermal reference reduced to the half chain, included deliberately as
the diverging control.  If the control had failed to diverge, the ladder
would have had no contrast and would have supported no convergence claim
for anything.

\subsection{Results}

Every consumer-relative restricted relative entropy converges
exponentially.  Successive differences shrink roughly fifty-fold per
rung.  The site consumer moves from $1.2\times 10^{-3}$ at the first
rung gap to $8.0\times 10^{-9}$ at the last, the pair consumer from
$9.5\times 10^{-4}$ to $5.7\times 10^{-9}$, and the half-chain consumer,
whose algebra grows with the system, from $5.2\times 10^{-4}$ to
$3.4\times 10^{-9}$, reaching nine-digit stability by $N=10$.  The
effective limits are
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
D_{\text{site}} &= 0.631800, &\quad D_{\text{pair}} &= 0.674747,\\
D_{\text{half}} &= 0.675183, &\quad D_{\text{global}} &= 0.723532,
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:limits}
\end{equation}
in nats, with the global divergence computed through $N=10$.  The Araki
route of Sec.~\ref{sec:modular} agreed with the sealed Umegaki instrument
at every rung and consumer within the $10^{-10}$ bar, and the
states-as-functionals route agreed through $N=8$, the largest size at
which it was run.

The diverging control diverged exactly as predicted.  The thermal
half-chain von Neumann entropy takes the values $0.746$, $1.097$,
$1.448$, $1.799$, and $2.151$ nats across the ladder, exactly extensive
at $0.1756$ nats per site, $0.3512$ nats per added site pair.  Figure~\ref{fig:ladder} shows the
contrast pair, the plateaus of the relative quantities against the linear
growth of the absolute one.  One quantity, one verdict.  The relative
quantities have limits and the absolute entropy does not, which is the
first quantitative confirmation of the thesis of Sec.~\ref{sec:intro}.

A corollary retroactively strengthens the
campaign.  The sealed quantum results were computed at $N=8$, and
Eq.~\eqref{eq:limits} shows the $N=8$ values already agree with the
extrapolated limits to the precision at which they were quoted.  The
campaign's sealed quantum numbers are therefore effectively
infinite-volume statements.  This holds in the
measured regime of a fixed physical excitation on a gapped reference at
fixed cut fraction, the result carries the exploratory label, and the
open questions about scaling regime and uniformity in
Sec.~\ref{sec:nonclaims} are untouched by it.

\section{Flip persistence at fixed budget}
\label{sec:flip}

\subsection{The budget decision, made before the run}

The earlier paper's flip was measured at budgets counted in stored
qubits, and a ladder forces a choice between two accountings, a fixed
absolute number of stored qubits and a fixed fraction of the kept region.
The choice was made before the run and recorded as binding, with fixed
absolute budget primary, for two reasons.  The task is local and its
relevant halo converges by the ladder result of Sec.~\ref{sec:ladder}, so
scarcity relative to the task is a notion independent of system size.
And a fractional budget has no type III meaning at all, because algebra
dimension is the quintessential type I artifact, so only the fixed-count
object can possess a limit.  The fraction was retained as a secondary
diagnostic.

\subsection{Results}

The protocol reruns the sealed flip design, with its training and
held-out scenario grids, its declared win margins, and its mandatory
anti-arm, on the kept halves of chains $N=6$ through $12$.  At budget
$k=1$ the flip holds at every rung, with the task arm winning the task,
losing on fidelity, and the anti-arm worst on the task everywhere, which
keeps every rung interpretable under the sealed rule.  The held-out task
gap between the arms is $0.10762$ at all four rungs, constant to
$5.3\times 10^{-10}$ by $N=12$.  The task functional, the task arm's
encoder, and the fidelity arm's miss are all local objects, so the task
gap is size-independent almost immediately.  The infidelity gap converges
from $0.2994$ to $0.2946$ with successive differences shrinking through
$3.5\times 10^{-3}$, $1.2\times 10^{-3}$, and $1.1\times 10^{-4}$.
Figure~\ref{fig:flip} shows both gaps.  At fixed absolute budget the flip
is a thermodynamic-limit phenomenon, which is what the budget decision
predicted and what a type III phrasing requires.

The secondary diagnostic did not trivialize, and the finding is reported
adjacent to the result it qualifies.  The flip region in budget count ran
$\{1\}$, $\{1,2\}$, $\{1\}$, and $\{1,3\}$ across the four rungs, with
collapse fractions $0.33$, $0.5$, $0.2$, and $0.5$, neither shrinking nor
stabilizing.  The cause is visible in the search records.  The
fidelity-optimal subset at intermediate budgets sometimes contains the
task site and sometimes does not, depending on which subsets best capture
the reference correlations at each region size.  The upper edge of the
flip region is therefore a discrete subset-selection artifact at small
region sizes, the clean convergent object is the fixed-count flip, and
the fractional diagnostic is untestable below substantially larger
regions.

\section{The wedge-family reformulation and an exactness theorem}
\label{sec:wedge}

The consumer-family quantifier of the campaign's quantum track
demanded, for a family of nested windows witnessing one shared
excitation, that a single constant relate each member's relative
entropy to a declared flux weight.  Under the lattice weight
$\theta^2$ that requirement tightened toward exclusion on the
measured grid, with joint survival falling from $1.0$ to $0.75$ to
$0.25$ as the family grew.  The type III reformulation replaces the
lattice weight by a quadratic form built from the reference state's
modular data, and its circularity audit requires that the form be
computable without evaluating the entropy it normalizes.

The declared form is the Kubo--Mori quadratic form.  For reference
state $\rho$, window $W$, and excitation $U(\theta) = e^{i\theta P}$
with $P$ the shared-site Pauli generator, the first-order
perturbation is $\delta\rho = i[P,\rho]$, its reduction to $W$ is
$\delta\rho_W$, and
\begin{equation}
Q_W \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2} \sum_{ij}
  \left|\langle i|\,\delta\rho_W\,|j\rangle\right|^2 k(p_i,p_j),
\qquad
k(p,q) = \frac{\ln p - \ln q}{p - q},
\label{eq:kubomori}
\end{equation}
with $p_i$ the spectrum of $\rho_W$ and $k(p,p) = 1/p$.  This is the
exact second-order coefficient of the Araki entropy, assembled from
the reduced reference and the reduced perturbation only, and the
code path that assembles it evaluates no entropy anywhere, so the
audit passes structurally.  A validation probe at $\theta = 0.02$
finds the measured entropy within $1.4\times 10^{-4}$ of
$\theta^2 Q_W$ on every window at every coupling point, the
sin-squared mismatch the theorem later identifies.

Measured on the same grid, windows, and excitations as the sealed
quantum-track declarations, the reformulated law
$S_W(\theta) = \lambda_W\,\theta^2 Q_W$ holds with joint survival
$1.0$ at every family size, and the universality is far stronger
than the declared spread bar.  The fitted $\lambda$ equals
$0.9885453204$ constant to thirteen digits across all eighty
window-and-coupling combinations, against a lattice-weight spread of
$0.078$ mean and $0.131$ maximum.  The modular form itself saturates
along the nested family exactly as the entropies do, the activation
dichotomy follows from localization since a probe disjoint from the
window gives exactly zero for both the entropy and the form, and the
route cross-check of Sec.~\ref{sec:modular} agrees to
$1.9\times 10^{-15}$ on the declared member.

The thirteen-digit constancy is a theorem, not a measured
coincidence.

\begin{theorem}[Pauli-rotation exactness]
Let $\rho$ be a full-rank density matrix, $P$ a Hermitian involution,
and $U(\theta) = e^{i\theta P}$.  Then for every $\theta$,
\begin{equation}
S\!\left(U\rho\,U^{\dagger} \,\middle\|\, \rho\right)
 \;=\; \sin^2\!\theta \; S\!\left(P\rho P \,\middle\|\, \rho\right).
\label{eq:exactness}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Since $P^2 = I$, $U = \cos\theta\, I + i \sin\theta\, P$, so
$U\rho U^{\dagger} = \cos^2\!\theta\,\rho + \sin^2\!\theta\, P\rho P
+ i \sin\theta\cos\theta\,[P,\rho]$.  Von Neumann entropy is
unitarily invariant, so
$S(U\rho U^{\dagger}\|\rho) =
 \operatorname{tr}[(\rho - U\rho U^{\dagger})\ln\rho]$.  The odd
term vanishes identically because $\rho$ commutes with its own
logarithm, $\operatorname{tr}([P,\rho]\ln\rho)
= \operatorname{tr}(P[\rho,\ln\rho]) = 0$ by cyclicity.  With
$1 - \cos^2\!\theta = \sin^2\!\theta$ the remainder is
$\sin^2\!\theta\,\operatorname{tr}[(\rho - P\rho P)\ln\rho]$, whose
coefficient equals $S(P\rho P\|\rho)$ because $P\rho P$ has the
entropy of $\rho$.  No symmetry of $\rho$ is used anywhere.
\end{proof}

Three corollaries close the loop.  For any window containing the
excitation site the reduction commutes with the rotation, so
$S_W(\theta) = \kappa_W \sin^2\!\theta$ exactly with
$\kappa_W = S(P\rho_W P\|\rho_W)$.  Matching Taylor coefficients at
$\theta = 0$ gives $Q_W = \kappa_W$, so the declared flux weight of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:kubomori} is the involution defect itself.  And the
fitted $\lambda$ is the pure kinematic constant
$\sum_k \theta_k^2 \sin^2\!\theta_k / \sum_k \theta_k^4
= 0.9885453204$ of the declared grid, which the eighty measured
values match to $1.3\times 10^{-13}$ with no refit, while
retroactively the quantum track's $\theta^2$-law residuals are
identified as the universal mismatch between $\sin^2\!\theta$ and
$\theta^2$, identical for every consumer, which the sealed record
indeed shows.  The theorem was verified on one hundred twenty random
full-rank states and random involutions at angles up to $2.0$
including $\pi/2$ with worst relative error $1.4\times 10^{-14}$,
and the algebraic core of the proof, the three-harmonic expansion,
the vanishing of the odd trace under the commutation hypothesis, and
the assembled trace identity, is machine-checked in Lean~4 with
Mathlib in the campaign repository, where the trace identity is
hypothesis-minimal, holding for arbitrary square matrices with
$\rho L = L\rho$.  The formalization is complete end to end.  The
matrix logarithm is instantiated through Mathlib's continuous
functional calculus for Hermitian matrices, its commutation and
unitary-conjugation properties are discharged from the library, and
the final theorem assumes only that $\rho$ is positive definite and
that $P$ is a Hermitian involution, with no interface hypotheses
remaining.

\section{What the bridge does not do}
\label{sec:nonclaims}

The bridge measured here is deliberately narrow.

The chain has no causal structure.  There are no light cones, no Killing
flow, and no horizon generators, and the collar of Sec.~\ref{sec:split}
is a distance in sites, not a causal separation.  No entropy-area
statement appears anywhere in this paper, and the diverging control of
Sec.~\ref{sec:ladder} must not be read as one.  No claim is made that any
extrapolated limit in Eq.~\eqref{eq:limits} equals a value in any quantum
field theory.  Convergence of a ladder of finite models is not existence
of a continuum limit, and the finite models stand in for split-inclusion
interpolators, never for local algebras.

One limitation was promoted to the standing open question of the
track and has since been measured.  The ladder grows the system at
fixed cut fraction, which probes an infrared limit, while the type
III pathologies of the continuum are ultraviolet, arising as the
collar of a split inclusion shrinks at fixed physical size.  The
declared candidate instrument, a collar-width sweep at fixed
physics, was run after this paper's ladder, its couplings and
excitation strength held fixed across the sweep though they differ
from the ladder's.  A fixed four-site
consumer is probed by an excitation approaching the cut from inside
at collar distance $w$, an excitation strictly outside the consumer
being exactly invisible at any distance, inherited from the sealed
instrument's no-signalling null.  At every collar
including zero the divergence converges in $N$ to ten digits by
$N = 10$, and the profile from the cut inward is $3.753\times
10^{-2}$, $3.931\times 10^{-2}$, $3.933\times 10^{-2}$,
$4.061\times 10^{-2}$ nats, finite at the cut-adjacent site with a
shallow boundary layer growing inward.  The consumer-relative layer
is therefore finite, convergent, and well behaved exactly where the
continuum absolute layer diverges, which locates the ultraviolet
pathology in the absolute layer, consistent with this paper's
thesis.  The true ultraviolet limit remains a continuum question the
lattice cannot decide, and the bridge's honest name is now an
infrared bridge with a measured lattice boundary layer.

The consumer-family quantifier, which an earlier draft of this paper
could only leave at the design stage, is now measured and proved in
Sec.~\ref{sec:wedge}.  Its wedge language remains declared analogy
throughout, the chain still has no causal structure, and the
remaining distance to quantum field theory is unchanged by the
reformulation's success.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper measured a bridge between the campaign's finite quantum models
and the operator-algebraic setting in which quantum field theory actually
lives.  The type-III-native routes, through the relative modular operator
and through states as expectation functionals with no partial-trace
primitive anywhere in the code path, reproduce the sealed instrument at
$9.4\times 10^{-16}$, five decades inside the declared bar.  The ladder
shows every consumer-relative restricted relative entropy converging
exponentially to the limits of Eq.~\eqref{eq:limits} while the
entanglement entropy of the cut, the quantity outsiders most expect the
program to depend on, diverges exactly extensively as the deliberate
control.  The flip persists at every rung at fixed absolute budget with a
task gap constant to nine decimals, under a budget accounting chosen
before the run because it is the only one with a type III meaning.  The
corollary stands that the campaign's sealed quantum results were already
infinite-volume statements to their quoted precision.

The relabeling clause applies to everything else.  The theorems cited
here belong to Araki, Uhlmann, Petz, Fredenhagen, Doplicher and Longo,
and Buchholz and Wichmann, and restating them adds nothing.  Whatever in
this paper merely renames known operator-algebra facts is not a
contribution.  The contribution, if any, is the measured bridge, the
demonstration that the campaign's specific sealed numbers are carried by
the objects that survive the passage and are already converged in the
growing-model limit, together with the honest boundary of
Sec.~\ref{sec:nonclaims}.  Every result carries the exploratory label.
Nothing in this paper claims anything about physical gravity, real
horizons, or the actual values of any quantum field theory.  The consumer
does not create anything physical by choosing what to observe.
