\title{Five Audits Under One Consumer Discipline:\\
Renormalization, Quantum Darwinism, Lattice Hydrodynamics,
Fluctuation Theorems, and Gauge Redundancy in Exact Finite Models}

\author{Andrew H. Bond}
\email{andrew.bond@sjsu.edu}
\thanks{ORCID: 0009-0003-2599-6158}
\affiliation{San Jos\'e State University, San Jos\'e, California, USA}

\begin{abstract}
This campaign models observation as consumption.  A consumer reads a
hidden substrate through a declared channel, spends a declared
budget, and acts on a declared task, and every claim is settled by
exact measurement in a declared finite model against bars fixed
before the run.  This paper applies that one discipline to five
bodies of physics and reports twenty-seven experiment runs.  In
renormalization, whether a coarse model represents the substrate is
set by the declared blocking rule, one rule keeps the coarse family
exact while another mispredicts a declared correlator by 260 times
its bar on the same substrate, the correlation length an observer
reads is the one its channel's symmetry sector selects, and a
declared perturbation grows under one blocking rule while shrinking
under another.  In quantum Darwinism, redundant records exist
exactly where the declared interaction wrote them, a maximally
scrambled state carries none, the order of interactions alone can
hand one observer a record, equal-size fragments of one state differ
in information by a factor of 36, and the rate at which two optimal
observers agree runs from 0.510 to 1.000 as declared record strength
grows, every disagreement computed from the declared channels.  In
lattice hydrodynamics, a reversible gas shows an entropy rise of
0.637 bits that retraces exactly under reversal, a shear mode
freezes because of conservation laws the substrate actually has,
the corrected substrate yields a viscosity of 0.842 that nine
declared measurement prescriptions reproduce within 0.07 percent,
and the decayed mode is recovered bit for bit by exact reversal
while a single flipped bit reduces the recovered amplitude to 0.018
of its value.  In fluctuation theorems, the Jarzynski and Crooks
identities hold to machine precision path by path, restricted
observers break them by exactly the amounts their declared detector
models predict, correct inference through the true detector model
restores them to machine precision while a misdeclared model leaves
a larger defect than no correction at all, and the feedback identity
holds with the acting controller's information and fails with a
bystander's.  In gauge theory, three routes to a Wilson loop agree
to every printed digit, every gauge-fixed observer's link reading
equals a gauge-invariant loop selected by its declared fixing,
breaking the constraint is first-order visible in the fiber sector
while the invariant sector responds at fourth order with measured
slope 4.000003, and a coupled matter field has no bare identity
apart from the observer's fiber coordinates.  Six of the
twenty-seven runs failed their declared bars.  Each failure is
recorded with its design error named, and each correction was run
as a separate registration.  Nothing in this paper is a claim about
any laboratory system.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

This campaign models observation as consumption.  A consumer reads a
hidden substrate through a declared channel, spends a declared
budget on the read, and acts on a declared task.  Earlier stages
measured what such consumers can distinguish in quantum models
\cite{QuantumPaper}, what entropy they observe in reversible
classical models
\cite{SourcesPaper}, how declared prescriptions shape readings of
rewriting systems \cite{WMPaper}, how declared constraints source
geometry \cite{RadiativePaper}, and how the same structure plays
out in games and decisions \cite{GamesPaper}.  The present paper is
the arc that carries the discipline across five established bodies
of physics.  The renormalization group of Kadanoff and Wilson
\cite{Kadanoff1966, Wilson1971, Wilson1975}, the quantum Darwinism
program of Zurek \cite{Zurek2003, Zurek2009}, the lattice gases of
Hardy, de~Pazzis, and Pomeau and of Frisch, Hasslacher, and Pomeau
\cite{HPP1976, FHP1986}, the fluctuation theorems of Jarzynski,
Crooks, and Sagawa and Ueda \cite{Jarzynski1997, Crooks1999,
SagawaUeda2010}, and the lattice gauge theory of Wegner and Elitzur
\cite{Wegner1971, Elitzur1975} each already contain an observer,
a blocking rule, a fragment, a coarse cell, a work meter, or a
gauge fixing.  The question asked here is uniform.  Which parts of
each standard story are properties of the substrate, and which are
properties of the declared consumer.

The discipline is the same in all five tracks.  Channels, tasks,
environments, budgets, seeds, and falsification bars are declared
in a track document before the runs they govern.  Verdicts are
computed from the declared items, never written first.  Every
result-bearing run writes an append-only evidence record, and
every number in this paper is read from those committed records.
When a run fails its declared bar, the failed verdict is retained
unchanged, the design error is named, and the corrected
declaration is run afterward as a separate registration.  Six of
the twenty-seven runs reported here failed, and
Sec.~\ref{sec:fails} presents all six with their named errors,
because the sequence of verdicts is itself part of the evidence.

Three claim types run through the paper and are never conflated.
Instrument claims state that the declared exact machinery passes
closed-form controls, and each track opens with one.  Structural
claims state that a named piece of standard physics is, in the
declared finite model, a property of the declared consumer rather
than of the substrate, or measurably is not, and they carry the
campaign's exploratory label.  Replication claims state that a
published exact result is reproduced in a declared finite
instance.  Nothing in this paper is a claim about any laboratory
system.  Every result is an exact measurement in a declared finite
model, and no run is labeled as supporting more than that.

The five tracks return one coherent conclusion.  What an observer
reads is set by its declared structure.  Representability,
correlation length, and relevance follow the blocking rule.
Redundancy and inter-observer agreement follow the declared
interaction.  Apparent dissipation follows the consumer's reversal
reach.  Fluctuation-identity defects follow the declared detector
model.  Gauge-variant readings follow the declared fixing.  The
apparent physics of coarse observers, in these models, is lawful
consumer arithmetic.

\section{Coarse representability, correlation length, and
relevance follow the declared channel}
\label{sec:rg}

The renormalization track asks which parts of the block-spin story
belong to the substrate and which to the declared blocking
channel.  Its instrument layer, RG-0, works on the one-dimensional
Ising ring, exact by transfer matrix and by full enumeration.
Decimation of every other spin of a 12-ring at coupling $K=0.5$
reproduces the 6-ring Ising distribution at the closed-form
coupling
\begin{equation}
  K' \;=\; \operatorname{artanh}\!\left(\tanh^2 K\right),
  \label{eq:decimation}
\end{equation}
here $K' = 0.21689$, with maximum deviation $4.2\times10^{-17}$ in
every configuration probability, the correlation length halves
exactly, and two decimations equal one decimation at the composed
coupling to $2.1\times10^{-17}$.  The channel-dependence control is
the seed of the track.  On the same substrate, majority-rule blocks
of three produce a coarse distribution whose best nearest-neighbor
Ising fit mispredicts the coarse next-nearest correlator by
$0.0260$, which is 260 times the declared $10^{-4}$ bar, while the
decimation channel's full-distribution residual is
$4.2\times10^{-17}$.  Whether the microscopic model is
representable in the coarse family is decided by the declared
channel, not by the model.

RG-1 measures which long-distance quantity survives a declared
family of channels.  The substrate is the two-dimensional Ising
model on a width-4 periodic cylinder at $K=0.3$, exact through the
$16\times16$ column transfer matrix, and the four declared column
functionals are decimation to the first spin, majority vote, the
adjacent pair product, and the column parity.  The transfer matrix
commutes with the global spin flip, so each channel's correlator is
a sum over the eigenvectors its output couples to.  The measured
cross-sector amplitude leakage is exactly zero for all four
channels, so each channel's decay rate is exactly its sector's
leading eigenvalue ratio.  The odd-sector correlation length is
$1.5026$ and the even-sector length is $0.5194$, a factor $2.893$
apart, and the channel decides which one the observer reads.  The
recorded verdict of the first run is FAIL.  Its route-agreement bar
compared the eigenvector sum against direct transfer-matrix powers
that subtracted order-one means from order-one moments, and at
separation 8 the connected even-channel correlator is near
$5\times10^{-8}$, so the declared comparison was numerically
impossible as written and missed its bar at relative deviation
$0.0386$.  The redeclared run, RG-1b, applies the mean subtraction
before the matrix powers, a cancellation-free computation of the
same quantity, and the two routes agree to $1.3\times10^{-14}$ at
every measured separation with every physics item unchanged.
Correlation length in this model is consumer-selected, not
consumer-invented, and the sectors themselves are the substrate's.

RG-2 transplants the campaign's budget flip.  The budget is one
bit, a deterministic bipartition of the sixteen column states with
all 32767 bipartitions enumerated, and the task is predicting the
sign of a distant column's magnetization.  The fidelity-optimal
bipartition maximizes the compressed marginal's entropy.  A
declared theorem fixes the task optimum, and the exhaustive search
and the theorem certify each other exactly in both runs.  The first
run's recorded verdict is FAIL because its declared setting, $K=0.3$
at separation 4, has almost nothing to predict.  The task-optimal
accuracy measured $0.6000482$ over a baseline of the same size, and
the fidelity criterion's 840-member tie set contained a partition
matching it exactly, so the flip was exactly zero.  A flip cannot
be measured where there is no signal.  The redeclared run, RG-2b,
moves to $K=0.6$ at separation 2, where the distant sign is
genuinely predictable, and the flip is positive, task-optimal
accuracy $0.95766$ against $0.95754$ for the best member of the
same 840-way tie.  The margin is $1.12\times10^{-4}$ against the
declared $10^{-4}$ bar, a pass with little room, so in this
setting the flip is present but thin.  Fifteen of the
sixteen coarse-state assignments across the two partitions disagree
with the raw leading-eigenvector sign, the finite-distance
correction the protocol asked about.

RG-3 is the universality gate.  Three declared microscopic models
are flowed under declared channels, and the family residual of a
coarse distribution is its total-variation distance from the best
nearest-neighbor Ising fit.  The nearest-neighbor model at $K=0.7$
flows exactly in-family under two decimations, with residuals below
$7\times10^{-16}$.  The model with an added next-nearest coupling
flows toward the family, residual $0.157$ to $0.032$ to $0.004$,
so the extra coupling is measured irrelevant under decimation.
Symmetry classes do not mix, the field model keeps a coarse
magnetization of $0.777$ while the symmetric models hold zero
within $10^{-15}$.  The first run's recorded verdict is FAIL on its
channel-relativity item, and the failure corrects the declaration's
picture of the flow.  The declaration predicted that majority
blocking would push the next-nearest model's residual up, and it
fell, $0.161$ to $0.0136$, because from an out-of-family start the
flow toward the trivial fixed point dominates any channel's
family violation, and majority carries the substrate further down
that flow than decimation does.  The redeclared run, RG-3b, makes
the corrected comparison from an exactly-in-family start.  There
one decimation leaves the family residual at $1.3\times10^{-16}$
while one majority-of-three blocking raises it to $0.0117$, so the
channel decides whether the family survives at all, while for the
out-of-family substrate both channels ride the dominant flow.
Universality in this model is real but channel-shaped.  What
converges is convergence itself, and which family it converges
through is the observer's choice.

\section{Redundancy is declared or absent and agreement between
observers tracks record strength}
\label{sec:qd}

Quantum Darwinism explains agreement between observers by
redundancy, many environment fragments carrying the same record of
the system \cite{Zurek2003, Zurek2009}.  That is intrinsically a
statement about a family of consumers, each reading its own
fragment.  The track works on one system qubit and six environment
qubits with exact density matrices, and its instrument layer, QD-0,
passes four closed-form controls.  A product state carries mutual
information $3.2\times10^{-16}$.  The GHZ branching state gives the
classical plateau exactly, one bit for every proper fragment and
two bits for the whole environment.  A branching state with
per-qubit record overlap $c$ matches the closed form
\begin{equation}
  I(S{:}F_f) = H\!\Big(\tfrac{1+c^{6}}{2}\Big)
   + H\!\Big(\tfrac{1+c^{f}}{2}\Big)
   - H\!\Big(\tfrac{1+c^{6-f}}{2}\Big)
  \label{eq:qdplateau}
\end{equation}
bits at every fragment size $f$, with $H$ the binary entropy,
to $2.2\times10^{-16}$.  The control that frames the track is a
seeded Haar-random pure state of all seven qubits.  Its mean
single-qubit fragment information is $0.051$ bits against a system
entropy of $0.999$ bits, while the full environment carries exactly
twice the system entropy.  Records are structure, not a generic
feature of purity.

QD-1 is the emergence gate.  Controlled rotations of declared angle
$\theta$ from system to each environment qubit produce branching
states whose fragment informations match Eq.~\eqref{eq:qdplateau}
with overlap $c = \cos(\theta/2)$ and nothing fitted, measured to
$2.7\times10^{-15}$ at every angle on the ladder from $\pi/16$ to
$\pi$.  Redundancy, the number of disjoint single-qubit fragments
reaching ninety percent of the system entropy, is 0 at every angle
below $\pi$ and 6 at $\pi$.  The recorded verdict of the first run
is FAIL on its basis-free control, and the failure is itself a
measured finding.  The control applied six sequential pairwise
Haar-random unitaries, system with each environment qubit in turn,
and ended with system entropy $0.665$ bits and redundancy 1 rather
than the declared 0.  The last-touched environment qubit carries a
redundancy-grade record, because the causal order of the
interactions is itself declared structure.  A sequence is
structure, and an observer can be handed a record by the schedule
of interactions alone.  The redeclared run, QD-1b, replaces the
control with a globally scrambled seeded Haar state with no
interaction schedule at all, and it carries system entropy
$0.9945$ bits with redundancy 0.  Redundancy in this model is
declared or absent.  The plateau appears exactly when and exactly
as the declared interaction's basis dictates, and maximal
entanglement without a declared basis carries no redundancy at
all.

QD-2 measures the consumer's budget.  For the symmetric consumer,
equal angles on all six qubits, the smallest sufficient fragment
size is 3 at every one of sixteen grid angles, and the closed form
says why.  A fragment holding half the environment has record
overlap equal to its complement's, so its information equals the
system entropy exactly at any nonzero angle.  The portfolio
consumer has declared unequal angles, so a fragment's overlap is
the product over the qubits it holds and fragments of equal size
are no longer equivalent.  All 62 proper environment subsets match
their closed forms to $1.1\times10^{-15}$, and the best and worst
two-qubit fragments carry $1.612$ and $0.045$ bits, a factor of
$36.0$.  Two consumers with identical fragment counts and
identical access sizes read the same state with a thirty-six-fold
information disparity.  A budget is a portfolio, not a number.

marginal success probability matches the Helstrom closed form
\cite{Helstrom1976} to $8.9\times10^{-16}$ at every angle, and the

\section{Shear freezes on HPP, viscosity on FHP is
prescription-invariant, and dissipation is the consumer's reversal
reach}
\label{sec:hd}

The hydrodynamics track asks whether a transport coefficient
belongs to the substrate or to the declared coarse-graining, and
whether the second-law appearance at coarse scale is entirely the
observer's.  The instrument layer, HD-0, runs the HPP lattice gas
\cite{HPP1976} on a $16\times16$ torus.  Mass 194 and both momentum
components are integer-exact at every step, double velocity
reversal reproduces the initial state bit for bit, and rotating
the initial state commutes with evolution bit for bit.  The
declared coarse observer's entropy rises $0.637$ bits from the
ordered start, and on the reversed run the entropy curve retraces
the forward curve with maximum deviation exactly zero.  The rise is
the observer's.  The substrate forgets nothing.

HD-1 aims a shear and a sound wave at a 64-torus ensemble, and its
recorded verdict is FAIL on the shear items.  The failure is the
substrate's own physics, exactly measured.  The shear amplitude did
not decay at all, decay ratio exactly $1.0$ at both wavenumbers
with fitted rates at the $10^{-18}$ floating-point floor.  The
head-on collision exchanges a north-south pair for an east-west
pair and streaming keeps each mover on its line, so the transverse
momentum of every column and the longitudinal momentum of every row
are separately conserved, and a shear mode can never relax.  These
are the spurious conservation laws of HPP, the pathology that
motivated the FHP model \cite{FHP1986}, here measured by the
declared instrument rather than assumed.  The sound sector passed,
with measured sound speed $1.008$ and $1.012$ of the lattice
prediction and dispersion ratio $2.008$.  The redeclared run,
HD-1b, converts the failure into exact bars.  The per-column
transverse momentum vector and the per-row longitudinal momentum
vector are integer-exact constant at every one of 400 steps across
the full ensemble, and the diffusive-scaling bar is withdrawn as
unmeasurable on this substrate.  The viscosity question moves to a
substrate without the spurious invariants.

HD-2 declares the FHP-I hexagonal gas on a $64\times64$ triangular
torus and validates it with exact geometry self-tests before any
physics is read.  The shear that HPP froze relaxes on FHP.  The
$k=1$ amplitude falls to $0.170$ of its window start and the $k=2$
amplitude to $0.027$, with rates $0.008114$ and $0.036818$ and a
rate ratio of $4.538$ inside the declared diffusive band.  The
measured viscosity is $0.842$ at $k=1$ and $0.955$ at $k=2$, beside
the FHP-I Boltzmann estimate $0.742$, a comparison and never a bar.
The prescription gate is the reason this track exists.  Nine
declared prescriptions, three cell sizes crossed with three
sampling cadences, applied to the same stored momentum series,
give fitted rates from $0.008107$ to $0.008113$, a spread of
$0.07$ percent of the mean against the declared ten percent bar
(Fig.~\ref{fig:hd}).  The campaign's rewriting-system track found a
growth rule whose measured dimension moved by 18 percent when the
bookkeeping prescription changed \cite{WMPaper}.  The contrast is
the finding.  An exponential hydrodynamic mode's rate survives
every declared linear cell average and every subsampling cadence,
so which observables a prescription can touch is itself a measured
distinction between substrates, not a slogan.

HD-3 is the dissipation gate.  An FHP-I update is a bijection, so
the exact inverse step is declared, and the forward shear ensemble
is run 300 steps and then inverted.  The initial state is recovered
bit for bit in all 50 realizations.  The projected shear amplitude
fell from $25.31$ to $2.15$ on the way out, ratio $0.085$, and the
inverse-run amplitude sequence equals the forward sequence reversed
with maximum deviation exactly zero, float for float
(Fig.~\ref{fig:hd}).  Every dissipated number came back, so no
strict dissipative law governs the coarse observable, and the decay
is the observer's accounting.  One declared bit flipped at the
turnaround destroys it.  The perturbed inverse returns its
realization at amplitude $0.437$ against a true initial $24.04$, a
return ratio of $0.018$ against the declared bar of one half, while
the 49 untouched realizations return bit for bit, a control inside
the item.  The Hamming distance between the perturbed and true
inverse trajectories floods from 1 bit to 3047 by 30 steps and
11007 by 60, then sits near 11200, which is the full-decorrelation
value of two occupied fractions on the realization's 24576 bits,
about 11182.  Dissipation on this substrate is declared or absent
in the consumer.  The coarse mode's decay is recoverable by any
observer with exact reversal reach and irrecoverable after the
loss of one bit.

\section{Restricted observers break the fluctuation identities by
exactly their declared channels}
\label{sec:ft}

The Jarzynski equality \cite{Jarzynski1997} and the Crooks relation
\cite{Crooks1999} are exact identities for an observer who sees the
full trajectory.  The track asks what happens to them for a
consumer with a restricted channel.  The instrument layer, FT-0,
declares a three-state system with a linear energy protocol over
eight steps and Metropolis kernels in detailed balance, and
enumerates the full path space of $19683$ paths exactly.  The
Jarzynski average
\begin{equation}
  \bigl\langle e^{-W} \bigr\rangle \;=\; e^{-\Delta F}
  \label{eq:jarzynski}
\end{equation}
holds with deviation exactly zero, and the Crooks relation holds
path by path, not only on average, with maximum deviation
$2.2\times10^{-19}$ over all $19683$ paths.  Mean work is
$-0.0788$ against $\Delta F = -0.1021$, a dissipation of $0.0233$,
and the frozen protocol yields exactly zero work on every path.
This is the replication half of the track, the published identities
reproduced at the finest grain the model has.

\section{Six runs failed their declared bars and each failure has
a name}
\label{sec:fails}

Six of the twenty-seven runs recorded verdict FAIL, every failure
was located and named, and every correction was run as a separate
registration with the original record retained unchanged.  The
campaign's previous paper followed the same pattern for its two
failures, a residual threshold where a proof was needed and a
double-counted tie set \cite{GamesPaper}.  The six failures of this
arc each taught something specific.

RG-1 declared a route-agreement bar that subtracted order-one means
from order-one moments after powering the transfer matrix, and at
the declared separation the connected value is near
$5\times10^{-8}$, so the comparison was numerically impossible as
written.  The correction subtracts the mean before powering.  A
declared bar must be computable at the scale it will be compared
against.

RG-2 declared a prediction task in a setting with almost no signal,
task accuracy $0.6000482$ over a baseline of the same size, so the
flip it was built to measure was exactly zero.  The correction
moves to a setting where the target is genuinely predictable.  A
flip cannot be measured where there is nothing to predict.

RG-3 predicted the direction of a channel comparison from an
out-of-family start, and the substrate's own flow toward triviality
dominated the channel difference.  The correction states the
channel-relativity claim from an exactly-in-family start, where it
is measured and stands.  A channel effect must be isolated from the
flow it rides on.

QD-1 declared a basis-free control that applied its scrambling
unitaries in sequence, and the causal order imprinted a
redundancy-grade record on the last-touched qubit.  The correction
uses a globally scrambled state with no schedule.  A sequence is
structure, and this failure is itself a finding about how observers
come to hold records.

HD-1 declared a shear-decay bar on a substrate whose spurious
conservation laws freeze shear exactly, decay ratio exactly one.
The correction measures the spurious invariants as exact
conservation laws and moves viscosity to a substrate without them.
This failure is a replication of the pathology that motivated FHP
\cite{FHP1986}, found by the declared instrument.

GG-2 declared a quadratic window for the invariant sector's
response, and the measured slope is $4.000003$ because loop parity
closes the quadratic channel identically.  The correction carries
the loop-parity argument as its prediction.  The declaration
underestimated how silent the substrate's invariant sector is.

Three of the six failures, QD-1, HD-1, and GG-2, are measurements
of real structure the declaration did not anticipate, and the other
three are named bookkeeping errors in the declarations themselves.
In all six the corrected redeclaration passed with the measured
physics unchanged, and in all six the failed record is part of the
evidence.

\section{Methods and reproducibility}
\label{sec:methods}

All runs follow the campaign's standing discipline.  Substrates,
channels, observables, seeds, tolerances, and bars were declared in
the five track documents before the runs they govern, verdicts are
computed from the declared items, and every result-bearing run
writes an append-only JSON evidence record containing the declared
parameters, the measured outcomes, and a canonical SHA-256 hash of
the record body.  The records for this paper are
\texttt{rg0-instrument.json} through \texttt{rg4-relevance.json},
\texttt{qd0-instrument.json} through \texttt{qd3-agreement.json},
\texttt{hd0-instrument.json} through
\texttt{hd3-dissipation-gate.json},
\texttt{ft0-instrument.json} through \texttt{ft3-feedback.json},
and \texttt{gg0-instrument.json} through
\texttt{gg3-minimal-coupling.json} in the campaign repository,
twenty-seven records in all, with the committed runners alongside
them.  The failed RG-1, RG-2, RG-3, QD-1, HD-1, and GG-2 records
are retained unchanged, and each corrected declaration is a
separate record with its own registration, so the sequence of
verdicts is itself part of the evidence.  Every number and every
figure coordinate in this paper is read from those committed
records.  The figure tables are generated deterministically by a
committed script, \texttt{make\_figdata\_gen2.py}, with no free
parameters, whose in-script assertions bind every emitted
coordinate and every headline number quoted in the text to the
record values it reads.  A reducer may summarize records but may
not rewrite them.
